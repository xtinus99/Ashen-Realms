*Seen in the Bonecage, Session 50.*

## What They Saw

In Session 50, as the company was leaving the Bonecage, a fresh wave of cheering turned them around. Vortegas had taken the Warden's Seat and ordered the arena cleared. Under a cold black corona, a many-armed figure vaulted down onto the floor. It was the Pallbearer, back for another try at the Bone-Warden.

They traded blows, and Vortegas won. The Pallbearer made the same promise he had made the first time and leapt out of the arena.

> “I'll be back.”<br>— the Pallbearer

## What They Know

The day before, a clerk at the Bonecage had told them the story. Vortegas's last fight in the arena had been sixty years earlier, against the Pallbearer. The Pallbearer lost, said he would be back, jumped out of the arena and wasn't seen again. When the company tried to place the name, nothing came to mind.

That is all they have: a title and two lost fights. They have no name for him and no idea why he keeps coming back.
