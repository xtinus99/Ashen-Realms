## What They Know

Not much. At the Living Stacks in Cerebrax, in Session 54, Viktor and Veezara learned that one sign of her arrival is that things start growing holes.

## The Abyssal Book

Thessaly gave Viktor the Abyssal book in Veinspire in Session 47. The cover is too worn to tell who wrote it. Three pages of it are known, and two of them are about holes.

The first speaks of the enslavement of the spirit. When Viktor read it, the book looked back at him.

> “How many holes must I make before I can see you?”<br>— the second page

> “If a person is placed next to a wall full of holes, would he start growing holes too?”<br>— the third page

Reading it has cost Viktor. In Session 50 he tried again at the Marrow Rest and failed. In Session 58, before leaving Marrowgate, he failed badly and carried two levels of exhaustion out of the city.
