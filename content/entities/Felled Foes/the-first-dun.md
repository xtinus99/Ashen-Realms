## At the bottom of the vault

The vault's aisles sloped down to the bottom, where the only thing the company could see was the book Cattermole wanted, chained down, with a light shining on it. When Veezara went up to it, the light turned red, and the First Dun appeared and attacked them: a twelve-foot figure wrapped head to foot in chains of small gold tags.

## How it died

Two Duns, smaller figures in the same chains, came into the fight. All three went down. The First Dun came apart in a flood of spilled gold tags, and the book didn't have a mark on it. The company cut the book free and carried it out.

<h4>Actions</h4>
<p><strong><em>Multiattack.</em></strong> The First Dun makes four Tag-Chain attacks. It can replace two of them with one Chain Slam. It can grapple up to four creatures at once.</p>
<p><strong><em>Tag-Chain.</em></strong> <em>Melee Weapon Attack:</em> +13 to hit, reach 25 ft., one target. <em>Hit:</em> 18 (2d10 + 7) bludgeoning damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 21). If the target has no tag, a tag clips through its ear.</p>
<p><strong><em>Chain Slam.</em></strong> The First Dun swings one creature it is grappling into another creature. <em>Melee Weapon Attack:</em> +13 to hit, reach 25 ft., one creature other than the grappled one. <em>Hit:</em> The target and the grappled creature each take 36 (8d8) bludgeoning damage, and both are knocked prone. <em>Miss:</em> The grappled creature takes 36 (8d8) bludgeoning damage.</p>
<p><strong><em>Call In the Debt (Recharge 5–6).</em></strong> Each tagged creature within 60 feet makes a DC 21 Constitution saving throw, taking 63 (14d8) necrotic damage on a failure, or half as much on a success. The First Dun regains hit points equal to half the total damage dealt.</p>
<h4>Reactions</h4>
<p><strong><em>Chain Ward.</em></strong> When a creature the First Dun can see hits it with an attack, a coil of chain catches the blow and it adds 4 to its AC against that attack, possibly making it miss.</p>
<h4>Legendary Actions</h4>
<p>The First Dun can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one option can be used at a time, at the end of another creature's turn. It regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.</p>
<ul><li><strong>Chain.</strong> It makes one Tag-Chain attack.</li><li><strong>Drag.</strong> It moves each creature it is grappling up to 20 feet.</li><li><strong>Collect (costs 2 actions).</strong> One creature it is grappling takes 31 (7d8) necrotic damage, and the First Dun regains that many hit points.</li></ul>
