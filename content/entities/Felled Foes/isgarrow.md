## The job

A crew of four from Crownfall, the Second Burial, had taken a contract on it. When the company reached the crag in Session 57, the four were already inside fighting it.

## What it looked like

It was a dragon's skeleton the size of a building, up and fighting round a pillar of rock in a sea-cave, wearing the dead who had come to collect it like armour. Brass seals sat in its eye sockets. White dust sifted from its breastbone, its left wing and its brow.

## How it died

The company joined the fight. The dragon went down and got up three times, and each time it came back with less of itself: first without one wing, then without the other, then without its tail. The fourth time it fell, it stayed down and burst in a wave of necrotic force thirty feet across. The burst killed Veezara, and Lazare brought him back.

## What it left

It blew apart into fragments. From its hoard, between the rocks of the den, the company took a **Ring of Free Action**, a **Belt of Dwarvenkind**, a **Scimitar of Speed** (now Karusa's) and a **Scarab of Protection**. The Second Burial kept an orb of necrotic energy, and the company was owed a quarter of the job's pay, about 3,000 gp, which it collected in Crownfall in Session 58.
