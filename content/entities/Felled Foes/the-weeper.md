## In the cellar

A man's height and shape, a hood of near-black shell over his head and shoulders, a roach's head with heavy mandibles and long antennae, split wing cases hanging behind him like a ragged cloak, and four arms: two long ones ending in serrated spikes and two shorter ones with hooked claws. In the middle of his back was an old apple, shrivelled black and grown over with shell.

## Session 65

<img src="images/S65 The Weeper, Moulted.webp" alt="The Weeper after the Moult, pale and soft, stepping out of his empty dark husk" class="content-image">

He was crouched over the body of a woman, eating, and between mouthfuls cried in a young man's frightened voice: *"Mother, where are you? I'm scared... I don't want to..."* The company killed him, and the scratching in the town's walls stopped. Farkas ate the apple from his back.

The old shell lies where it split.</p>
<h4>Actions</h4>
<p><strong><em>Multiattack.</em></strong> The Weeper makes one Mandibles attack and two Claw attacks.</p>
<p><strong><em>Mandibles.</em></strong> <em>Melee Weapon Attack:</em> +13 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. <em>Hit:</em> 28 (4d10 + 6) piercing damage plus 13 (3d8) psychic damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 20). While he's grappling a creature, he can use his Mandibles only on that creature.</p>
<p><strong><em>Claw.</em></strong> <em>Melee Weapon Attack:</em> +13 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. <em>Hit:</em> 16 (3d6 + 6) slashing damage.</p>
<p><strong><em>Skitter.</em></strong> He runs, the way a roach runs: he moves up to his speed, over walls and ceiling if he likes, without provoking opportunity attacks, and makes one Claw attack against each creature he passes within 5 feet of.</p>

<p><strong>Into the Walls.</strong> He goes into one crack or hole and comes out of another within 60 feet, without provoking opportunity attacks.</p>
<p><strong>Claw.</strong> He makes one Claw attack.</p>
<p><strong>The Squeak (Costs 2 Actions).</strong> He cries out for his mother, a young man's voice breaking into a roach's squeal.
