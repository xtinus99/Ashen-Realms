## Round the tower

An eel so big that two of its great olive-black coils lay over the tower's ring wall. At every feeding the basket swung out over the water and its head came up the wall to meet it.

## Session 65

Farkas tried to talk to it, but it kept calling him a snack. The company killed it. It let go of the tower and went down into the green dark, and the tower stopped shaking for the first time any of the folk could remember.

<h4>Actions</h4>
<p><strong><em>Multiattack.</em></strong> The eel makes one Bite attack, and one Throat Jaw attack against the creature it has grappled, if any. It can use Crack the Floor in place of the Bite.</p>
<p><strong><em>Bite.</em></strong> <em>Melee Weapon Attack:</em> +14 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target. <em>Hit:</em> 30 (4d10 + 8) piercing damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 18). Until the grapple ends, the target is restrained and the eel can't Bite another target.</p>
<p><strong><em>Throat Jaw.</em></strong> <em>Melee Weapon Attack:</em> +14 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature grappled by the eel. <em>Hit:</em> 26 (4d8 + 8) piercing damage. If the target was already grappled when the eel's turn began, the second jaws drag it down the gullet: the grapple ends and the target is <strong>swallowed</strong>. A swallowed creature is blinded and restrained, has total cover against attacks and other effects from outside the eel, and takes 21 (6d6) acid damage at the start of each of the eel's turns. The eel holds one swallowed creature at a time. If the swallowed creature deals 35 damage or more to the eel in a single turn from inside, the eel spits it out prone within 10 feet of its head, which is the sea unless the head is over the tower top.</p>
<p><strong><em>Crack the Floor.</em></strong> The eel tightens the coil under one side of the top. The floor there splits: each creature in a 15-foot square of the eel's choosing, touching the ring wall, makes a DC 22 Dexterity saving throw or falls through to the half-built floor below, taking 14 (4d6) bludgeoning damage and landing prone. That square is a hole for the rest of the fight. Every time it does this, there's less tower to stand on.</p>
<h4>Reactions</h4>
<p><strong><em>Snatch.</em></strong> When a creature comes within 15 feet of the eel, the eel makes one Bite attack against it.</p>
<h4>Legendary Actions</h4>
<p>The eel can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. It regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.</p>
<p><strong>Rise.</strong> The head comes up over the wall anywhere round the tower.</p>
<p><strong>Shake a Coil.</strong> One creature standing on or climbing one of its coils makes a DC 22 Strength saving throw or is flung 20 feet, into the sea if that's where 20 feet goes.</p>
<p><strong>Bite.</strong> The eel makes one Bite attack. If it's already grappling someone, it lets go of them first: they drop where they are, into the sea if the head is over the water.</p>
<p><strong>Drag Down (Costs 2 Actions).</strong> The creature the eel is grappling makes a DC 22 Strength saving throw. On a failure the eel pulls it over the edge into the sea and lets go.</p>
