## What he looked like

A long-limbed living skeleton. Both heels and most of each lower leg had been replaced with big perforated chambers of ivory, which glowed red at the vents before they fired. He wore copper-threaded wraps on his hands and feet and carried no weapon.

## His last card

Iven faced all four of them on open sand in Session 51. Surrender was allowed and the purse was 400 gp. The company beat him, and he died in the bout. His record ended at 12–8.

The victors took both of his Hollowheel legs, fused combustion chambers and all. The legs were that card's spoils.

## What became of the legs

Farkas and Karusa paid 1,000 gp to have the legs and Mhorra's preserved paw made into a graft, White-Hollow Stride. The company later sold the finished graft, along with Viktor's Boots of the Unmoored, for 3,500 gp.
