## At the bottom

The biggest blank of all, crouched on a hoard of everything the hallway had taken, twice Farkas's height even crouching, with red bricks fused into its back like scales, Ambrose's lantern burning in its chest, cut rope round one arm and Ambrose's scar on its forearm.

## Session 64

Farkas and Anvil killed it. As it died it said: *"Curse her..."* Everything it had taken left it, and a glove of its own grey skin dropped from it: the Grey Hand.

<p><strong><em>Ambrose's Lantern.</em></strong> The lantern sunk in its chest lets it see within 60 feet. Douse or destroy it and the Hoarder is blind beyond its blindsight and loses Swallow the Light.</p>
<p><strong><em>Everything It Has Taken.</em></strong> When the Hoarder dies, every mark, page, weapon and object it has taken returns to its owner, wherever that owner is.</p>
<h4>Actions</h4>
<p><strong><em>Multiattack.</em></strong> Three Slam attacks.</p>
<p><strong><em>Slam.</em></strong> <em>Melee Weapon Attack:</em> +12 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. <em>Hit:</em> 16 (2d8 + 7) bludgeoning damage plus 3 (1d6) psychic damage. The target must succeed on a DC 19 Constitution saving throw or its hit point maximum is reduced by the damage taken until it leaves the hallway, and the Hoarder takes one mark or written thing from it. Once per turn, when it hits a creature holding a weapon with a Slam, it can also close its grey flesh on that weapon: the creature makes a DC 20 Strength saving throw or the weapon is pulled into the Hoarder's body, taken, as with Hoard.</p>
<p><strong><em>Unwrite (Recharge 5–6).</em></strong> The Hoarder breathes out grey in a 30-foot cone. Each creature in it makes a DC 19 Wisdom saving throw, taking 36 (8d8) psychic damage on a failure, or half as much on a success. On a failure the Hoarder also takes one mark or written thing from it, and it can't speak or cast spells with verbal components until the end of its next turn: it has lost the words.</p>
<h4>Reactions</h4>
<p><strong><em>Hoard.</em></strong> When a creature within 10 feet of it misses it with a melee weapon attack, the Hoarder closes its grey flesh on the weapon. The attacker makes a DC 20 Strength saving throw or the weapon is pulled into the Hoarder's body, taken. It comes back when the Hoarder dies.</p>
<h4>Legendary Actions</h4>
<p>The Hoarder can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. It regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.</p>
<p><strong>Climb.</strong> It moves up to half its speed, along the floor or the walls.</p>
<p><strong>Swallow the Light.</strong> One light source within 60 feet goes out. A light made by a spell is suppressed until the end of its caster's next turn.</p>
<p><strong>Slam.</strong> It makes one Slam attack.</p>
<h4>Lair Actions</h4>
<p>On initiative count 20 (losing ties), the Hoarder takes one lair action. It can't use the same one two rounds running.</p>
<p><strong>The walls close.</strong> The chamber's walls fold 5 feet inward all round (three times at most), swallowing any pillar they reach. A creature against a wall as it moves makes a DC 15 Strength saving throw or is pushed 5 feet and restrained until the end of its next turn.</p>
<p><strong>The floor swallows.</strong> Each creature on the floor, and not on the hoard, makes a DC 15 Strength saving throw or slides 10 feet toward the hoard.</p>
<p><strong>Out of the walls.</strong> A <strong>blank</strong> steps out of the wall anywhere in the chamber. No more than two in one fight.</p>
