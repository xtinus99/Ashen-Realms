## What it looks like

A dazhul's face opens in three muscular leaves around a single heavy tongue. There is an acid sack inside it.

## In the chalk cut

In Session 55, on the road from Cerebrax with O.R.V.E.K, the company ran into three dazhuls in a shaded chalk cut beside water. O.R.V.E.K destroyed all three.

Farkas hauled one carcass away for its acid sack, reckoned to be worth about a thousand gold. It sold in Marrowgate for 800 gp. The other two bodies stayed in the cut.

<p>A trapped arm cannot hold or manipulate anything, and anything it held is dropped. A trapped leg knocks the target prone, and the target cannot stand until the grapple ends. A trapped wing cannot be used to fly. The DM applies the nearest equivalent to creatures with a different body plan.</p>

The jaw can also be targeted directly (Armor Class 16); damage dealt to it is dealt to the dazhul. Track damage dealt specifically to the jaw after each of the dazhul's turns.
