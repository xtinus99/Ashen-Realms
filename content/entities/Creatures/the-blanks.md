## What they look like

Smooth grey human shapes with no faces, no hair, no nails and no marks. A blank that has taken a mark wears it on its blank skin, and one wearing a name speaks in its owner's voice.

## In the hallway

The company killed seventeen: three in the near halls, six in the great hall, and eight at the table of the copied house, where they sat in the poses of a family with Kenwyn in the ninth chair. As each one wearing one of his marks died, the mark went back onto him.
