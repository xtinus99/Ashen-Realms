## What it does

A big four-toed hunter of the bone-ridge country. It denned under a giant's ribcage in the eastern Bonefield, among gnawed bone and old kills. It blends in with the bone and dust, pounces, ambushes, and burrows: when Farkas's roar drew it out, Ratsby watched it go into the ground behind the third ridge and come at them underground.

**Known traits:** Bonefield Camouflage, Pounce, Ambush; burrow 25 ft.

## The hunt

The company took the bounty off the Roll in Session 40. Anvil stamped on the ground over the tunnel, and Farkas brought the Skyfather's Reckoning down on it hard enough to force the Stalker up, and it went for him. The fight ran three rounds, and all three of them came out healthy. Ratsby opened it with a hidden strike from behind a half-buried femur. Anvil took the pounce on his armour. Farkas's maul broke its back in the third round, Anvil pinned its neck with his warhammer haft, and Ratsby put a dagger in its eye.

Her two cubs were in a side-chamber of the den: cat-sized, bone-grey, with large dark eyes, watching without fear. Anvil killed them both. Among the scraps in the burrow was a small leather collar, outgrown and kept, with a name-plate that read MILA.

The Wall-Clerk at the Roll confirmed the species from the head and paid 90 gold.
