## What it looks like

A big grey giant, swollen and scarred, strapped into iron fittings and carrying a cleaver of bone.

## In the Bonecage

In Session 50 Ferrin offered the company a Wildcard and didn't say what was behind it. They signed anyway. The Pen Floor had two containment recesses and two marked jars of lime-acid, and what came out onto it was Grolch. The four beat it and took 120 gp, and the win cleared every one of them to ask for a Knuckle promotion match.

<p><strong><em>Channel Sweep (Recharge 5–6).</em></strong> Grolch swings its cleaver through a 15-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking 22 (4d8 + 4) slashing damage and falling prone on a failure, or half as much damage on a success.</p>
<h4>Bonus Actions</h4>
<p><strong><em>Frenzied Pursuit (After Breaking Point Only).</em></strong> Grolch moves up to half its speed toward a creature that damaged it since the end of its last turn. This movement does not provoke opportunity attacks from that creature.</p>
