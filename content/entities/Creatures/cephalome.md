## What it looks like

Most of it is skull, close to three quarters of its bulk. Two huge forelimbs hold the weight up, and a short body and two thin hind legs drag along behind. Its eyes, breathing slits and jaw hinges are scattered across the lopsided head, none of them where they should be.

## In the Bonecage

In Session 51, after a long rest, the company went back to the Bonecage and took a Wildcard on open sand for a fixed 400 gp purse. Ferrin didn't say what was behind it, and they signed anyway. What came out was Cephalome, and the company killed it.
