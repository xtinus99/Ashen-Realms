## What it looks like

A pale, horseshoe-shaped creature as broad as a cottage, walking on six thick limbs. Its low shell is chalk-white and scored with branching red vessels, and two long black-edged foreclaws fold beneath the rim. A crown of translucent crimson and violet fronds rises from an opening in its back.

## Outside Cerebrax

In Session 54 Farkas stayed outside Cerebrax while the others slept in the city. In the night, right outside the city, a Choroid Harvester attacked him. He killed it alone, decided it wasn't safe to rest out in the open, and walked to a nearby farm.

<p><strong><em>Amphibious.</em></strong> The Harvester can breathe air and water.</p>
<p><strong><em>Pressure Sense.</em></strong> While its Filter Crown is intact, the Harvester knows the direction of every active spell, charged memory crystal, and magical effect within 120 feet. It learns no spell name, creature identity, thought, memory, or other information from this sense.</p>
<p><strong><em>Filter Crown.</em></strong> The crown is a Large object attached to the Harvester (AC 16; 30 hit points; immunity to poison and psychic damage). Damage dealt to the crown is not dealt to the Harvester. If the crown is destroyed, the Harvester loses Pressure Sense and Crown Siphon. A destroyed crown regrows after the Harvester finishes a long rest.</p>
<h4>Actions</h4>
<p><strong><em>Multiattack.</em></strong> The Harvester makes two Shear Claw attacks. It can replace one attack with Crown Siphon.</p>
<p><strong><em>Shear Claw.</em></strong> <em>Melee Weapon Attack:</em> +10 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. <em>Hit:</em> 17 (2d10 + 6) slashing damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 17) if it is Large or smaller. The Harvester has two claws, each of which can grapple one target.</p>
<p><strong><em>Crown Siphon.</em></strong> One creature grappled by the Harvester must make a DC 17 Constitution saving throw, taking 31 (7d8) psychic damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. The Harvester gains temporary hit points equal to half the damage dealt. This is a pressure drain; it does not reveal or remove the target's memories, spells, or proficiencies.</p>
<p><strong><em>Ventricle Surge (Recharge 5–6).</em></strong> The Harvester drives a wave of dense fluid through a 60-foot-long, 10-foot-wide line. Each creature in the line must make a DC 17 Strength saving throw. On a failure, a creature takes 45 (10d8) bludgeoning damage, is pushed up to 30 feet away, and falls prone. On a success, it takes half as much damage and is neither pushed nor knocked prone.</p>
