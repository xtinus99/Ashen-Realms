## What it looks like

About fourteen feet tall when it rears up. It walks on two huge arms, planting its knuckles and hauling a hanging belly after them, while two short legs catch up behind. Its head never cleared its shoulders: a broad, tired, human-looking face is stretched across a skull sunk between chest and back, and a small unfinished hand flexes under one collarbone.

## In the Bonecage

In Session 49 the company turned down a Wildcard. Nobody else signed for it, so the Bitter Standard took it, and the Wildcard was Acromel. It wiped the team out and was hauled away in its cage, still eating one of the team's female orcs.

In Session 50 the company took a Deep-Pen Wildcard without knowing what was in it, and Acromel came out onto the sand with Dolich. During the fight something else came out of Acromel and joined in. The arena counted it as part of Acromel's card, so it changed neither the result nor the purse. The company killed all three and took the 400 gp purse. Before the bodies were taken away, Viktor filled a vial with Acromel's blood, and later sold it with his other samples.
