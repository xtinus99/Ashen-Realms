## What they look like

Gaunt figures wrapped head to foot in chains of small gold tags, with the loose ends trailing along the floor behind them. They are smaller than the First Dun.

## In the vault

Two of them came into the fight at the bottom of the vault in Session 58, while the company was fighting the First Dun. The company killed both.
