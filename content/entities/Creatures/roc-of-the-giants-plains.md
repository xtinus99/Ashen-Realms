## In the storm

It rode the storm over the high plains and came for the steading's aurochs, as it did every storm. The company and Hereward of the Long Knife killed it at the fold. No aurochs was taken.

<p><strong><em>Talons.</em></strong> <em>Melee Weapon Attack:</em> +14 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. <em>Hit:</em> 36 (6d8 + 9) slashing damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 19).
