## What it looks like

About fourteen feet tall and thin, on bowed legs with oversized feet. Its arms are long enough to brush the ground, and a small, old-looking face sits on top of a hanging throat. A second face, squashed flat, shows through the skin of its swollen belly. It walks rather than charges, taking long dragging steps with its arms feeling the space ahead.

## In the Bonecage

It came out onto the sand with Acromel in the Deep-Pen Wildcard the company took in Session 50 without knowing what was behind it. The company killed it, along with Acromel and the thing that came out of Acromel, for a 400 gp purse. Viktor filled a vial with its blood before it was taken away, and later sold it with his other samples.
