## On the road

On the road to the duelling field it came up out of the ground under the road and attacked: first a ridged fin of grey-blue plate through the soil, then the whole beast, as big as a barn. The company killed it.

<p><strong><em>Deadly Leap (Recharge 5–6).</em></strong> If the bulette jumps at least 20 feet as part of its movement, it lands on its feet in a space containing one or more other creatures.
