## What they look like

Spiders the size of a horse or bigger, built out of bone. The body is a bulb of fused skulls, the eight legs are articulated spines and femurs, and the face is a mosaic of jawbones and teeth. They spin white bone-silk that gleams like ivory and is as hard as it looks: a strand bends, but it doesn't break. When one clicks, the others answer.

## In the weaver cave

In Session 55 the company went into the weaver cave beyond Last Tally for bone poppies, with O.R.V.E.K. Something clicked overhead, and more clicking answered it from around the cave. Seven weavers came for them: four Skeletal, two Blessed and one Ancient. All seven died.

In the nest the company found a fresh cocoon holding a dead pilgrim, a Ring of Resistance against psychic damage on the body, and pale poppies growing out of picked bones at the back.
