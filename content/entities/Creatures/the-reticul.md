## What it looks like

Sixty feet of armoured body on twenty short legs, moving at a steady walk. Its head is a hinged scoop wider than a wagon: the top half is a lattice of bone mesh and the bottom half a bed of soft pink lobes. Small shielded eyes are scattered along its body.

## In the Bonecage

In Session 57 the company asked Ferrin for a Wildcard, and as always he didn't say what was behind it. They signed for an open bone-sand floor with no Public Draw. In the first dark-hours the Deep Pen gate opened, and a Reticul came up the freight ramp and started ploughing the sand.

They beat it. It went still on the sand with its scoop down, the official ended the card, and Deep Pen handlers came out with hooks, chains and lime to clear it away. The purse was 1,000 gp.

<p><strong><em>Void the Strainer (1/Day).</em></strong> The Reticul tips the scoop and dumps everything held in it into a 15-foot square. Creatures released land prone. It does this when the mesh is broken or the load is wrong, not out of mercy.</p>
