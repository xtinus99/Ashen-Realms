## What it looks like

Six long legs carry a face stretched out like a drumhead, so wide that its eyes and mouth sit yards apart across the skin.

## In the Bonecage

The second of the company's three cards in one night in Session 55, and the first of two Wildcards. They signed without knowing what they would face, and a Tarnal came out onto the sand. They killed it for a fixed 400 gp purse.
