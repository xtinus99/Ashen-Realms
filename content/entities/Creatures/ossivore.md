## What it looks like

A skeletal humanoid that moves wrong. Its joints bend the wrong way, its spine undulates, and its fingers have too many knuckles. The bones are polished white and seamless. The eye sockets don't reflect light, and the jaw opens far wider than it should. When it moves, bone grinds on bone, low and continuous.

## On the coast road

In Session 57 the company walked west along the bone beaches from the Northern Containment, looking for the four from Crownfall. Further on they stopped to rest, and during Viktor's watch three ossivores ambushed them. The company killed all three, made camp, and went on.

<p><strong><em>Bone Extraction.</em></strong> <em>Melee Weapon Attack:</em> +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature grappled by the ossivore. <em>Hit:</em> 21 (4d8 + 3) piercing damage as the ossivore begins extracting bones. If this damage reduces the target to 0 hit points, the ossivore kills them by removing their skeleton entirely, gaining 10 temporary hit points. A creature killed this way cannot be raised as an undead with a skeleton (it has none) unless <em>regenerate</em> or similar magic restores its bones first.</p>
<p><strong><em>Bone Rattle (1/Day).</em></strong> The ossivore vibrates its bones at a frequency that disrupts other skeletal structures. Each creature with bones within 20 feet must make a DC 13 Constitution saving throw. On a failure, the creature takes 14 (4d6) necrotic damage and has its speed reduced by half until the end of its next turn as its bones temporarily lock up. On a success, the creature takes half damage and its speed isn't reduced.</p>
