## What it looks like

About twelve feet long. The body is a low, headless frame of black muscle and cartilage on four limbs. A whole person's skin hangs off it, attached along the spine and hips, with its empty arms and legs dangling underneath and its face upside down in the lowest fold of the belly. When it moves, the frame goes first and the skin follows a moment behind.

## In the Bonecage

The last of the company's three cards in one night in Session 55, and the second Wildcard, for a 600 gp purse. They didn't know what they would face. What came out was the Skin-Sleeved, and the company beat it. The night's three purses came to 1,150 gp.

<p><strong><em>Living Outer Skin.</em></strong> The attached skin has AC 14 and 60 hit points. Damage dealt to it does not reduce the inner frame's hit points. If the skin is destroyed, the Skin-Sleeved loses its physical damage resistances and cannot use Cast the Sleeve or Tighten. The skin cannot regain hit points.</p>
<p><strong><em>One Continuous Organ.</em></strong> The Skin-Sleeved can cover only one creature at a time. Forced movement does not detach the skin, but moving the Skin-Sleeved more than 30 feet from its covered target pulls that target with it.</p>
<h4>Actions</h4>
<p><strong><em>Multiattack.</em></strong> The Skin-Sleeved makes three Cartilage Strut attacks.</p>
<p><strong><em>Cartilage Strut.</em></strong> <em>Melee Weapon Attack:</em> +11 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. <em>Hit:</em> 19 (3d8 + 6) bludgeoning damage.</p>
<p><strong><em>Cast the Sleeve (Recharge 4–6).</em></strong> The Skin-Sleeved throws its attached outer skin at one Large or smaller creature within 20 feet. The target must succeed on a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw or become covered. A covered creature is grappled (escape DC 18), restrained, blinded, unable to breathe, and has total cover against effects originating outside the skin. It can attack the skin from within without disadvantage.</p>
<p><strong><em>Tighten.</em></strong> One creature covered by the outer skin takes 36 (8d8) bludgeoning damage. The Skin-Sleeved then moves the creature up to 15 feet to an unoccupied space beside the inner frame.</p>
<h4>Bonus Actions</h4>
<p><strong><em>Lagging Drag.</em></strong> The Skin-Sleeved moves up to half its speed. A creature covered by its skin moves with it and does not provoke opportunity attacks.</p>
<h4>Reactions</h4>
<p><strong><em>Skin Recoil.</em></strong> When the outer skin takes 20 or more damage during one turn, the Skin-Sleeved releases any covered creature into an unoccupied space within 5 feet.</p>
