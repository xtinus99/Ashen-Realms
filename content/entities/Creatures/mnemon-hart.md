## What it looks like

A towering white deer with six legs and long split hooves. Three cloudy facets sit among its branching antlers, one smoky red, one bruised violet and one cold blue.

## Outside Cerebrax

In Session 54 Farkas spotted one in the Idea-Dunes outside Cerebrax, grazing on a pale ridge across a shallow basin and lifting its crown between mouthfuls. He told the others, and the company fought it and killed it.

<p><strong><em>Three Recall Facets.</em></strong> The Hart has three antler facets: <strong>Blow</strong>, <strong>Word</strong>, and <strong>Passage</strong>. Each is a Medium object attached to it (AC 17; 20 hit points; immunity to poison and psychic damage). An attacker within 30 feet can target a facet without penalty. Damage dealt to a facet is also dealt to the Hart. A destroyed facet cannot light or be used again during the encounter.</p>
<p>An intact dark facet lights when its event occurs within 60 feet of the Hart: Blow when the Hart takes damage from an attack; Word when the Hart takes damage from a spell or fails a saving throw against a magical effect; Passage when a creature the Hart can see moves at least 20 feet during one turn. A lit facet remains lit until spent. The glow and the event recorded inside it are plainly visible.</p>
<p><strong><em>Deflecting Facets.</em></strong> When the Hart is targeted by a spell attack, or is the only target of a spell, one of its intact facets can catch the spell. The Hart is unaffected, and the spell shoots out of that facet at a random creature within 60 feet of the Hart other than the Hart itself, which can be the caster. The spell uses the caster's spell attack bonus and spell save DC. A spell with an area of effect can't be caught, and each facet can catch only one spell.</p>
<p><strong><em>Facet Ward.</em></strong> While at least one of its facets is intact, the Hart has resistance to all damage.</p>
<p><strong><em>Refuse the Record (Reaction).</em></strong> When the Hart takes damage, it can darken one lit facet to reduce that damage by 13 (3d8).</p>
<h4>Actions</h4>
<p><strong><em>Multiattack.</em></strong> The Hart makes one Crown-Gore attack and two Split-Hoof attacks.</p>
<p><strong><em>Crown-Gore.</em></strong> <em>Melee Weapon Attack:</em> +10 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. <em>Hit:</em> 22 (3d10 + 6) piercing damage.</p>
<p><strong><em>Split-Hoof.</em></strong> <em>Melee Weapon Attack:</em> +10 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. <em>Hit:</em> 15 (2d8 + 6) bludgeoning damage.</p>
<h4>Legendary Actions</h4>
<p>The Hart can take 2 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. It can take only one at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. It regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.</p>
<p><strong><em>Remembered Passage.</em></strong> The Hart darkens its lit Passage facet and moves up to its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.</p>
<p><strong><em>Recited Blow (Costs 2 Actions).</em></strong> The Hart darkens its lit Blow facet and makes one Crown-Gore attack.</p>
<p><strong><em>Thought Chime (Costs 2 Actions).</em></strong> The Hart darkens its lit Word facet. One creature it can see within 60 feet must make a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw, taking 22 (5d8) psychic damage and losing its reactions until the end of its next turn on a failed save, or taking half as much damage on a successful one.</p>
