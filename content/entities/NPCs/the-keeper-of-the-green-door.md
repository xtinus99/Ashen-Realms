## At the door

He answered with his sleeves rolled to the elbow and a linen apron over his clothes, his hands red from hot water. He has the steady tiredness of someone who's been tired for a long time, and he looks at sick people the way someone who has spent years with them does.

## Session 59

Viktor came to his door with his purple veins. The Keeper examined them, said he'd never seen it before and that it was in the vessels themselves, and asked whether Viktor heard anything with it. Viktor read nothing in him but a careful, tired carer. When Viktor told him about his connection, the Keeper took him up to his four residents, who don't talk and aren't there behind the eyes.

Viktor told him what he heard from the fourth. The Keeper said nothing about it. Shown Viktor's crystal, he kept his hands at his sides and called it a stone, didn't ask who Viktor's patron was, and told him to keep it put away, since people in the city look at anything shiny like it's a mirror. When the fourth resident's shadow answered Viktor, he was surprised that Viktor could communicate with the patient.
