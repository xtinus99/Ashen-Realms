## The perch

In Session 45, heading south off the floe toward the Liftworks, the company climbed to a perch in the Tidebreak rib-cliffs and asked the old man living there who he was.

He told them only that he was a hermit. He gave nothing else away: not why he keeps to the cold edge, and not what he watches for out there. He handed them a warmth stone for the road and let them go on their way.
