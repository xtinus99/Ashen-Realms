Every scale of his coat has a little seal struck into it. One high on his chest is newer than the rest, where he cut out an oath he once broke.

## Session 63

He told Veezara that a paladin's power going out in his hand means a broken oath, and showed him Lemnor's way back: say aloud to a witness what he failed to do, then swear again on hot iron. At the deep forge he heard Veezara out without judging, watched him strike his oath into bronze, and gave him the struck scale on a cord.
