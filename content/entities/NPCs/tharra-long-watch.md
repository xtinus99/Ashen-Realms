## The keeper

A full giant of King Hrothgar's kingdom on the western high plains, sent to sit with the first giants' dead so that they are never left alone. She has kept the rim eleven winters, and speaks to the dead under her breath in a deep old tongue.

## Session 62

She read the clan-marks on Farkas's maul as her own line's, and it glowed in her hand: he is of her blood. Farkas told her he believed they had come from the future, and then the whole history of his world, and she believed him. Told that no one sat with the dead after the kingdoms fell, she said she would sit with them the harder while she was there. She gave the company her hall for the night and asked Farkas to come back to the rim before he goes home.
