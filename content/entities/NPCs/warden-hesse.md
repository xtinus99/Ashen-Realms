## The toll

Travellers who come to Calvaria by road meet Hesse under the arch, and it takes a toll from each of them before they pass. Leaving the city costs nothing.

## What the company paid

Hesse asked each of the company for a secret. Farkas, Viktor and Karusa each gave one, nothing that mattered, and went through. Veezara refused. Hesse took hold of him and put him back outside the arch, and he is **barred from the gate for good**.

Veezara got into Calvaria anyway, because Poor Tam spoke to Hesse.
