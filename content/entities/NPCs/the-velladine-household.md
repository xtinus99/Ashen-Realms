## Mr. Hobb

An elderly man in immaculate black. In Session 53 he opened the manor's doors before anyone knocked.

> “Welcome to Velladine Manor. His Lordship is delighted that the road has brought us company. May I have the names by which you wish to be announced?”<br>— Mr. Hobb

The company gave him their names. The evening reception had already started, and His Lordship would not receive new guests halfway through an introduction, so Hobb set their reception for six the next evening. He led them past silver lamps and family portraits to guest rooms that were already made up, and introduced Farkas and Viktor to the guests in the drawing room. When Viktor suggested he might be able to arrange a trade, Hobb gave him a tour of the wine collection. At six he escorted the company into the ballroom and announced them.

## The butlers

One was working on the garden out front. One told the company that the music box in the ballroom was a gift from the lord's wife, to be handled with care, and that nobody was allowed to touch it. When the company switched it off anyway, one came rushing over, switched it straight back on, and asked them to please be more careful.

## The carriage handlers

Two of them, sitting out front and watching a butler work on the garden. When Farkas asked who they were, they said they were carriage handlers. They had driven some of the guests up to the manor, and the horses were over in the stables. Farkas went and looked. The stables were empty.

## The guests

<img src="images/S53 Drawing Room.webp" alt="The drawing-room guests at Velladine Manor" class="content-image">

About a dozen of them, well dressed, in the drawing room between the fire and two long sofas. Near the far wall a pair gave the same short laugh twice, at the same interval, with the same tilt of the head, and nobody remarked on it. Viktor tested them with an ability: they were not alive. He and Farkas took them for ghosts, though the result didn't say what they actually were.

Hobb introduced three of them.

**Lady Cecily Vaund** warned against hurrying a third movement.

**Justice Brannick** insisted the western boundary levy was temporary.

**Ruy Varro** is a fencer, bragging by the fireplace. When Viktor asked him about the next evening, Ruy told him that Hobb would announce the guests at six, before the waltz and supper, and that Lord Velladine would come down later with his family. Then he went back to his boast.

> “Aurelian will not touch me before the fifth exchange.”<br>— Ruy Varro

His wrist turned too far as he gestured at the fire, then settled back under his cuff. Viktor asked him what year it was, and Ruy answered without a moment's hesitation.

> “Fourteen hundred and thirty-six, Aedwynn's Walk.”<br>— Ruy Varro

Outside the manor it was 2,836 AS.

## Miravel, Tavian and Neris

<img src="images/S53 Reception.webp" alt="The evening reception at Velladine Manor" class="content-image">

Aurelian's wife, son and daughter. They came down to the reception with him: Miravel on his arm, Tavian and Neris one stair behind. When Aurelian offered Miravel his hand she took it on the next measure, and Tavian and Neris stood at the side of the floor and watched their parents dance.

By Aurelian's own account, his wife and children were killed in front of him more than four thousand years ago.
