## On the road

In Session 55 the company left Cerebrax on the Ossathari road and found someone walking the same way: tall and broad, with a curved frame rising over his shoulders like a lantern handle. They closed on him until they could hear heavy footsteps and chain shifting under plate.

He turned without breaking stride. His head is a long metal shape with a thin amber slit in it, and a small oil flame burns behind a brass grille in his breastplate. His armour is smoke-black with worn brass edges, his cloak is ragged, and a poleaxe rides sheathed along the carrying frame.

He is a warforged. Apart from Anvil, none of the company had ever seen another, so they confronted him.

> “Designation, O.R.V.E.K.”<br>— O.R.V.E.K

## The errand

> “O.R.V.E.K must gather medicine located east of Ossathar. Medicine known as bone poppy.”<br>— O.R.V.E.K

The company decided to go with him. On the road he told them about his work, and why he hadn't been doing it.

> “Function of O.R.V.E.K: escort travellers. Travellers reach Cerebrax safely. Father's health has declined. O.R.V.E.K has been unable to perform function.”<br>— O.R.V.E.K

The bone poppy was for his father.

> “Bone poppy will help father remember. Father sometimes forgets O.R.V.E.K.”<br>— O.R.V.E.K

At dusk he refilled the lantern in his chest, and at camp he stood motionless and awake through his own rest.

## The chalk cut

In a shaded chalk cut the company ran into three dazhuls, and O.R.V.E.K destroyed all three. Afterwards he slid the poleaxe back into the frame over his shoulders and checked his lantern.

## Last Tally and the cave

At Last Tally he went to the hospice and waited until Loveday had finished with a patient before asking her for bone poppies for his father's medicine. She sold him three dried heads. He needed only two, so he gave the third to Viktor. When Viktor wanted more and Loveday pointed them to a cave, O.R.V.E.K agreed to come along and keep them safe.

He led them in through the widest gap in the threads at the cave mouth, holding a strand of bone-silk aside with one gauntlet. Seven weavers came for the company in the cave, and all seven died.

## Parting

After a long rest in Loveday's hospice, O.R.V.E.K left in the morning with his medicine, bound back for Cerebrax.
