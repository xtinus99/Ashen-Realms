A giant of the duelling field, about thirty-five feet tall like the others there. He speaks simply and slowly, as the field's giants do.

## Session 63

Farkas beat his boy one on one. Tostig sat down in the dirt to hear Farkas's story and the maul's, liked the part about Hadrik best, and said the fall of the giants' kingdoms sounded like a lie, though Farkas didn't look like a liar. He knew the maul for western giant work. He has never been west and doesn't know the smith's name, but he sent Farkas to Phlegre, Hrothgar's city, to ask for the smith who reads the marks, and told him of Ceolric, Hrothgar's warleader, who fights on the field twice a year.
