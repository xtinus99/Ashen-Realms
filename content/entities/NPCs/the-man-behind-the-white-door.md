## What he looks like

Cased head to foot in white lime, smooth even over his face apart from one slot for the eyes. Each of his wrists ends in a bunch of grey hands, all moving.

## Session 59

He told the company to go away. They kept pushing and opened his door anyway, and once he'd decided they weren't there to harm him he let his guard down. Asked who he was, he said it was none of their business. All he wanted was more bodies, he said, and his brethren would have their vengeance soon. The company made out that he was a Shadar-Kai, asked him a few questions about the Isles and left.
