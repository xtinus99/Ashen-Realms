A tall human woman of about forty with short red-brown hair and a scar through one eyebrow. She captains Lemnor's levies and knows every soldier by name. They call her "Mam".

## Session 63

She told Veezara what the south knows of the war in the north: Tiryn and Lycene have fought over the chalk uplands between them for longer than anyone remembers, and nobody agrees who started it. She took Veezara's and Karusa's names, beat Veezara in the yard and gave him a potion of healing, and sent her levies running laps with the promise of the tavern after. She asked what had brought the company to Lemnor, and nobody has told her.
