## The commander

He commands at the Northern Containment, and the way into the dome is his to give. The company found him in a tent at the Ash Yard, doing paperwork, after the two guards on duty sent them to him.

## What he told the company

In Session 56 the company asked whether they could go into the dome. He scoffed. They could, he said; it would be their funeral. A group of guards had gone into the hole a few weeks ago and never come back, so they could do what they liked with that.

## The company's visit

The company went into the dome, saw the creatures walking around it and the hole in the middle, and left.
