An elf pyromancer of the Bastion of Fireblood, with platinum hair slicked back, a scorched charcoal coat over red, brass vials at his belt and smoke coming off his cuffs.

## Session 62

He came down in the lane outside the house on the Ring on a scroll of *teleport*, insulting the company from the start, and went through the portal with them only because Veezara didn't want to see him cross alone. He kept the watch alone in the great forest when Adelais, the woman on the dark horse, came into the firelight.

Near Epidaure he wanted vengeance on Talaris for the war Talaris wages on the Bastion in his own time. After a moral debate Veezara agreed, and Malcius burned Talaris to death with *fireball*. In Pergame's church he was rude to a nun, asking whether she knew anyone named Mareatha. The next morning he was arrested, sentenced by the council of eight, and beheaded at the guillotine before the fountain.

He woke outside the house on the Ring, in his own time, without his things, and long rested there.
