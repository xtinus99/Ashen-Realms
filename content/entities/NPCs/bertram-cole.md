## What he looks like

A man split down the middle: frost on his left half, cooling-iron cracks glowing on his right. He carries a double-headed axe of ice and red-hot iron.

## What the company knows

He is a consort of Kaedris; they knew it from a History check when they saw him.

## Session 60

As the company left the Umbrasseur Isles by boat, they saw him entering. At the bridge at the neck they found Gulm maimed by frost and fire; Gulm said the fire man had attacked him when he only asked for food. In the Blooming Rot they told a lizardfolk fort captain about him, and the captain sent the news out into the Ways.
