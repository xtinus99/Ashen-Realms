## The man in the elf's face

<img src="images/S60 Elf-Faced Man.webp" alt="A man wearing a handsome young elf's face, his shadow measuring with its hands on the wall behind him" class="content-image">

A handsome young elf's face with blond hair tied back, a good dark coat and grey gloves, and a voice twenty years older than the face. He did the talking in the arcade outside the reading room: somebody had paid for Veezara's face. Give it up and that was the end of it; if not, they would take it while he slept, and his friends slept too. While he talked, his shadow on the wall measured the width of Veezara's jaw.

He came through the window of Farkas's room that night and was killed. On him the company found a maskwright's brass fitting tag stamped *P. LIGHTFOOT · MASKWRIGHT · THE ROW* and *FITTER · No. 3*, 85 gp and some silver, grey kid gloves, a pair of Dimensional Shackles lined with grey leather stamped S.K., and a jar of skin-dressing oil. The young elf's face he wore carried no licence number.

## The docker

<img src="images/S60 The Docker.webp" alt="A big man in a heavy docker's face, a laced seam up the back of his neck" class="content-image">

Big, in a heavy docker's face with a laced seam up the back of his neck. He stood behind Veezara in the arcade. He came through the window with the others, escaped, and saw Karusa without her hat.

## The patched thing

<img src="images/S60 Patched Face.webp" alt="A man-shaped thing in a patched, stitched face, and a hide folded on the floor" class="content-image">

A man-shaped thing in a patched face that didn't blink when it should, moving low along the wall like a coat being dragged. It couldn't come in under the salted door. It was killed, and its hide lay folded against the wall afterwards with its face beside it.
