## Four in black

A carter at the Ash Yard had seen a party of four walking toward the mountains west of the Northern Containment, on some contract. In Session 57 the company went west along the coast to the north crag, found a camp outside a cave, and found the four inside, already fighting the dragon.

They fought it together. It got up three times, each time with less of itself, and the fourth time it stayed down and burst. The burst killed Veezara, and Lazare brought him back at Drey's word. Afterwards Drey thanked the company and told them they had a share coming: some of the dragon's hoard, lying between the rocks, and a quarter of the job's pay, **about 3,000 gp**. The four kept an orb of necrotic energy from what was left.

They shared their stew on the ledge, and when they left for Crownfall they asked if the company wanted to come along. The company said no and wished them well.

## In Crownfall

In Session 58 the company went to the Selvik operation in Crownfall and asked for their bounty, giving Augustin Drey's name. After a while Drey turned up himself, confirmed who they were, and they were handed their quarter for the dragon: **3,000 gp**.

## Drey

The broad man in front, fighting behind a coffin he uses as a shield. He runs the job. When the dragon's last burst killed Veezara, he told Lazare to get him up. He wanted to know why the dragon had stayed down before he told the Hall it was done.

> “Get him up, it is not yet his time.”<br>— Drey

> “It kept getting back up. This time it didn't.” … “I'd like to know why before I tell the Hall it's done.”<br>— Drey
