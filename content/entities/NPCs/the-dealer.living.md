## Session 60

Veezara came to the Green Door to buy information on legends the company could follow, and bought four from him at 50 gp each: the Wrong Hand, Certainty, the Meadow Girdle and the Dog-Bone Whistle. Their stories are in Four Legends from the Green Door.
