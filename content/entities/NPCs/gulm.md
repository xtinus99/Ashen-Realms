## On the bridge

Squat and barrel-bodied and exactly as wide as the bridge, crouched with a knee and a set of huge knuckles on each parapet. He has no neck, just a broad flat jaw across his whole front with four small dark eyes set wide above it. His left arm is enormous. His right is long and thin, and its hand lies open on the bridge in front of him, palm up. His voice is small and mild, like an ordinary man's.

> “Food? You got food?”<br>“Gulm eat first. Then you walk.”

## Session 59

He wouldn't let the company pass without food. Viktor tried *modify memory* on him, which angered him, and he attacked Viktor. Veezara tried to walk past him and was flung back. Karusa handed him a ration and he let her pass, and the others did the same. He told them there was no need for violence, and that he doesn't like violence. Farkas wouldn't pay, and went round by the coast.

## Session 60

When the company came back north, nobody was sitting on the bridge. Halfway across, the stone was split and white with frost in one long stripe, and the parapet was scorched black. Gulm was wedged on a rubble ledge under the arch. His long right arm was gone at the shoulder, the stump frost-blackened, his face was burned raw down one side, his big left fist was burned, and two of his four eyes were swollen shut. He asked quietly if they had food.

Asked why he didn't go and get food himself, he said it was too far, and he would wait. He didn't understand why the fire man was so angry: he had only asked him for food, and the man attacked him. And he didn't want to go back, because they always take his food. The company told him they might come back to check on him.
