## Faro, the doorman

<img src="images/S58 Faro.webp" alt="Faro, a bull-headed demon in a brass-buttoned greatcoat with an iron door-bar over his shoulder" class="content-image">

A bull-headed demon in a brass-buttoned greatcoat, with an iron door-bar over his shoulder, standing on the casino steps. He told the company to leave their weapons at the counters to the right as they went in. Farkas didn't want to hand his over, so Faro offered to hold it for him, and Farkas took one look at the size of him and agreed.

## Tontine, the cashier

<img src="images/S58 Tontine.webp" alt="Tontine, a grey tiefling cashier chained by the wrist to her counter" class="content-image">

A grey tiefling chained by the wrist to her own counter in the cashier's cage. She sells chips for gold only, one for one. When the company came in she was pushing a man's eleven coins back at him because he'd said twenty. Viktor and Karusa bought 50 gp of chips from her, and Viktor later cashed his back in at her cage.

## Primero, the host

<img src="images/S58 Primero.webp" alt="Primero, a blue-grey tiefling host in a white dinner jacket, with a serpent's tail and a scorpion sting" class="content-image">

A slim blue-grey tiefling in a white dinner jacket from the waist up and a long snake from the waist down, with a scorpion's sting curled over his shoulder. He found the company before they'd taken ten steps and introduced himself. He took the rest of their weapons and handed them to a worker behind a counter to box, explained that chips were gold only, one for one, and pointed out the rooms by colour.

## Mumchance, the pit boss

<img src="images/S58 Mumchance.webp" alt="Mumchance, a scarred tiefling with iron-capped horns and a dice cage on a chain" class="content-image">

The Dice Pit's pit boss, a huge scarred tiefling with broken horns capped in iron and a dice cage on a chain. He runs the giant iron cage at the back of the room: you call a number from one to six, he rolls three dice, and you're paid one to one for every die that matches. Viktor played it and cashed out ahead.

> “Brelan. On the table. Not on my rail.”<br>— Mumchance

## Brelan

<img src="images/S58 Brelan.webp" alt="Brelan, a lava-cracked demon with chained wrists" class="content-image">

A huge demon, black-skinned with orange glowing through the cracks. The house chains his wrists together before he plays. He lost a roll at craps while Viktor and Karusa were in the Dice Pit and slammed both fists on the rail.

## Piquet, the head dealer

<img src="images/S58 Piquet.webp" alt="Piquet, a grey tiefling dealer in a green visor, with a bandolier of steel cards" class="content-image">

The Card Room's head dealer. She deals steel cards that ring when they land and can't be bent or marked, and keeps one stuck upright in the rail as a warning. Viktor sat down to blackjack against her and lost all his chips: 70 gp.

## Écarté, the Crown of Horns

<img src="images/S58 Horned Crown.webp" alt="Écarté, a tiefling woman wearing a crown made of other people's horns" class="content-image">

A tiefling who had her own horns sawn off at the root and wears a crown of horns won from others. At the card table she offered a man her whole stack for his signet ring. He refused, and Piquet dealt on.

## Scopa, the Starving Lord

<img src="images/S58 Starving Lord.webp" alt="Scopa, a skeletal devil with a too-wide mouth and a sword-length silver fork" class="content-image">

A skeletal devil with a mouth too wide, eating in the orange dining room, with a sword-length silver fork worn at his side like a rapier. Veezara asked him for a word. It took some persuading, but Scopa told him where they were, Greed's casino, and that a fellow with a cat's head who got people out, for a price, could be seen hanging about outside. His name was Cattermole. Scopa added that he would pay for food from wherever they came from.

> “An ear. Sure, have one. Sit down, you're blocking the pie.”<br>— Scopa
