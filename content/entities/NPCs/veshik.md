## The ring

Ratsby watched Veshik's Curiosities on Salvager's Square for three afternoons before he made the grab in Session 40: a silver ring with a pale stone, off the third shelf. Veshik noticed the empty tray a second too early and hit the alarm-bell behind his counter.

> “Stop him! The ring! He took the ring — somebody stop him!”<br>— Veshik

Captain Jawbone brought Ratsby in.

## The fee

At the Kennels Watch guardhouse Veshik gave a short statement, got his ring back, checked it, and pocketed it.

> “That's the ring. We're square.”<br>— Veshik

Then he came back and invoked Section 9 of the Commercial Code, the complainant's inconvenience fee, and demanded fifteen gold. He called the figure standard for the species and flicked a hand at Ratsby without looking at him. The Section 9 table is a city-published one, and it is weighted against non-humans. Ratsby argued it, and Jawbone told him the shopkeep was allowed to be rude. Ratsby paid the fifteen.
