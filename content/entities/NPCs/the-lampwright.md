## At the rim

A heavy-jawed man in a brocade waistcoat with his hands black to the wrist from lamp-soot. He reads the house rules off the wall before a bout and takes the names.

## Session 60

He had to tip his head right back to look at Farkas. He gave him the night's card and purses, took one look at the size of Farkas's shadow with the lamp set low, and matched him against the Twice-Lit. When it was over he paid out 600 gp less the house's fifth: 480 gp.
