## The council

Eight who rule Pergame from carved chairs in a long hall under the great dome.

## Session 62

On Maren Thane's reading they sentenced Malcius to death for the killing of Talaris, and set Veezara a blood-price of 1,000 gp, paid to Talaris's widow and children, and banishment from the heartland. If he is found in Pergame or its lands again, he answers as Malcius did. Malcius was beheaded at the guillotine in the square before the fountain.
