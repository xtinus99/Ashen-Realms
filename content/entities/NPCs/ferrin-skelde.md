## Keeper of the Ribward book

Ferrin registers new fighters, offers cleared Wildcards, places ordinary cards, and receives promotion match requests. He is blunt about the ladder: enough wins earn the right to ask for a promotion match, but the promotion itself must still be won. On a Wildcard he never says what's waiting behind the gate.

## The party's record

Ferrin booked every party card from their first Splinter fight onward. Harrowmark gave them their first loss in Session 52, and by the end of Session 55 Farkas, Veezara and Karusa stood at **11–1** and Viktor at **10–1**.

In Session 56 the company asked him for a **Rib promotion match**. He offered four solo opponents and refused to book them another Rib pair, naming the Harrowmark loss as his reason. When they asked for the **Ossuary Concord** instead, the Ribward's own headline team, he set out the arithmetic without softening it and did not recommend it. They won. All four became **Ribward Ribs**: Farkas, Veezara and Karusa at **12–1**, Viktor at **11–1**.

He also told them what comes next. Sternum needs 15 credited wins, a 65% win rate and three wins against Rib opposition.

## The Reticul

In Session 57 they came back and asked for a Wildcard. As always with a Wildcard, he didn't tell them what it was. It came up out of the Deep Pens: a Reticul.

Like every Wildcard, it counted toward their records: Farkas, Veezara and Karusa went to 13–1 and Viktor to 12–1.

They beat it for a purse of 1,000 gp.
