An old giant, broad for his kind, with white hair and beard tied back and forearms burned smooth.

## Session 65

He looked at the Skyfather's Reckoning a long time and called it Long-Watch work. In his hand it glowed. He read its marks, the hearth first, and stopped at his own: "That is my mark." / "I never made this." / "This is my work. But it is old. Older than me." Farkas told him only that it came from his mother, and he took it that the maul had gone down the line, which is what he makes them for. He told Farkas about Ceolric, and sold him a Forgework Dragon Shield.
