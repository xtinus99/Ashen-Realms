## The column

On the trek to Veinspire in Session 47, the company came round a bone-spur into the line of a blood-red column: some forty soldiers in red tabards and grey mail behind crimson banners, with three wagons and blood-hounds ranging the flanks. It was marching west toward a grounded tower out past the bone-plain. The company dropped into a dry gully under Karusa's new cloak and let it pass. A blood-hound cast toward the gully, tested the air, and was whistled back.

> “We're almost at the tower! Keep marching!”<br>— the column commander

Two footsore soldiers were talking as the ranks filed past: something about cutting a thing off a woman and riding home, "the one that don't die," and a defector gone sour.

## At the gate

That night, before the company had found a bed in Veinspire, a rider came in at the red-stone gate off the western dark at a near-collapse gallop. He wore the blood-red and grey of the column, blood-stiff and shaking, with one arm gone at the shoulder. The guards hauled him down, and Viktor asked him what had happened.

It came out of him in pieces, laughing and weeping by turns: the tower, a woman, she killed everyone. She cut the column down without even seeming to try, then walked up the floor afterwards, laughing, and left. He lived by going down under a slash and lying still among his own dead until she was gone. Pressed, he kept coming back to one thing.

> “She used to be one of theirs. An inquisitor. She vanished years ago.”<br>— the column's survivor
