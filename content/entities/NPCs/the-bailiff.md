## What he looks like

An empty livery coat with white gloves at the ends of its sleeves, a gold half-mask over a shimmer where a face should be, the Court's seal on a chain, and a caged violet lantern on a pole. When he walks past, rooms go quiet and people find somewhere else to look.

## Session 60

He came into the Grey Pot while Veezara and Viktor were there and told the innkeeper he was on the food office's round for the Court and needed the kitchen and the service door. The innkeeper let him through. He finished in the kitchen, nodded once and left.
