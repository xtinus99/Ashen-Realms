He reads strangers for the queen.

## Session 64

He brought the company the queen's invitation, and said he would read them afterwards. In the Queen's Hall he read the walls aloud for Veezara: the queens of Acheront, the old paired kings and queens, King Osric's name struck through and Queen Avice's beside it, and the bare stone beside Elspeth's name.
