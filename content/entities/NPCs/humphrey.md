## At the camp

A lean, bald older man travelling with Goodknight and the carriage he was escorting to Cerebrax. When the company sat down at Goodknight's fire in Session 54, Humphrey handed the bowls of stew round, counted them, and went back to the carriage.

> “That's Humphrey. Keeps my armour together.”<br>— Goodknight

The company camped with them that night and travelled on with the carriage to Cerebrax after breakfast.
