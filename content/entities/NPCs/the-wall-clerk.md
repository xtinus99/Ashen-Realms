## Behind the counter

The senior clerk at the Roll, House Selvik's posting-board in the deep Kennels. When a job is done, the Wall-Clerk checks the proof, files it, pays out, and hangs a small token for it on the Notice Beam.

## The company's bounties

In Session 40 the clerk checked the Bonefield Stalker's head, confirmed the species, filed it and paid 90 gold, and a hooked-claw token went up on the Beam. Anvil mentioned that they had dealt with the cubs as well. The clerk filed the lineage as terminated and waived a small observation fee.

In Session 41 the same clerk checked the Brackish Pool Alpha's head and paid 75 gold, and a four-toed-paw token went up.

> “Second close in three days. You're climbing the Wall. Twin Reach still your next?”<br>— the Wall-Clerk

## Postings

In Session 40 the clerk briefed the company on two of the higher postings: the vault recovery at Twin Reach, and a survey of the Bastion approach that was above their standing by the Wall's rules, and which the clerk wouldn't open for them. In Session 42 the clerk briefed them on the caravan that didn't come home, and deferred the Twin Reach settlement to Steward Mavris.

## The cloaked figure

Also in Session 42, a huge cloaked figure ran out of patience behind the company, lifted a Red off the Wall and pushed up to the counter to claim it. The Wall-Clerk knew him on sight, and the colour drained out of his face. He stamped the claim through fast and did nothing to keep him.
