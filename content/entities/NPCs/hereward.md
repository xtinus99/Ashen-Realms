At his belt hangs a long knife of dark iron, longer than a man, with clan-marks scarred into the base of the blade.

## Session 64

Hereward's aurochs had broken out across the road with a storm coming. The company helped him and his sons get them in, then fought the roc that came with the storm beside him, and killed it; for the first time in ten storms it took no cow. He fed them in his hall and in the morning pointed them west to Phlegre.

## Session 65

In the morning a stair had opened in the floor of his hall while he slept. The company shook him awake, and he was as confused by it as they were. He waited at the edge of the frost, and when they came back up he said, "You came back."
