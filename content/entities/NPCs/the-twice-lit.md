## On his step

He wears a fencing master's face, lean and grey-haired with a pointed grey beard, with a buttoned black doublet and tall boots, and keeps his gloved hands behind his back. He pays the house extra for two lamps. Hung either side of his step, they throw two shadows across the white, both in a fencer's guard, both holding a long thin blade that isn't in his hands. His feet never move.

## Session 60

Farkas fought him for a 600 gp purse and his shadow won. The Twice-Lit nodded across the pit at Farkas before the bout, politely, like a man at a dinner, gave him the same small nod after it, and went up the far stair.
