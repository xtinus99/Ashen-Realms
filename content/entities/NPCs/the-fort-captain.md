## The captain

He commands a fort short of warriors since the levy. The ground told him the company was coming before they arrived: four, one very heavy, carrying no fire.

## Session 60

He gave the company food and hammocks and asked where they were going, since east of the fort there was only the lake. When they told him about Bertram Cole going into the Umbrasseur Isles he took it as war news, went down a root-stair under the keep, and sent it out into the Ways.
