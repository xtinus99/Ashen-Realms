## Salvager's Square

Captain Jawbone came into Salvager's Square in Session 40, a few moments after Veshik's alarm-bell: a senior deputy in his late forties, in a brown Watch surcoat, with a plated iron jaw and a bad left foot. The jaw clicks on every other step. He stopped fifteen feet from Farkas and didn't draw, and the crowd backed off. He read the scene: the goblin in the giant's hair, the giant standing calm, the warforged within talking distance, and Veshik a hundred feet back, pointing. His eyes stayed on Anvil a beat longer than they needed to.

> “Right. All three of you. With me.”<br>— Jawbone

He asked Ratsby quietly whether he knew the giant and the warforged, and told him a lie would make the next half hour longer. Ratsby said they were friends. It fell apart a moment later, when Farkas asked at full volume what Ratsby was going to get him out of, and Anvil said plainly that the goblin was no friend of his. Jawbone let Anvil and Farkas go with statements. When Ratsby tried to hand over a pebble instead of the ring, Jawbone just looked at him until he gave it up.

Farkas refused to come along. Jawbone took a long look at the half-giant's face, filed it, and walked south with Anvil and Ratsby.

## The guardhouse

At the Kennels Watch guardhouse, Veshik demanded the complainant's inconvenience fee under Section 9 of the Commercial Code: fifteen gold, which he called standard for the species. Ratsby argued the number was Veshik's bigotry. Jawbone answered him level and without heat.

> “The shopkeep was rude. The shopkeep is allowed to be rude. You're in Crownfall, get used to it.”<br>— Jawbone

It was fifteen gold or three days at the Coin Street workhouse. Ratsby paid, and on his way out called Jawbone a metal-jawed freak.

> “Goblin. I've been called worse by smaller.”<br>— Jawbone

Jawbone has Ratsby's face and his booking on file.
