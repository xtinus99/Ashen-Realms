## The portrait

The company saw his portrait before they met him. It hangs in the hall of Velladine Manor with the other family portraits: a tall, pallid man in formal black, with a faded wine-red ribbon tying back his hair and a slender sword at his side. Viktor rolled a 23 on Medicine studying it, came away suspecting a vampire, and told the others.

## The reception

About twenty minutes into the reception in Session 53, Aurelian came down in formal black with Miravel on his arm, and Tavian and Neris one stair behind.

> “My wife, Miravel. Our son, Tavian. Our daughter, Neris. This house has had too many evenings without new names in it. You honour us by lending it yours.”<br>— Aurelian Velladine

When the company asked about the world outside the estate, he seemed not to recognise that any of it had changed. He answered as if the manor's world were still the present one.

## What he told them

Pressed further, he told them that more than four thousand years ago he had fought for his city. A group of vampires invited him to join them, and he refused. He was turned, and his wife and children were killed in front of him. Veezara asked who had done it, and Aurelian named **Faustus Sangrave**.

He wants vengeance. The company judged that a straight fight with Faustus would be a death sentence, and the conversation brought them no closer to him. They came to see their host as a frail man turned against his will, not an evil to be struck down in his own house. Nobody promised him vengeance.

Viktor asked him about his blackened veins. Aurelian had seen marks like them on members of a cult he destroyed in the South, a cult that had taken people from a nearby village for sacrifices. He gave no name for the cult, and no place or date.

## One night

The company asked to stay one night, and he agreed. He would be fine, he said, as long as he could see his wife. Then he turned from his guests and offered Miravel his hand for the dance.

> “You are welcome to join us. Hobb will show you back to your rooms whenever you wish.”<br>— Aurelian Velladine

In the morning the music box was off and the manor was a ruin. His room was empty. The company sensed him down in his coffin, left him undisturbed, and went on toward Cerebrax.
