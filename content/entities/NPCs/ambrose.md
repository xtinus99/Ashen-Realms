## Session 64

He went into the hallway as far as the first turning, and something took his oldest achievement scar off his arm. He kept a chalk line and two guards on Demelza's parlour floor, and asked the company to bring back what it took. When the frame closed behind them his scar was back. He went up to the queen to stand for them.
