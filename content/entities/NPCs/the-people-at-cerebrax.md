## The gate assessor

An assessor in slate-grey with three styluses pinned in her hair, at one of the desks at the Assessor's Gate. She stamped a traveller's report and pointed the company to the back of the queue.

> “End of the line, please. One traveller at a time.”<br>— the gate assessor

## A gate guard

At the gate, Viktor tried talking to one of the guards telepathically. The guard wasn't having it and told him to stop. Brancalic, the city's language, passes entirely mind to mind, and Viktor can understand it but not say anything back in it.

## A senior reader

A stooped woman in black, at work in the Living Stacks. Viktor and Veezara saw her hand a folio back to an attendant, correct something, and go back to her work.

## The man in the cream coat

A pale man in a cream coat, standing at the edge of a three-hundred-foot drop outside Cerebrax with a notebook under one arm. When Farkas came near, the man brightened, excited and cheerful, and asked if Farkas would like to join him.

Farkas told him it was pointless. So the man jumped.

He fell all the way to the shelf below. About fifteen minutes later he came climbing back up over the edge, saw Farkas, and asked: since Farkas didn't want to join him, maybe his friend would? That was how Farkas spotted Karusa, who had followed him out without his knowing. The man put the same question to her, and she gave him a firm no.

So he jumped again. Farkas sighed and left him to his experiment.
