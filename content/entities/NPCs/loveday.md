## The hospice

Last Tally's hospice has forty cots and a stove, and boots on a shelf. When the company came in with O.R.V.E.K in Session 55, Loveday was moving quietly between the sleepers with a tin lamp.

O.R.V.E.K waited until she had finished with a patient, then asked her for bone poppies for his father's medicine. Loveday sold him three dried heads.

## The cave

Viktor wanted more poppies and asked Loveday where he could find them. She pointed the company to a cave out past the end of the paving. They found the poppies there, growing out of picked bones at the back of a weavers' nest.

When they came back, they took a long rest on her cots.
