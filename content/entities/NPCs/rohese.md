## Session 62

She watched the company from a stool beside her father's throne, then came after them down the pillared hall to ask whether there was an Ithake in their world. Veezara told her there was nothing there but death, and then about the whole world as he knew it. She was deeply shaken, backed away from them, and went back to the throne room doors alone.
