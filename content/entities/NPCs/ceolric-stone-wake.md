Huge even for a giant, in riveted iron plate and a fur cloak, with a scar across the nose and bronze rings in his ears. He drills the young at first light, and wants a war the kingdoms won't give him. Tostig of the duelling field knows him: he comes east to fight there twice a year.

## Session 65

Farkas told him he was half giant, and Ceolric laughed: "Maybe quarter giant." Farkas asked him a number of questions and thanked him.
