## Session 64

The company found him at the foot of the grey stair, sitting in the ninth chair at a table of faceless things in a grey copy of his mother's house, his arm and half his face gone smooth. When they killed the one wearing his name he knew himself again. Veezara carried him out, and when the frame closed he was whole again.
