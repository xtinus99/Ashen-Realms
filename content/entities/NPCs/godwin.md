## Session 62

He came up the lane on his stick when four armed strangers appeared in it, and told them where they stood. She had drunk at the village well some days before, walking west toward the Middle, and nobody had seen her since. He took their portal for a riddle, cared only that they did no harm, let Veezara look round the village, and asked them to send word to Amphiale if they saw her.
