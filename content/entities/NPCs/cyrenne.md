## In the Living Stacks

In Session 54, at a crystal table in the Living Stacks, a woman with ash-silver hair was checking a crystal record against a written passage. Viktor recognised her as his mother. She looked up as an attendant passed, and her eyes stopped on him, stylus held over the unfinished line.

Viktor tried to convince her he was her son. She refused to accept it, and stayed at her work.

Only Viktor and Veezara saw it.

## Bone poppies

On the road out of Cerebrax, O.R.V.E.K said he was fetching bone poppy because it would help his father remember him. Viktor thought the poppies might come in handy: his mother didn't remember him either. At Last Tally he got one dried head from O.R.V.E.K and asked Loveday where to find more, thinking he'd be making the same medicine, and the company picked four more in the weaver cave.
