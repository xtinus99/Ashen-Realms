## Session 62

She took the company's weapons at the church door, and Farkas's maul nearly pulled her arms out of their sockets. Veezara showed her the torn badge from the house on the Ring; it meant nothing to her. The next morning she came to the inn with two more guards, took Malcius to a guardhouse for the killing of Talaris, and brought him before the council. After he died she gave his things to Veezara.
