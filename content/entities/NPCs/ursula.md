She cuts the first marks on Acheront's newborns, and has cut them on every child born in the city these forty years.

## Session 63

She saw Karusa unmarked, asked whose child she was, and was told "Nobody's." She offered to cut Karusa's identity marks with a name of her own choosing. Asked for her name, her mother's line and her island, Karusa said: "Karusa. No mother. No island." Ursula cut the name into the inside of her left wrist and left the other two places bare, because she would not cut what wasn't true.
