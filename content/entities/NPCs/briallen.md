## Session 62

She knelt with her hands on the moss and tried to find the Maker, and couldn't find her anywhere. Warned of the Usurpation, she judged that the Maker isn't lost or harmed but doesn't wish to be seen, and told the company she was last seen going to the Middle. When they came back from it half dead, she gave them a place to rest, looked down the Middle through the stream's water, and saw it green.
