## The Hall of Mending

<img src="images/Hall of Mending.webp" alt="The Hall of Mending in Crownfall" class="content-image">

In Session 43, while the company re-armed in Crownfall after Ratsby's resurrection, Anvil went to the Hall of Mending. He said he wanted to watch people suffer and try to understand it.

Edra met him as a stranger, gave him nothing, and turned him back out.
