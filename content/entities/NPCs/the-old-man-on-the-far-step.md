## On the far step

An old Shadar-Kai, gaunt, grey and barefoot, standing on a step set apart from the others with his hands at his sides. He doesn't move at all.

## What people say

The lantern-seller at the Last Lamp said everybody goes to watch him, and that he has been winning for eleven years. The Lampwright put him on the card at 1,500 gp and said he'd bet against him, as everybody does.

## Session 60

He stood on his step through the whole of Farkas's bout with the Twice-Lit, and still hadn't moved when it was over.
