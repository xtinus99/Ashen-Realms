## At the Common Cup

When Farkas, Anvil and Ratsby walked into the Common Cup in Session 40, Anvil picked out Torvin Drennan at the bar: well dressed, drinking a House vintage, with a forged compass-pin at his collar. He noted them coming in but didn't come over.

Later that night Korven Strake walked Ratsby out past the bar on his way to the guardhouse, and Torvin spoke up.

> “Another job, Korven?”<br>— Torvin Drennan

> “No shortage of these kinds of people in the Kennels.”<br>— Korven Strake

Korven didn't slow down. Torvin raised his wine an inch. He finished it past midnight and left, still without coming over.

The Velvet Thorn, the inn where the company stayed in Crownfall, is affiliated with House Drennan.
