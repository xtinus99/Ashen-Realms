## Outside the arch

The company met Poor Tam in Session 56. He was sitting outside Calvaria's road gate when Warden Hesse put Veezara out for refusing to give it a secret.

When Veezara asked Tam to help him get in, Tam told him a joke. Veezara couldn't resist it and laughed helplessly for a full minute. Then Tam went and spoke to Hesse, and Veezara was let in. Nobody named a price for that, and nobody said Veezara owed him anything.

## The game

Before the company left Calvaria, Tam offered them a game: make him laugh, and draw one card from **the Ashen Deck** he carries. Farkas, Veezara and Viktor all failed. Karusa made him laugh and drew.

Her card was **the Open Grave**:

> *An empty grave waits beneath a name you have not yet spoken.*

She spoke a name too quietly for anyone to catch it. Nothing appeared beside her, and the card's face faded blank. The deck answers a person once, so her sitting with it is over.

> “I knew it wouldn't work... of course you would be unwilling...”<br>— Karusa
