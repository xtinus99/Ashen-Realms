## Session 62

The company met him in the woods near Epidaure, collecting herbs to make medicine, and he told them his name. He seemed far too different from the Talaris they knew. After a moral debate Veezara agreed that Malcius should attack him, and Malcius burned him to death with *fireball*. His last words were "Why...?" and "Alys, take the kids and run...".
