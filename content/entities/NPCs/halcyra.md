A long green-bronze dragon crusted with coral and barnacles, who lies along a black reef in the strait.

## Session 64

The company passed her as she played a game with a sea captain she called Mordecai, blowing him off the end of a plank with a jet of boiling steam. When they went out onto the reef she had never seen a warforged or a gold dragonborn, and asked them what they were.
