## At the desk

A lean man in a severe black coat with the pale, ordinary face of a middle-aged clerk. When he writes an arrival into his ledger, his shadow on the wall behind him writes along with him, with a stylus of its own.

## Session 59

He registered the company and explained the visitor bond before they agreed to anything: a paper token, and a mark that goes on the shadow. They gave the Grey Pot as their lodging.
