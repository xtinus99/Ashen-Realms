## Supper in the Outer Folds

Toward evening in the Outer Folds, the company saw a carriage ahead of them come to a halt, and the people with it started making camp for the night. Escorting it was a broad man in rounded, banded armour, one shoulder plate missing, with a crooked golden sun on his faded tabard. With him were Humphrey and the travellers from the carriage.

The company went over. He lifted his visor on a weathered face and a greying moustache, introduced himself as Goodknight, and told them he was a knight.

When the company sat down to eat with him, he called it a very sound proposal and unstrapped a spare folding stool from the mule. He filled their bowls with hot stew before his own. After a while he asked whether they had come far.

> “That's Humphrey. Keeps my armour together.”<br>— Goodknight

## Morning

The company slept at his camp. At first light Goodknight stood a little apart with his visor up and his arms spread toward the warmth, then went back to stirring the pot. After breakfast the company travelled on with the carriage to Cerebrax.
