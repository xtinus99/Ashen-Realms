## The Common Cup

Kettleback keeps the Common Cup in the deep Kennels. In Session 40, after the spar in Salvager's Square, Farkas, Anvil and Ratsby walked there together, and she gave them a warm welcome. They sat at the bar. Ratsby ordered a small ale. Farkas ordered a barrel and tried to pay four silver with four gold, and Kettleback made the change without a word.

That night Korven Strake caught Ratsby at his bench and walked him to the guardhouse. Farkas and Anvil took rooms upstairs for four silver.

## The morning after

The next morning Farkas remembered Ratsby, after a delay, and asked Kettleback what had happened to him. She gave him the plain facts: Ratsby had been dragged away by a Selvik mercenary called Korven.
