## At the pond

When Viktor dripped his blood into the pond and denied it, the ground rumbled, a chair of roots grew up out of it, and then he was sitting in it.

## Session 61

He fought the company. He struck Viktor with *feeblemind*, and Viktor's patron took the empty body. When the company had killed what Viktor became, he was gone.
