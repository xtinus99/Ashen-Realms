## The Ivory Inn

The Ivory Inn is built of pale timber and bleached stone, and its common-room fire reaches only half as far as it should. Its keeper is a tired woman with kind eyes. When the company came in out of the Pallid Waste in Session 48, she asked nothing about where they had come from. Asked about the town, she gave them the shape of it: the inn, the clinic, two stalls, and a forge when the smith's home. Then she told them the trouble.

> “Two of ours are missing. They go east to the forest and come back. Regular journey. They should've been here yesterday.”<br>— the keeper of the Ivory Inn

> “A day late doesn't sound like much outside. Here, it is.”<br>— the keeper of the Ivory Inn

The company agreed to find out what had happened, and she put three iron keys on the counter, no charge. Where the two were last seen she couldn't say. They go to the forest and come back; that was all she had.

## The news

The company came back at ten at night and carried the white paw into her late common room, and the conversation stopped. They told her both of the missing were dead. Her hands settled flat on the counter. "Both of them." She said she would tell the others, and thanked them for going.

> “I've never seen anything like that. But I believe you. Thank you for bringing the truth back. Waiting would've been worse. Your rooms are still yours.”<br>— the keeper of the Ivory Inn

She gave them their rooms free a second night.

## Breakfast

In Session 49 the company had breakfast at the Ivory Inn before they left the Crossroads for Ossathar.
