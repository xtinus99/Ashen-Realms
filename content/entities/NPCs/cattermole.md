## The cat on the rail

A starving devil in the casino's dining room told Veezara about him: a fellow with a cat's head who could be seen hanging about outside, and who got people out for a price. The company found him sitting on the balustrade by the casino steps. His grin didn't move, even while he talked, and he talked mostly in riddles.

> “Four went down the throat, and four came out the front. That doesn't happen often.”<br>— Cattermole

## The job

He could put all four of them back on the road where they'd been taken, he said, together and in one go. The price was a job, which he gave them as a riddle: something that held every name in Greed and had none of its own, lay open all day and told you nothing unless you read it, was chained to the floor, weighed as much as a man, and was kept by someone who hid the whole house it sat in. Nobody got it. He gave them a map anyway, just a line from the casino out across the grey to a vault.

> “Follow the line. It goes where nobody looks.”<br>— Cattermole

## Paid in full

When they came back with the book, he was waiting on the rail. They handed it over, and he told them to count to three.

> “You didn't answer my riddle, I did say I would be disappointed.”<br>— Cattermole

On three they were under open sky again, on a bone-chalk plain east of Vor'Khalem, nowhere near the road they'd been taken from.
