A broad, loud sea captain with one eye milk-white, master of a caravel of pale gold-grained wood.

## Session 64

He was playing his plank game with Halcyra, the dragon of the reef, when the company passed, and lost: her steam knocked him clean into the sea. He shouted "Missed!" and asked if that was her breath or had she farted. When the company came out onto the reef he wanted to know who they were, and offered to guess where they had come from, the loser to buy the rum.
