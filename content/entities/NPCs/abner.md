## On the lowest platform

An old jaguar of the grove's Tabaxi, on the lowest of the platforms in the northern trees.

## Session 61

He took the company for salvagers and talked with them about the country around the grove: the fruit and the stream, the two mosses, the dock at the yard that the Tabaxi keep away from because something lives in it, the Red Wood to the south and the kneelers in it, the blind girl who burns the red trees at the border, the man who plants them again at night, and the part of the grove where the Warden lives. He let them long rest under the platforms.
