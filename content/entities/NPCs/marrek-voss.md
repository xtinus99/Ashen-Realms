## The bounty

Marrek Voss posted the den at the Brackish Pool on the Roll, at 75 gold. In Session 41 the company went out the East Gate and east along the old Sprawl-trade track through grey scrub, and reached his pastures by late afternoon. He was waiting at the fence-rail in a heavy leather coat, and let them speak first.

> “Are you the ones that took the job?”<br>— Marrek Voss

He pointed southeast and gave them the brief. There was a brackish pool in a hollow half a mile out, with the den on the north bank and six canids at last count. They hunted for an hour after dark and an hour before dawn, and slept in daylight. He'd lost two ewes the night before last and two more the week before, and sunset was two hours off.

## The count

He met the company at twilight when they came back.

> “Six? You're sure? All of them?”<br>— Marrek Voss

He closed his eyes for a second. Then he decided to ride back to the Roll with them and confirm the kills himself, since the Roll pays the same morning when the poster is there. The company took a long rest at his farmhouse first.
