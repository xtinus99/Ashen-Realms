## On the rise

A giant turned to stone, with one eye half open.

## Session 60

Farkas spoke to it in Giant. For a long time nothing happened. Then the stone groaned, its eye turned down to him, and it answered in a voice like millstones grinding, so slowly that each word came on its own:

> “Keep it. I keep it.”

Farkas concluded it was there for a purpose, to guard nature, and bade it farewell.
