## Behind the counter

A big, soft-spoken man in the face of an old half-elf, with fine cracks showing at the corners of his eyes.

## Session 59

He gave the company rooms. In the morning he had fresh bread out and offered to send for Ribhaven fish. When Viktor asked for a library he said the city has none, since people there don't write down more than they have to, and sent him west over the bridges to the reading room in the Gray Market.
