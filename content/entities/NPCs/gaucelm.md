## Session 63

His bronze blade was the one Fabrissa meant to test hers against in the square. He told Veezara who Kaedris was and where to find her. That evening Farkas found his workshop open, and Gaucelm, who knew the maul for giant work at a glance, agreed to forge a new longsword and set the Mnemon Hart's three facets in a bronze ring at its guard: Mind-Me.
