## The hidden escort

At the Liftworks, Viktor had sensed a hidden escort riding with Maldwin Crale's cargo. In Session 46 the company caught up with Crale's column on the road to Thornwall: a covered carriage and empty cradle-wagons running back from the Liftworks and Caldwharf. Two drow rode concealed inside the carriage, and Viktor's Whisper-Bound kept their two cold minds pinned the whole way.

## On the road

The company travelled with the carriage the rest of the way, on separate fires, splitting the watches. On Veezara's watch one of the twins parted the canvas and let him see her watching him. He interested her, and she made no move on it. Farkas and Viktor caught nothing of the pair.

## Thornwall

At the Thorngate, Crale's carriage was waved through on the Sallow name and the twins peeled off toward the Lean. That night they were drinking somewhere in the Lean, off duty. Viktor's sense placed them, and nobody went looking.

The next morning, crossing the Lean, the company passed the two of them road-packed and walking to the Thorngate, their job done and bound for Caldwharf. The twin who had let Veezara catch her watching held his eye a beat past polite, then walked on. The company let them go.
