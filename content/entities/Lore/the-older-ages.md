*The history of the Ashen Realms in brief, as Veezara read it in the Living Stacks. Dates count back from the present year, 2,836 AS. Anything older than the Maker's Walk is a scholar's estimate.*

## Before the Peoples

### The Shaping (about 3,000,000 to 214,000 years ago)

The Maker shaped the world the way an artist shapes clay: stone, sea and sky first, then mountains, rivers and forests, with no life in it beyond the land itself. Her first creations were the Two Dragons, Aethranox and Vaelumbra, set in the sky to carry its light. Their thirteen hours of sun and thirteen of moon have never varied, and the 26-hour day still runs on them.

### The Age of Titans (about 214,000 to 114,000 years ago)

Her first living things were not mortal. They were vast beasts, some mindless and some dimly aware: the Striders, burrowers whose tunnels later became trade roads; the Canopy Maws; the Calcite Tides, swarms that reshaped coastlines; and the Hollow Singers, which may have made the first music. Most of them died out on their own. Their bones lie in hills that farmers take for ordinary ground.

## The Mortal Ages

### The Verdant Age (about 114,000 to 64,000 years ago)

The first mortal peoples arose in many places at once, each shaped by its own land and unaware of the others. They built tribes, then settlements, then rough cities. Their languages had no common root. They worshipped the sun and the moon and fought small, brutal wars.

### The Bright Awakening (about 64,000 to 44,000 years ago)

Magic was found, not invented. It had been in the soil, the water and the air all along. Kingdoms that learned to use it pulled ahead of those that didn't, and the First Towers rose to hold it. Writing grew partly to record spellcraft. Nobody managed to work out where magic came from.

### The Shattering Wars (about 44,000 to 39,000 years ago)

The first wars to cover the whole world, each one setting off the next over thousands of years: the Resonance Campaigns, fought with sound-magic; the Calcification Crusades, which turned enemies into living stone; and the Deep War, fought in the Strider tunnels. Whole civilisations collapsed from exhaustion. Their battlefields are still there, where the ground is glass, sound doesn't carry or gravity pulls sideways.

### The Age of Discovery (about 39,000 to 29,000 years ago)

Curiosity took over from conquest. The world was mapped, sailed and dug, and the explorers found that it hummed: underground rivers too regular to be natural, caverns where the hum could be heard, warm layers in the rock, and vents that breathed in a steady rhythm. The faith of the age, Divine Naturalism, held that an unseen power sustained the world. Many of its pilgrim roads are still walked.

### The Trembling (about 29,000 to 28,300 years ago)

A natural cataclysm. Continents split, coastal civilisations drowned, volcanoes erupted in chains and the underground rivers flooded. The Severing of Idhra broke a landmass away from the mainland into the island chains that exist today. The histories also record the Red Floods and the Silent Decades.

### The Maw Age (about 28,300 to 25,300 years ago)

Creatures from deep inside the world came up and stayed: Marrow Crawlers, River Parasites, the Breathing Swarms and the Deep Drakes, which are not true dragons. Mortals walled their cities, formed the first monster-hunter orders and fought beside old enemies. A few of those orders survive in a diminished form, and so do some of the deep creatures.

### The Gilded Meridian (about 25,300 to 17,300 years ago)

The mortal golden age: the Resonant Academies, the Thousand Cities (a web of independent city-states), teleportation networks, weather control and new arts of healing. It produced the first mortals powerful enough to stop ageing. Ruins of the Thousand Cities are scattered across the world, and some of its laws are still in use.

### The Convergence (about 17,300 to 13,300 years ago)

The separate peoples finally met, across oceans and continents. They fought the Language Wars over trade tongues and over whose history was the true one, and their faiths clashed. They also intermarried, which is where half-elves and the other mixed lineages come from. A Great Census of all the peoples was begun and never finished.

### The Dimming (about 13,300 to 10,336 years ago)

A slow decline that nobody noticed at first. Magic grew harder to reach, rivers slowed, crops thinned and winters lengthened. The humming caverns fell silent one by one, grey patches spread where nothing would grow, the Academies closed and prophets rose.

## The Maker's Walk (about 10,336 to 2,836 years ago)

Then the Maker revealed herself and walked among the peoples she had made. The Dimming reversed within a generation: magic flowed again and the silent caverns thundered back to life. She healed the land and asked questions, and she did not demand worship. People who had seen her built the Basilica of the Unincarnate in this age, and her Greenwarders tended her sacred groves.

About 5,636 years ago she began to draw the wisest, the strongest and the most ambitious mortals close to her, and the years were counted from then: AW, Aedwynn's Walk. The household at Velladine Manor still gives the year as 1436 AW, which was about 4,200 years ago. Thirteen of those she drew close stood out from the rest, and she lengthened their lives. Disease was rare, war was local, magic was everywhere and the world was green.

## After the Silence (2,836 years ago to now)

### The Usurpation

Exactly 2,836 years ago, in a single night, those thirteen murdered the Maker and divided her among themselves. Each took a part of her and became a Sovereign. The Ashen Calendar counts from that night as Year 0 and calls everything since After the Silence. Within a generation her name, and the count of her Walk, had passed out of common knowledge.

### The World Changes

Her essence bled into the land. Green things turned fleshy, rivers thickened and reddened, stone took on the texture of bone and the soil softened. Each Sovereign's territory twisted toward its master's obsession, and the land between them, the Unclaimed, went grey. Within a few generations most mortals could no longer imagine a green world.

The Ten Houses founded Crownfall about 133 AS, on ground where three Sovereigns' influence cancels out. It was the first free city of the new world.

### The Domain Wars and the Long Middle

The Sovereigns fixed their borders in the Domain Wars, from about 336 to 836 AS. The centuries after brought Veinspire's expansion and its Arterial Inquisition, the Clockfall (about 1,036 AS), the Silent Centuries (about 1,136 to 1,336 AS), the Bone Ascension (about 1,236 AS) and the Dream Drowning (about 1,436 AS), and great migrations of people into the Unclaimed.

### The Defiance (about 1,831 AS to now)

Mortals came to see the Sovereigns as thieves, and thieves can be robbed. Movements rose against them: the Seven Heretics, the Resonant Theorists, the Organ Hunters, the Free Cities Alliance centred on Crownfall, the Temporal Rebels and the Aedwynn Reclamation Cells. The Vein-Cutter Uprising failed, the Liberation of Three Cities half succeeded, the Silence-Breaker Riots were crushed, and the Bone-Architect Schism is still going on.
