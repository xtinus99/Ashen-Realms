*Four stories the Dealer sold Veezara, each with a thing at the end of it.*

## The Wrong Hand

A dagger that is always in your other hand. The salvager Hugh Tarrant brought it back from the Right Hand, and he still has it at Salvager's Rest.

## Certainty

The rapier of the duellist Godfrey Hale, who walked into the Pallid Waste after the man who shamed him. Neither came out. On clear days the rapier can be seen standing upright in the white, a day's walk in.

## The Meadow Girdle

Woven from the grass of the Standing Meadow by the herder Wat Durrant, who grew to twice his size wearing it and died of it. It hangs on his grave-post at the meadow's edge.

## The Dog-Bone Whistle

Carved by Bess Varley, who ran with a hunting pack for twenty years until she lost it at a river crossing, and the pack ate her. It lies in the pack's den in the Palm of the Left Hand.
