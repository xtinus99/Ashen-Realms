The great bowl in the middle of the Palm. The river goes over the lip into clean white mist, and the mist smells only of water. Far below lie the white bones of the first giants: ribs like overturned hulls, and a man-shaped skull the size of a house on its side among the rocks. The ground at the lip doesn't tremble.

## The keeper's hall

A hall of grey stone on a spur of rock along the rim, built at giant scale. Its doorway is taller than three Farkases and the steps up to it come to his knee. Smoke goes up from its chimney, and its floor has room by the fire for a dozen people of Farkas's size. A great oak stands along the rim nearby.
