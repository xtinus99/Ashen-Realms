## Finding it

The dead plain stops at a line of grey cliffs, with grey water below and surf breaking white on the rocks. From the rocky beach a worn path climbs to a door set flush into the cliff, limewashed so white you only see its edges standing right in front of it. Lime has dried in streaks down the rock beneath it.

## Inside

One big chamber that smells sharp and chalky, like a builder's lime pit, with something sweeter and rotten under it.

- Four round stone basins of milky water sunk into the wet grey floor, and iron lanterns in the corners turned down low.
- A rope along the far wall hung with sackcloth bundles the size of people, dripping white. Their shadows on the wall stand upright.
- A ledge of cut-off soles of feet laid in rows, face up, each with a number on it.
- In the middle, a frame of lashed splints holding something twice a man's height, sewn together from grey bodies with white hair for thread. Loose hands along its seams are still pushing a needle through.

The man who lives here is a Shadar-Kai in a lime cast.
