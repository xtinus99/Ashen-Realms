## The bar

Kettleback's tavern in the deep Kennels, with rooms upstairs at four silver.

## Session 40

After the spar in Salvager's Square, Farkas, Anvil and Ratsby walked here together and sat at the bar. Ratsby ordered a small ale. Farkas ordered a barrel and tried to pay four silver with four gold, and Kettleback made the change without a word. Torvin Drennan was at the bar with a House vintage. He noted them but didn't come over.

A hooded man came in, walked straight to Ratsby's bench, sat down, pulled back his hood, and said "Hi" with a crooked grin. It was Korven Strake of House Selvik, on contract to bring the goblin in. He gave Ratsby a choice: 200 gold on the table to close the contract, or the guardhouse. Ratsby couldn't cover it and ran, and Korven caught him one-handed and carried him out.

Farkas and Anvil stayed, over the half-finished barrel.

> “Me must become stronger. Me must look for strong.”<br>— Farkas

It was the first time Farkas said his quest out loud: a trophy to take back to Thurr'gath. The two of them took rooms upstairs. Farkas slept on bruised ribs, and Anvil stood at the window all night.

In the morning Kettleback told Farkas what had happened to Ratsby, and he and Anvil went to bail him out.
