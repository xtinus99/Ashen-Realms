## East of the Crossroads

Walking east from the Pallid Crossroads in Session 48, the company watched the town fade behind them the way it had first appeared. The white didn't last this time. The ground hardened into flat bone-chalk, the horizon came back as a thin, painful line, and far to the east stood green, where the dead north should have none: single crowns showing above the mass of a forest even at that distance, trees the size of towers.

## The wreck

<img src="images/S48 Wagon Wreck.webp" alt="The wrecked ore wagon in its collapsed bowl" class="content-image">

Short of the treeline the flat ground breaks into a collapsed bowl, and in it sat the wagon of the two travellers the Crossroads had lost. One wheel was torn off, the frame twisted around a snapped axle, the canvas torn open and the crates thrown and split. There was a cold fire-pit, and no people or animals. Two broad drag-trails began beside the wagon, climbed the bowl's eastern wall and ran on to the treeline, with loose heels cutting narrow grooves inside each one.

Under the driver's bench, wrapped in oilcloth, was a small trade ledger: columns of goods and dates, then one fast final entry.

> “Iron shipment delayed. Lost a day waiting on the bars. Reached Dinfall late. Leaving at first light.”<br>— the ledger's last entry

Dinfall appears nowhere else in it: no map, no description, only the name. The company opened every crate, and every one held iron ore. The shipment was still aboard. Viktor cast Mending over the wreck, break by break, until the wagon could be driven again. Later that day Bjarnok took it, ore and all, to finish the delivery.
