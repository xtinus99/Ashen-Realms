## Soft beds in a bone city

The Marrow Rest sits in a quiet marrow chamber. It is practical, comfortable, and carefully run. The keeper keeps an exact ledger, remembers returning guests, and treats room arrangements as records rather than gossip.

## The party's rooms

The party first rented a room here in Session 49 and used the inn as its Marrowgate base. Their later arrangement put Farkas and Viktor in separate rooms, with Veezara sharing a third room with Karusa. They returned here to recover between Bonecage cards. After surrendering to Harrowmark in Session 52, they came back once more before leaving the city.

In Session 55 the keeper knew them at once as returning guests and had the dazhul carcass they were carrying put in the back-yard freight pen. They rested here when they arrived, and again after the three cards that ended that session.

On the morning of Session 56 the common room was full of ordinary business, and a lodge runner came in to collect the Reed-Cutters' kit. The company rested here again before taking the north road.
