## The bay

A sheltered half-moon of grey water inside two arms of rock, calm after the open sea, with the yard all round it.

## The yard

Long stone sheds with their slate roofs whole and their doors open, a sawpit with its trestle still across it, and a rope-walk running back up the slope for two hundred yards in a dead straight line. The paving between the buildings is worn smooth, with turf and sea-pink in the joints. Nothing has fallen down, though nobody has worked here in a very long time. Inside the sheds, under the smell of old resin and salt, the tool racks are empty, with pale outlines on the wall where the tools hung.

## The keel

On the slipway lies an unfinished hull, a keel the length of a street with its stem and first ribs standing. The blocks under it have gone grey and soft with age; the keel hasn't changed. Its wood is pale, almost cream, smooth as skin, cool and dry in the spray, and fitted without a single nail or peg.

## The dock

<img src="images/S61 Tidemother.webp" alt="A huge pale cephalopod rising out of dark water" class="content-image">

A long stone basin cut into the rock at the head of the bay, its sea gate broken, its water perfectly still. The Tabaxi of the Bloomhollow keep away from it: something lives in it. A Tidemother lay there with a tentacle barely showing on the ground, and grabbed Viktor when he came close. The company fought it until it went out to sea.
