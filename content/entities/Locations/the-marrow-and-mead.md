## The post

A trading post and tavern for merchants who risk the passage between the Unclaimed and the Rot: a long, low building of old timber and stone where a track comes out of the trees and runs off into the grey. Its yard is full of wagons that look as if they've come through something, wheels caked with spore-muck and covers patched. The sign over the door shows a bone and a cup.

## Inside

Warm, smelling of wine, and busier than anywhere for days. The house red glows faintly at the bottom of the bottle. The keeper is broad and well fed, and wears thin leather gloves though it's warm.
