## The house

A low black-timbered building three streets from the quay, with its windows covered and a lamp over the door. Inside it's dark and low-ceilinged. Everything that might shine is covered in dark linen: the mirror behind the bar, the brass on the taps, even the backs of the spoons on the tables.

## The pot

The place smells of stew, thick and salty with something sweet under it, from a pot kept warm behind the counter. In the morning there's fresh bread, and the innkeeper will send for Ribhaven fish for anyone who doesn't want food from the pot.

## Who runs it

A big, soft-spoken innkeeper in the face of an old half-elf. The company gave the Grey Pot as its lodging on the visitor bond and took rooms there.
