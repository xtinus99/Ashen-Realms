## How a card works

The Bonecage is Marrowgate's great arena: a vast public complex of fused ribs, fighter lodges, gates, warrens, and floors built to change between bouts. Every fight begins with fixed terms. Ordinary cards use a purse-only Public Draw. A Wildcard is one particular opponent from outside the normal roster; the house doesn't say what it is, but sets the payment before anyone signs. A fighter can turn down a Wildcard without penalty. Results, with the winner, the method and any injuries, are cut into a public slate before the next bout.

## The Ribward ladder

The party entered through the Ribward as Splinters and fought their way up to Knuckle, then to **Rib** in Session 56. Rib rank brings housing, trade or work sponsorship, and the right to bring challenges at the Measure Court. Sternum, the next rank, needs 15 credited wins, a 65% win rate and three wins against Rib opposition.

They defeated the Closed Rank; Viggo and Munro; Grolch; the Two Endings; Acromel and Dolich, with the thing that came out of Acromel; Iven Drask; Three on the Nail; and Cephalome.
