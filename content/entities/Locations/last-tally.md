## The north road

The bone paving runs north out of Marrowgate to Last Tally, sounded by road crews and counted at every post.

## The gates

At the south gate the road-watch checks tokens. Past the north gates the paving breaks up into rubble. Veiled pilgrims wait at a swap bench, and a chalk tally marks the March gate. At the black-stone Trace gate a warden stands with her maul resting head-down.

- **The Trace gate** opens onto the Pilgrim Trace, the road to Calvaria.
- **The March gate** opens onto the sealed road to the Northern Containment.

Last Tally's clerks and gate-watch record every departure and return.

## The hospice

Forty cots and a stove, and boots on the hospice shelf. **Loveday** keeps it, moving between the sleepers with a tin lamp. She sold O.R.V.E.K three dried bone poppy heads.

## The company at Last Tally

The company first came in Session 55 with O.R.V.E.K, who bought bone poppies from Loveday for his father's medicine. She pointed them to a cave out past the paving, where they killed seven weavers. They came back, took a long rest in the hospice, and O.R.V.E.K left for Cerebrax in the morning.

In Session 56 they came back north as Ribs, left the March gate for later, and took the Pilgrim Trace to Calvaria first. When they came back down the Trace their return went into the ledger, and they took the March gate onto the sealed road.
