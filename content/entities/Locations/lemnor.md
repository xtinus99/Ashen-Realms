Warm hill-country of hot springs, where frost on the grass at dawn is gone by mid-morning and terraced vines and orange trees stay green in winter. Steam comes out of cracks in the hillsides. The towns are low and thick-walled, built for dwarves, with their forges dug down to where the earth is hottest, steam breathing up through iron grates in the streets and the ring of hammers coming up through the paving. The street of the weapon-smiths stays open after dark, because the forges never go cold.

## The barracks

A long squat block of dark stone with slit windows on the west side of town, steam rising from a grate at each corner, with a beaten-earth drilling yard out front where the levies drill in bronze scale.

## The deep forge

<img src="images/S63 The Deep Forge.webp" alt="A cavern whose rock glows orange with heat, a slab of glowing stone with iron laid on it" class="content-image">

Down a steep, low stair from the barracks, a cavern where the rock itself glows orange with the earth's heat. There is no fire, no bellows and no smoke; iron is laid on a slab of the glowing rock and worked there.

## The Hot Spring

<img src="images/S63 The Hot Spring.webp" alt="A long low bath-house hall round a sunken pool of milky blue water" class="content-image">

The bath-house inn on the square, built over a spring: a long low stone hall round a sunken pool of milky blue water, with steam rolling out from under its eaves. The rooms at the back have warm stone floors and need no fire.

## The vent fields

Steaming hillsides above the hot springs and the terraces outside the town.
