## The streets

The Blackfold's streets are narrow and busy, and every shadow in them leans the same way, toward the middle of the city, wherever the lamps are. Every shop window that might show a reflection has dark cloth over it, and people carry their lamps so their shadows fall behind them. Stepping on someone's shadow is rude: the company saw a woman slap a little boy's hand for it, and the stranger nodded at her as if she'd done the right thing.

So many people in the city wear faces that a disguise draws no attention.

## Getting in

Foreigners register at the Shadow Quay and take a visitor bond, then cross by boat into the city.

## Districts the company has seen

- **The Gray Market,** west over two bridges of solid shadow that fold up out of the way for the ferries. Narrow arcades under layers of awnings, and every shop sign hung so the lamplight throws its shadow onto the street: you read the shadows rather than the signs. The reading room and the Last Lamp are here, and the Shadow Pits are under the lamp-oil warehouse at the end of the row.
- **The Mirror Quarter,** quieter and more careful. The shop windows are smoked, the brass plates dulled, and the public basins have lids marked *by appointment*. Only outside the court buildings do mirrors stand uncovered, tall ones in iron frames with clerks beside them. The Keeper's House is two streets back from the court.

## At the centre

The Obsidian Core, Ultharion's seat, is at the centre of the city.
