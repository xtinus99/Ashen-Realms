## The hollow

Half a mile southeast of Marrek Voss's pastures, the ground drops into a forty-foot bowl around a flat, oily pool. The den is a half-collapsed cave-mouth on the north bank, ringed with chewed bone and pale fur. The bones at the entrance weren't all sheep.

## Session 41

Marrek Voss had posted the den on the Roll for 75 gold: six canids at last count, hunting an hour after dark and an hour before dawn, and sleeping in daylight. The company went in during the daylight window, and it turned into a hard fight. The pack didn't fold the way the count had suggested, and it pressed them badly before the end. They killed all six and none got away, but Ratsby was badly hurt. The Alpha's head went back to the Roll in canvas on Farkas's back.

## What was in the den

Searching the den, Ratsby turned up the chewed remains of at least two human bodies among the sheep-bone and rotted hide. Tangled in the fur around a vertebra was a silver chain with a thumbnail emerald in worked silver, forty or fifty years old. Marek bought it at the Exchange for 190 gold.
