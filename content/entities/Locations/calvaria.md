## The city in the skull

Calvaria is built inside a skull so large that its mouth, the Jaw, holds a district of pilgrim hostels and stalls. The company reached it in Session 56 after crossing the Pilgrim Trace from Last Tally.

## The road gate

Travellers who come by road meet **Warden Hesse** under the arch. It asked each of the company for a secret. Farkas, Viktor and Karusa gave one; Veezara refused, was put outside, and is barred from the gate for good. **Poor Tam**, who was sitting outside, got him in anyway.

## The pilgrims

Most of Calvaria's people are pilgrims, drawn by what they say is the Maker's voice at the heart of the city. Every so often one of them is chosen to go in. The last two who went in hadn't come out. The company saw a pilgrim chosen, and the next day saw him walking the street in happy, unaware glee, laughing and muttering about the sun and moon, and dragons.

## The Jaw

At the Skullward post a pilgrim gives a name, has their veils counted back in, and hands over a day's water or food for the city stores. Coin is taken, but they would rather have the food. A clerk in a bone booth beside the teeth logs every arrival for Marrowgate. Pilgrims are ordinary here. Strangers only get interesting when they ask to go further in.

The company took a hostel in the Jaw and had a long rest. Before they left, Tam offered them his game with the Ashen Deck.

## Leaving

Hesse only tolls those coming in, so leaving is free. The company walked back down the Trace to Last Tally.
