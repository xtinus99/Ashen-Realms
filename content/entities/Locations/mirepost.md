## The post

You smell Mirepost before you see the roofs: a sweetness like fruit a day past eating. It's built of pale, faintly ribbed timber that gives a little under the hand, because it's fungus, and it can't rot because it already has. It's the last stop on the outside before the Rot.

## Onward

North of the post the track climbs into the forest, toward Spore Gate.
