## The cage over the harbour

Two colossal ribs curve toward each other over the harbour like the bars of a cage. Under them are deep-water berths, bone-cranes and tide-bells, and the Harbour-Warden's assessors stand on the quay.

## The company's visit

In Session 57 the company came down the coast to Ribhaven from the north crag, their first time there. Nobody there knew them, and they didn't stop. They took the Rib Road south.

## The Rib Road

The road runs from Ribhaven to Marrowgate. When the company took it there were salt wagons and fish carts in the lee of the coast ribs, salt terraces below the road with the brine setting into shapes like tiny finger joints, and wagons leaving the coast stopped and marked for the flesh they carried. At midnight every fixed bone structure along the road groaned at once, and everyone on the road stopped until it passed.
