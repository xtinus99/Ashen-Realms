## The treeline

Seen from the chalk flats, it is a green mass with single crowns standing above it, trees the size of towers. Crossing the treeline in Session 48 was like walking through a standing wall: leaves broad enough to roof houses, pale trunks like towers, ferns above shoulder height, and moss deep enough to swallow a boot.

At first the forest was deafening, with wingbeats, deep calls between the trunks, and clicking under the ferns.

## The silence

The company was following two drag-furrows from the wrecked ore wagon, with a blood trail threading the middle of them. Then the highest calls stopped, and the silence worked its way down: the canopy first, then the middle branches, then the undergrowth, until the company's own passage was the loudest thing left. Then the trail quit, tracks and blood both, between one stretch of moss and the next. They pressed on with Farkas leading, and nobody looked sixty feet up.

The **White Silence** dropped on them out of a tree without a sound. Farkas took the hardest hit he had ever taken and yelled in defiance, and the sound rolled out through the silenced wood. **Bjarnok the Weather-Eater** heard it. He had been hunting the White Silence for a long time, and between him and the company she died.

## The deep wood

Bjarnok lives in the deep wood and hunts things larger than himself. The people who bring in the ore usually send word ahead, he said, so that their crossing of the forest can be made safe, and no word had come this time. He told the company that anyone the White Silence took was dead. The two travellers' bodies were never recovered.
