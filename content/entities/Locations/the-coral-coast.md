White coral sand on a warm strait, clear turquoise over pink and gold coral, warm even in winter. Behind it lie the lizardfolk marshes, reed-channels and stilt-houses. Lizardfolk fish from the beach and draw their flat marsh-boats up on the sand. Across the strait the isles lie dark on the horizon under a haze of dusk.
