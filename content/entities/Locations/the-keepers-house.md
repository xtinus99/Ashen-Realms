## The door

A narrow, tall house on a quiet street, with a green door, smoked windows and a dulled brass plate: *Registered Convalescent Residence: Four Beds*. The step is scrubbed, and the hall smells of soap, lamp oil and something sweeter underneath, like a sickroom that's been kept very clean for a long time. The second stair is loose.

## The front room

Where the Keeper works: a scrubbed table, a basin and jug, rolled bandages, stoppered bottles with handwritten labels, and one good lamp.

## Upstairs

Four small, clean rooms off a narrow landing lit by one low lamp. Each has a smoked window, a bed with tight corners, a chair, and a jug of water under a cloth. In each bed a resident lies breathing slowly, eyes open on the ceiling, washed and combed. They don't talk, and they aren't asleep.

The lamp on the landing throws each resident's shadow onto the wall, and the shadows aren't lying down. Three of them sit beside their beds, the way someone sits with a sick friend. The fourth stands with its back to its bed, facing the door.
