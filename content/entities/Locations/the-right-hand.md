## Getting on

Over the fold of grey stone at the Pallor's still end, where the arm meets the body: a hard, steep scramble on bare rock.

## The head of the Hand

High, rough ground cracked into long ridges and gullies that all run south-east toward the sea. To the east the land ends in cliffs over a broken grey-blue sea scattered with rocky islets.

## What's wrong with it

The weather turns without warning: clear and still, then black sky and hail, then hot light again, though no cloud moves. Night can fall halfway through a morning, with no dusk and the stars in the wrong places, and lift an hour later with the shadows pointing the wrong way.

## The Palm

Where the old structures stand whole: a wall of fitted stone forty yards long that simply stops, an arch with nothing on either side, steps that climb to a flat top and go nowhere. A giant turned to stone stands on a rise and says it keeps the place.

## The walking tortoise

<img src="images/S60 Giant on the Rise.webp" alt="A giant of stone standing on a rise, calcified, one eye half open" class="content-image">

A tortoise the size of a house crosses the Palm, its shell grown over with moss and crooked little trees and pocked with openings, some hacked wider long ago. Inside is a space far larger than the shell: a tree on its side, a cartwheel, and a salvager's tent with a lantern burning on its pole. It walked away from Viktor, but answered Farkas when he used *speak with animals*: “TAKE, ONLY 1 TAKE, NO TAKE MORE.” It let each of the company take one thing, and only one, and they slept inside it while it walked all night.

## Places on the Hand

<img src="images/S60 Tortoise.webp" alt="A tortoise the size of a house, its shell grown over with moss and small trees and pocked with openings" class="content-image">

- **The Bloomhollow,** the pale grove in the north, and the Tabaxi platforms.
- **The old yard,** an abandoned shipyard on the bay below it.
- **The Red Wood,** south of the Bloomhollow.
- **The Pitfall,** the great bowl in the middle of the Palm.
- **The Ring,** the finger broken by a seam of white light.
- **The island of the Painted Apocalypse,** a short way off the north-east coast. The company saw it from the shore.

## Session 61

Another giant, half grey stone and half old weathered flesh, walked its path straight across the company's way in a glade south of the Red Wood. It didn't stop or look down.
