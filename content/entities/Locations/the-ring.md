## The seam

Halfway down the finger it is broken along a seam of hard white light that runs across it from coast to coast, hanging in the air from the grass up into the cloud and humming faintly, like a wet finger round the rim of a glass. Beyond it the far half of the finger lies turned a quarter round on its side, its forest growing out sideways. Where the seam meets the sea, the water has gone into flat grey plates. Drift that came through lies along the shore below.

## The house

<img src="images/S61 The House.webp" alt="The inside of a one-room stone house: a cold hearth, a pallet, a crooked hearthstone over a hollow, claw marks by the door" class="content-image">

One house of white stone stands on the seam, alone. Its door lies flat on the ground outside, its hinges torn out of the frame. Inside is one room with a beaten earth floor, lit white and flat by the seam through the doorway: a cold hearth with old ashes, a pallet with a few rotted threads of blanket, a hearthstone levered up and dropped back crooked over a box-shaped hollow dug into the earth, deep claw marks in the plaster beside the doorway, and seven tallies scratched into the lintel inside the door.

Veezara took a torn-off metal badge from the ashes and a silver holy symbol of the Vein-Mother's church from the hollow. Nobody knows why they were there.

## The portal

After the company slept in the house, a portal stood outside the door.
