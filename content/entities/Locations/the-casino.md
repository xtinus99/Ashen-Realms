## How the company got there

They didn't choose to come. Halfway along the road from Ossathar to Cerebrax, on the third watch, Farkas sensed something, and then everything went dark. They came round on a flat grey plain with all their kit, under a black stone ceiling instead of a sky. The only colour anywhere was this building in the distance, gold-lit, so they walked to it. A starving devil inside told Veezara they were in Greed's casino.

## The door

A bull-headed doorman stands on the steps. Weapons go in the counters to the right as you go in, and the host had a worker box the company's. Chips are gold only, one for one, bought and cashed at a cage where the cashier sits chained to her counter.

## The rooms

The lobby is black-and-white marble and gold, with a grand stair up to shut doors and curtained doorways all round it in seven colours: gold for the wheel, green for cards, red for dice, blue for the bar, orange for dining, pale blue for the machines, and violet, which is private.

<img src="images/S58 Dice Pit.webp" alt="The Dice Pit: red felt tables and a giant iron dice cage" class="content-image">

- **The Dice Pit (red):** craps tables and a giant iron dice cage. At the cage you call a number from one to six, three dice are rolled, and you're paid one to one for every die that matches.
- **The Card Room (green):** poker and blackjack under an onlookers' gallery, dealt with steel cards.
- **The dining room (orange):** where Veezara found the starving devil.

## Outside

Cattermole sits on the balustrade by the steps. The only way out of Greed the company found was his: a job, and a count of three.
