## The road there

The company first set out for Cerebrax from Marrowgate in Session 52. The fitted Ossathari road gives out onto an exposed bone ridge, and on the way they went into a tomb in the mountain and spent a night at Velladine Manor. They reached the city in Session 54, after a day's walk across the northern Unclaimed and a night at Goodknight's camp in the Outer Folds, the long pale rises of ground before the city.

Seen from the road, the city's skull-glow hangs green over the pale folds of ground, and crystal grit banks up along the black road.

## The Assessor's Gate

Everyone who comes in stands in line. The black road runs into a vaulted fold of pale tissue, hardened like waxed stone. Desks stand in two rows under hanging vellum and columns of diagrams, styluses scratch, and the stamps keep coming down. An assessor in slate-grey sent the company to the back of the queue.

Cerebrax's language is **Brancalic**, and it passes entirely mind to mind. Viktor tried talking to one of the guards telepathically, and the guard told him to stop. His invocation lets him understand Brancalic, but not say anything back in it.

When their turn came, the company paid in gold and was let in.

## Inside

Viktor and Veezara went into the city, to **the Living Stacks**, where Viktor found his mother, Cyrenne. Viktor, Veezara and Karusa took long rests in the city that night.

## Outside the walls

Farkas wanted to explore outside instead, and Karusa followed him without his knowing.

<img src="images/S54 Crystal Grazers.webp" alt="One of the crystal-shelled creatures grazing in the grit" class="content-image">

Near the approach, three dog-sized creatures were grinding their way through crystal grit. Their spiral shells had translucent facets, and as they turned, a face or a hand closing a door showed for a moment in the crystal. Further round, a pale man in a cream coat was standing at the edge of a three-hundred-foot drop, and twice jumped off it. Out on the plain, about two hundred yards off, an eighteen-foot figure of milk-white crystal hovered past with a hollow prism turning in its chest. It never turned toward Farkas.

<img src="images/S54 Crystal Figure.webp" alt="The hovering figure of milk-white crystal Farkas saw on the plain" class="content-image">

Beyond the gate lie **the Idea-Dunes**, where the company killed a Mnemon Hart. That night, right outside the city, a Choroid Harvester attacked Farkas while he was alone. He killed it, decided it wasn't safe to rest out in the open, and walked to a nearby farm.

## Leaving

In Session 55 the company left Cerebrax together on the Ossathari road and met O.R.V.E.K walking the same way.

In Session 58 they set out for Cerebrax again, north-west from Ossathar. Two days out, roughly halfway, Farkas sensed something on the third watch, and then everything went dark. They never reached the city. When they came back from Greed, they were nowhere near the road they'd been taken from.
