## The road out

In Session 40 Farkas, Anvil and Ratsby took the Bonefield Stalker bounty as honest money, cleared the East Gate by the third bell and walked east on the old Sprawl-trade track. They camped at a bone-ridge windbreak that night. On third watch Ratsby heard a kill-cry far back to the west, something large killing something else. It came once and didn't repeat.

## The field

The road ran out at midday on a horizon of bone. The eastern Bonefield is open ground broken by bone-ridges thirty to fifty yards apart, with fine bone-dust underfoot. The bones are giants' and animals', left from a battle from before the Conquest: eight-foot femurs, vertebrae the size of millstones, cleaved skulls with weapon-marks on them. The three read it as a place where giants had died fighting something. Farkas stood in front of his ancestors' bones for a moment, and then walked on.

## The den

Under a giant's partial ribcage, three ribs arching ten feet up, there was an enclosed space. Anvil read it as a den: a packed sleeping-spot, gnawed bone, an old kill-stain on the back wall, and four-toed prints in the dust, recent and repeated.

Farkas's roar drew the **Bonefield Stalker** out, and it burrowed and came at them underground. They killed it in three rounds. In a side-chamber were two cat-sized, bone-grey cubs with large dark eyes, which Anvil killed. In the burrow Ratsby found a small pile of things that weren't food, among them a rusted leather collar with a name-plate that read MILA.

They left the bodies where they lay, carried the Stalker's head and six front claws home, and reached the East Gate the next afternoon.
