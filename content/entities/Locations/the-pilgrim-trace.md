## The road north-east

The Trace leaves Last Tally by the Trace gate, where every name going out and coming back is read and written down. Within a hundred paces of the gate the paving breaks up into rubble, and after that it is skull plain, marked by cairns.

## The company's crossings

They walked it twice in Session 56, out to Calvaria and back, and no fight found them either way. On the bare stretch they came within sight and sound of something vast, grinding, and followed the cairns where they bent away from it.

Their return was entered at the Trace gate when they came back to Last Tally.
