## The vertical capital

Marrowgate rises through three colossal vertebrae fused together into a city. Streets cross the outer bone shelves and pass through cavernous marrow chambers, while lifts, process stairs, and long ascents bind the stacked districts together. Ossathari law is everywhere: in placement tokens, public ledgers, controlled trade, and the worked bone of nearly every wall. At midnight the whole city groans as its bone settles, and everyone stops until it passes.

## Places the party knows

- **The Marrow Rest:** the lodging house they use as a base.
- **The Bonecage:** Vortegas's vast public arena, where they are now Ribward Ribs.
- **The Eternal Archive:** where the archive keeper handles searches and supervised comparisons.
- **The Marrow Markets:** lawful rings of potions, graft-work, equipment, and stranger goods.

## Their time in Marrowgate

The party entered with its Ironwatch record in Session 49, joined the Bonecage as Ribward fighters, and stayed through Session 52. After losing to Harrowmark, they left Ossathar on foot for Cerebrax.

They came back in Session 55. Their Unclaimed tokens got them through the gate again, and they sold a dazhul's acid sack in the city for 800 gp before going north to Last Tally. When they returned, they won three cards at the Bonecage in one night.

In Session 56 they won their Rib promotion match against the Ossuary Concord and went north again, by way of Calvaria, to the Northern Containment. In Session 57 they came back the long way, from Ribhaven down the Rib Road, rested in the city, and beat a Reticul from the Deep Pens on a Wildcard. **The company is in Marrowgate now.**
