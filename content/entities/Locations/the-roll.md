## The Wall

The Roll is House Selvik's posting-board in the deep Kennels. Jobs go up on the Wall by tier. A taker signs at the counter and gets a Selvik token for the job, and when it's done, the senior Wall-Clerk checks the proof, files it, and pays out. A small token for every closed job goes up on the Notice Beam.

Some postings are above a crew's standing by the Wall's rules, and the clerk won't open them. The Black Tier corner is clerk-mediated and invitation-only, and has to be earned by working the Wall. Velvet Thorn pointed Anvil at it when he asked her about weird cases.

## The company's contracts

**Session 40.** The Bonefield Stalker bounty paid 90 gold, and a hooked-claw token went up on the Beam. The clerk briefed two higher postings: a Selvik vault recovery at Twin Reach, three days west, for 300 gold and 30% of the salvage, and a 900-gold survey of the Bastion approach, in Kaedris's land, which three crews had taken before and two hadn't come back from. The company wasn't allowed the survey. They signed the den at the Brackish Pool (75 gold) and Twin Reach.

**Session 41.** The Brackish Pool closed for 75 gold, with Marrek Voss there to confirm the kills, and a four-toed-paw token went up.

**Session 42.** Back from Twin Reach, the company was slow to turn in, and a huge cloaked figure pushed past them to claim a Red. Ratsby started to complain, looked up into the hood, and lost his words. The clerk knew the figure and stamped his claim through fast. Then the company signed an Orange: "Our caravan didn't come home." It was a Selvik-chartered run, six hands and two wagons, gone up the north road toward the Vor'Khalem fringe and more than five days overdue. Selvik wanted the manifest, the cause and any survivors, in that order, for 300 gold. Twin Reach was settled separately, between Selvik and House Vander.

**Session 43.** The company turned the caravan contract in at dusk: 300 gold, and another 150 or so in strongbox salvage.
