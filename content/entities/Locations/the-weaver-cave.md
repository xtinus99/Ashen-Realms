## The way in

Loveday pointed the company here when Viktor asked where to find bone poppies. Past the paving at Last Tally there is only white dust and skull rubble. The cave opens in a low rise of chalk and bone, its mouth strung with threads as hard as ivory. O.R.V.E.K led them through the widest gap and held a strand aside with one gauntlet. It bent, but it didn't break.

## Inside

The chamber runs a long way back into the rise. Bridges of bone-silk cross it at two heights, standing wall panels split up the back of the floor, and cocoons hang from the roof. One fresh bundle hung low by the left wall, with a pair of boots showing underneath and a chalk number on one sole. At the back, pale poppies grow out of picked bones.

## Session 55

Something clicked overhead, and more clicking answered it from around the cave. Seven weavers came for the company: two Blessed, one Ancient and four Skeletal. All seven died.

The fresh cocoon held a dead pilgrim. The company took a Ring of Resistance against psychic damage off the body and picked four bone poppies from the back of the cave. There was nobody in there left to save.
