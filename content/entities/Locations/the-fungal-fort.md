## The fort

A wall of living thorn, head-high and woven tight, with a gap for a gate. Behind it a huge hollow fruit-body serves as the keep, with smokeless glow-light in its doorways and hammocks slung high under its ribbed ceiling. Fungus towers rise out of the forest around it like chimneys.

## Its garrison

Only a few lizardfolk stand on the gate, with room on the wall for many more: the fort has been short of warriors since the levy. The ground tells the captain who is coming before they arrive.

## The Ways

Under the keep a root-stair goes down into the dark. News sent down there travels on: when the captain went below, a sound like a lot of people whispering at once came up through the floor.

## East of the fort

Only the lake.
