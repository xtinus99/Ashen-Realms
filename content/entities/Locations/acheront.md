The old city of the Shadar-Kai, free under its queen, in its own twilight: the light never goes dark, it goes grey and stays. The city rises in tiers over a web of canals, with grey stone bridges and long low black boats on the water. Every lamp is turned to exactly half-light, and a lamp-warden walks the quay adjusting each one with a long brass hook. Every doorstep has a threshold-stone worn hollow in the middle.

The Shadar-Kai here are written on: lines of fine raised scars on their arms, throats and faces that say who they are. Children carry a few marks, and the old so many that there is barely skin between them. The first marks, cut on a newborn's wrist, say three things: a given name, a mother's line and an island of birth.

## The Long Canal

<img src="images/S64 The Long Canal.webp" alt="A long dark canal between grey houses, low black boats and lamps at half-light" class="content-image">

A long canal between grey houses where the glass-polishers of the Glass Steps live. No house in Acheront has a door; a visitor stands in the empty frame until someone looks.

## The Queen's Hall

<img src="images/S64 The Queen's Hall.webp" alt="A long grey hall lit at half-light, its walls cut with lines of script" class="content-image">

At the top of the city: a long grey hall with doorframes and no doors, its walls cut from end to end with the scar-tongue, lamps at half-light, and a channel worn down the middle of the floor by the feet of everyone who has walked to the queen. The walls carry the queens of Acheront, oldest by the frames and newest by the stone seat. The oldest stretch is cut in two columns, from when Acheront had a king and a queen side by side.
