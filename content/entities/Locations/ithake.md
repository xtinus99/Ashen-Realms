The kingdom on the Little, the smallest finger of the Hand. A road of pale stone, worn into grooves by carts, runs down the finger, and it is never empty of petitioners walking to the king.

The town is walled, pale stone and tiled roofs climbing the finger in terraces to the white palace at the tip. A long hall of pillars hung with green banners leads to the throne room, which has no back wall: three tall arches open straight onto the sky and the sea. Petitioners wait at the palace gate in a line, in their turn, and anyone in the kingdom may be heard.

There is an inn two terraces below the palace, a low white house with a vine over the door.
