## Salvager's Square

A market square in the Kennels, busy with trade in the late afternoon, with a dry fountain in the middle and Veshik's Curiosities on one side. The company's first three met here in Session 40. Ratsby ran out of Veshik's shop with a stolen ring and climbed Farkas to hide, while Farkas was busy squaring up to Anvil. A House Selvik factor stood near the fountain with two guards, and children chased each other around the rim. Most of the people who heard the shop's bell decided it wasn't their problem. Captain Jawbone of the Watch took Ratsby and Anvil away.

When Farkas finally got his fight with Anvil, a young Watch deputy turned up, six months on the job. He cited the ordinance against public combat in a market square and offered them the legal version: an arm-wrestle was fine, since the Code said nothing about contests of strength. Weapon-bouts had to go outside the wall. Ratsby took him aside, and for a quiet cut the deputy agreed to watch from the corner and step in only if anyone drew real blood. The pot came to 87 gold, most of it the Selvik factor's money on Farkas. Anvil won, and the deputy called it.

> “The warforged takes it.”<br>— the young Watch deputy

Ratsby had already gone down the south alley with the pot. The deputy, paid and keen to keep his report short, filed Farkas's and Anvil's statements as true, put a Watch flag out on Ratsby, paid everyone back their stake, and hurried off to the guardhouse.

## The guardhouse

The Kennels Watch guardhouse is a two-storey House Varn building a short walk south of the square, smelling of ink and lamp-oil. Someone paced the upper floor reading the day's log and didn't come down. A clerk takes names at the desk. Ratsby was booked here twice in Session 40: once by Jawbone, for the ring, when he paid Veshik's fifteen-gold Section 9 fee rather than do three days at the Coin Street workhouse, and once by the night-deputy after Korven Strake brought him in, when his kit went into the personal-items locker and he went into cell three. In the morning Anvil talked the day-deputy's bail down from 100 gold to 80 and paid it.

## The Common Cup and the Roll

Deep in the Kennels are **the Common Cup**, Kettleback's tavern, and **the Roll**, House Selvik's posting-board, where the company took its first contracts. The Green Door, the Dealer's magic shop, is in the Kennels too.

## The Kennels yard

In Session 43 Farkas took his Watch token to the Kennels yard for a supervised bout, and drew Kira "Blackhand" Vost of House Varn, who was in the city on an inspection sweep. She beat him.

## The East Gate

The Kennels sit by the city's East Gate. The Valdris guards there wave Roll-token crews through without searching their weapons. Beyond it, the old Sprawl-trade track runs east through grey scrub to Marrek Voss's pastures and the Brackish Pool, and on to the eastern Bonefield.
