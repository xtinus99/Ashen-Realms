## The arch

North of Mirepost the trees grow watchtower-tall and furred with moss, and the sky becomes a green ceiling a long way up. It rains, soft and steady. Spore Gate is an arch of pale, ribbed, petrified fungus taller than a gatehouse, standing over the track on its own. At its foot lie drifts of discarded outside masks, cloth and leather, some already growing something small and white.

## Past the gate

The air thickens with spores, a fine gold haze that settles on sleeves and eyelashes and smells of flowers and rot at once. Masks matter from here on.

## The greeting-grove

<img src="images/S60 Greeting Priest.webp" alt="A lizardfolk priest in a cloak of moss and grass, with lanterns hung from a crooked staff" class="content-image">

Just beyond the arch the track opens into a ring of benches all grown from a single root. A lizardfolk Cycle Priest and two attendants keep it. They hand out masks and ask travellers for their fire. The company took the masks; they had no fire to give.
