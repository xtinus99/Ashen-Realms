A fishing village across the narrowest part of the Ring. Two dozen low houses of white stone with slate roofs stand between its two shores, with boats drawn up on each, a well at the crossing, a drying-green for nets and a goat-fold on the slope. You can walk all of it in less than an hour.

There is no seam here and no Break: the finger runs on south, straight and green, and the sea off both coasts is ordinary water. The house that stands alone on the seam in the company's own time is a lived-in cottage.
