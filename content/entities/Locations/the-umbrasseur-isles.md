## The shade

At the Isles' edge the light stops. It doesn't fade. Behind you the sky is lit as usual, and ahead is a soft grey dusk that stays the same however long you walk in it. Morning there only shows in the bells.

## The way in

The long arm of land runs south from the coast toward the Isles, with an inlet on one side and scrub on the other that grows warmer toward the Bastion. At the end of it, black basalt pylons stand at the water's edge, and from them a bridge of solid shadow runs out over slate-coloured water to the first islands of the city. Guards at the pylons send foreigners across in single file, not side by side, to register at the Shadow Quay.

When the company came, a crew was working on the span.

## Law and history

In the Isles a shadow has rights in law, and the Isles' Shadar-Kai were the islands' own people before Ultharion took them. What the company read about both is in Shadow Law and the Isles' History.
