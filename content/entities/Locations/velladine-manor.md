## Off the road

The company found the manor in Session 53, walking from the tomb in the mountain toward Cerebrax. Veezara's spyglass picked out warm windows beyond a bone ridge, and they turned off the road to look.

A narrow lane climbs a sheltered rise to a three-storey house. Pale walls and open iron gates enclose black trees, clipped hedges and a carriage circle. When they arrived a carriage stood under the portico with no animal in its traces. For a house in empty country, it was remarkably well kept.

The doors opened before anyone knocked, and Mr. Hobb was waiting in the amber hall.

## The waltz

From the lane onward the company could hear a formal waltz, the sort of tune a bedside music box plays, carrying clearly over the whole estate. It never got louder as they came closer, and it was just as clear behind a shut door.

<img src="images/S53 Music Box.webp" alt="The music box in the ballroom: a black box with two small silver dancers inside its open lid" class="content-image">

It comes from a music box hanging above one of the pedestals in the middle of the ballroom: a black box with two small silver dancers in its open lid. It played the exact same tune the whole time they were in the house. A butler told them it was a gift from the lord's wife, to be handled with care, and that nobody was allowed to touch it. The company switched it off while nobody was looking. A butler came rushing over, switched it straight back on, and asked them to please be more careful.

Viktor took another look at it during the reception. It is magical, and not in just one way: he could make out several kinds of magic coming off it, mostly transmutation and illusion.

## The house

The hall is lit by silver lamps and hung with family portraits, and the painted eyes move together with the music. The guest rooms were already made up when the company arrived, with washed basins, turned beds, writing desks and evening clothes folded and waiting.

In the drawing room a dozen well-dressed guests sat between the fire, a hunting portrait and two long sofas. Near the far wall a pair gave the same short laugh twice, at the same interval, with the same tilt of the head, and nobody remarked on it. Viktor tested the guests with an ability. They were not alive.

Out front, two carriage handlers said the horses were over in the stables. Farkas went and looked. There wasn't a single horse.

## Below the cellar

Hobb showed Viktor the wine collection. Behind one barrel a section of wall caught his eye, and the company sent Karusa back later to look quietly. One stone is loose, with a button behind it. Pressing it opens a concealed door onto stairs going further down, below the cellar.

Karusa went down alone and came back unnoticed. At the bottom was one coffin, empty and looking seldom used, with a family photograph beside it. Nobody opened the coffin or looked closely at the photograph.

## The reception

The company arrived after six, when the evening reception had already started. His Lordship would not receive new guests halfway through an introduction, Hobb explained, so their reception would be the next evening. At six Hobb escorted them into the ballroom and announced them. Musicians play behind a screen, their bows half a beat behind the music box. About twenty minutes in, Aurelian Velladine came down with his wife and children and greeted his new guests. He let the company stay the night.

## When the music stopped

When the company woke in Session 54 the music box was off, and the house had changed. The rooms were cold and musty. The comfortable beds had become sagging frames and the folded finery brittle grey scraps. Dust lay over the furniture, and where the polish had been there was split plaster and bare stone.

Aurelian's room was empty. The company sensed him down in his coffin and left him undisturbed. They took nothing, damaged nothing, and walked out of the silent manor toward Cerebrax.
