## The fissure

Where the arm of land narrows, a deep, dry fissure cuts across it from the inlet almost to the eastern shore. Now and then a stretch of its wall twitches like a muscle.

## The bridge

One old stone bridge crosses it. At its north end lies a heap of picked grey bones and empty feed-sacks, some of them years old. Gulm sits in the middle, exactly as wide as the bridge, and nobody crosses until he has eaten.

## The long way

Farkas went round by the coast instead, and fought rift crawlers, the coast's crabs, on the way.
