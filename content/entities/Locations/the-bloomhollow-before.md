The great grove of the Greenwarders, in the north of the Palm. Its trees are pale-barked and enormous, trunks wider than houses, with walkways of lashed wood running between them high up and platforms round the trunks. A clear stream runs through ferns below.

## The southern end

The outer grove runs south to where the Red Wood stands in the company's own time. Here there is no red: pale trees in green leaf, no eyes on any of them, and the pond lying clear with green leaves floating on it. The great hollow tree is only a tree, with a dry hollow full of fallen leaves.

Grove-folk, winged and a hand tall, watch from the branches and are gone when looked at.
