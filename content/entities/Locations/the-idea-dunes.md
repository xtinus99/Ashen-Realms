## The near dunes

White-grey ridges banded with green glass, outside Cerebrax. A black road runs through the drifts, with gold inlays pointing back to the gate.

In Session 54 Karusa stopped hiding from Farkas and walked beside him through the near dunes. Viktor and Veezara, coming out of the Living Stacks, caught up with them on the road.

## The white hart

Farkas spotted a hart in the distance and told the others. It was a towering white deer with six legs and split hooves, with three cloudy facets among its branching antlers, one smoky red, one bruised violet and one cold blue. It was grazing on a pale ridge across a shallow basin, lifting its crown between mouthfuls.

The company fought the **Mnemon Hart** and killed it.
