A lone steading on the road across the giants' high plains, where the grass goes up to the sky and the wind never stops. One long hall of stone slabs, big enough to stable a ship, with a hearth down the middle big enough to roast an aurochs; a stone fold for a herd of black aurochs the size of houses; and a single slab of stone outside for a table, where the giants eat in the wind. The benches come up to a man's chin.

Three days west, the forges of Phlegre glow on the edge of the sky at night.
