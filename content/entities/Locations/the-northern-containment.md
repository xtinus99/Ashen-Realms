## The sealed road

North of Last Tally, the March gate opens onto a sealed road closed to ordinary traffic. Names go into a book at the gate and the soldiers keep a chalk head-count on the gate post. Outbound cages on the road are scrubbed clean. Teams coming back carry hooked poles and burning lime, and every shelter along the way is built facing away from the north.

## The Ash Yard

The road ends at the Ash Yard: empty transport cages along the sides, tents for the soldiers, and the dome in the middle.

## Inside

Two guards keep the way in, and the commander decides who goes inside. When the company asked in Session 56, he scoffed and let them in: it would be their funeral. A group of guards had gone into the hole a few weeks before and never come back.

Inside the dome, all manner of abominable creatures walked around, and none of them paid the company any attention. In the middle was a hole, with creatures climbing out of it and going back down. One look was enough, and the company left.

## Leaving

In Session 57 a Bonecage carter at the Ash Yard recognised them from the arena and told them he had seen a party of four heading for the mountains to the west, on some contract. They did not rest at the Ash Yard. They went round the dome and down to the coast after them.
