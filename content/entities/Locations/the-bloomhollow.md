## The grove

Pale trunks that go straight up, smooth as skin, wider than the oaks and with no low branches, under a canopy so high the light comes down in long slanting shafts. The moss is ankle-deep and springy, and it's warmer here than out on the cliffs. The rain falls straight down and stays ordinary rain, though the weather on the rest of the Hand turns without warning. The stream is clear enough to count the pebbles, and the animals here don't run.

## What grows here

Abner's word: the yellow-green fruit by the stream is good to eat and the stream good to drink. Of the two grey-green mosses, the soft one closes cuts, and the dark one on the north sides of the trunks puts you to sleep.

## The Tabaxi platforms

<img src="images/S61 Tabaxi Platforms.webp" alt="Tabaxi platforms and rope-walks high among pale trunks" class="content-image">

Fifty feet up in the northern trees, platforms of lashed wood ring the trunks, joined by walkways of plank and rope, with knotted ropes hanging to the moss. The grove's Tabaxi live and work up there. At dusk, and on the nights a man walks the border planting red trees, they pull the ropes up. Abner, their old Rope-Father, let the company rest beneath them.

## Deeper in

Further south-west along the stream the trees grow older and larger. Deeper in is a part where the Warden lives, which doesn't let everyone in. The trees there let only Veezara and Karusa through, and spread their tops to stop anyone flying over.

## The border

South of the grove is the Red Wood. The Tabaxi won't go south of the stream at the border. A blind girl burns the red trees at the border every day with a green sword, and they leave food for her.
