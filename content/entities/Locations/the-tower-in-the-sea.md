A rough round tower standing alone in a flat grey-green sea that runs to every horizon, with no land anywhere. Its top is half-built, about a hundred feet across, with a low ring wall broken by three wide gaps and stacks of building stone. A few dozen tower-folk live on it, in coats of fish-skin. At the north edge stands a feeding arm with a net basket that swings out over the water.

When it was done, the stone turned soft and grey, the sea went flat as slate, and the world came apart.
