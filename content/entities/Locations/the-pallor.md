## The water

Pale, still water, a crust of white mineral underfoot, and the wreckage of failed crossings along the bank, with the Right Hand on the far side.

## The shallows

Something flat and eight feet across lies buried in the silt a few yards out, with two small eyes on stalks above it. Karusa saw it and kept the company off it.

## The still end

<img src="images/S60 Eyes in the Shallows.webp" alt="Two eyes on stalks above the silt at the edge of something broad and flat buried in the shallows" class="content-image">

North along the shore the land on the west rises and closes in, until the shore ends in a steep fold of grey stone. There the dead water pools deepest and stillest, and whatever its currents carried north lies washed up along the crust. Over the fold is the Right Hand.
