## The guest

In Session 42 a huge cloaked figure, a guest of House Thane, pushed past the company at the Roll, and the Wall-Clerk went pale at the sight of him. Anvil went after him and got no answer. He reckoned a guest of House Thane would be lodged in the Threshold.

## The rite

When the Husk King killed Ratsby out on the Vor'Khalem fringe in Session 43, the company carried his body to the Threshold. Maren Thane read the corpse before they had finished explaining it, named the thing that made it from the body alone, and judged the death a wrong one. Her price was 500 gold and the full account of the Husk King.

She worked the rite through the night at Sister Varen's shrine, in the old tongue. Ratsby drew breath again at dawn, alive on the shrine floor with the night's cold still in him.

## The figure in the corner

As the rite closed, the company noticed the shrine's other resident: a hooded figure keeping to the corner, the same cloaked man who had pushed past them at the Roll. Anvil asked him again who he was and got no answer at all. Maren offered only that he was a guest of the Threshold.

> “He keeps his own counsel. As do I.”<br>— Maren Thane
