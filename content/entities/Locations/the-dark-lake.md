## The approach

East of the fort the path runs downhill out of the fungus forest. The trees thin and yellow, and the ground turns soft and dark red-brown, giving like wet leather. A bitter smell comes up, sharp at the back of the throat even through a mask.

## The water

A great dark lake, red-brown and bruise-purple, its currents moving in long even sheets, all the same way. A yellow crust runs along its shore, dry and cracking on top and sticky underneath, and nothing grows at its edge. Up close it makes a low, constant, sliding hush.

Nothing drinks from it. The game trails come down to the water and turn along the shore, and none go in. On the far shore a stream runs out of the lake clear, and elk drink from the stream, well back from the lake.

## The camp

On the near shore, set back from the crust: a resin-sealed tarp on stilts, a hammock, a ring of stones that never held a fire, and two waxed boxes, one of fresh-cut mushroom caps each tagged in bone with the name of a dead person, the other a courier's kit. Nobody was there.
