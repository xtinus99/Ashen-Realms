King Hrothgar's city, on a long rise above the giants' high plains. Its forges run along the foot of the rise and never go out; at night the whole side glows red, and the glow can be seen from days away. Small folk are guests here under the king's law.

## The Long-Watch forge

A giants' forge with a crane and chains beside a banked hearth, where Eadwulf Ember-Hand, forge-father of the Long-Watch line, works.

## The training hall

A hall with a sand floor where the young giants drill in pairs at first light, wrestling and throwing each other.
