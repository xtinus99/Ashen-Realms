The warm lowland north of the strait is farm country: wheat and barley in strips miles long, vines on the south slopes, windmills on the low hills, poplars along the roads.

Where its old roads cross there is only open stubble, a single poplar at a field corner and a crow on a fencepost. Nothing is built on it.

## The guardian

<img src="images/S64 The Guardian of the Crossroads.webp" alt="A tall cloaked guardian of bronze and bone with a staff at the centre of a crossroads" class="content-image">

At the exact centre of the crossing stands a route guardian of bright bronze and white bone, its grey cloak whole and the script on its staff fresh-cut. It holds the crossing itself and nothing beyond it: *"The route is closed to the unwitnessed. Present yourselves or turn back."* The fields round it are open.
