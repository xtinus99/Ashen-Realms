A plain trampled to bare earth where the lowland runs out against the great eastern forest, wide enough to drill an army on. Giants about thirty-five feet tall fight here in the open, inside a ring of other giants. Carts and wagons draw up along the road to watch, with a beer-wagon, a smith's cart, a bookmaker chalking odds at a cart's tail, and farm boys up the poplars. A bout runs until one giant yields or goes down; slapping the earth twice means you yield. An old giantess sets bones on a bench at the field's edge.
