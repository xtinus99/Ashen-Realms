A town of tall plastered houses at dusk, reached down a stair of seamless grey and a short grey hallway. Every door is barred from the outside with planks bolted across or iron bars in brackets, and the wood is scored with long scratches. There's a bowl on every step, empty, and soft apples in the gutters. Some windows are black with flies; others show empty rooms and holes gnawed through the floorboards. Something scratches under the street, in the walls and the drains.

One house, the biggest, has no bar on its door. Its cellar is brick vaults with holes all along the bottoms of the walls, a channel of brown water, and barrels of apples.

When it was done, the town went grey and came apart, and the stair was gone.
