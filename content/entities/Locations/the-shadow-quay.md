## The harbour

The shadow bridge comes down onto a harbour that reflects no lamps: long black piers on slate water, and every moored ship throwing its shadow in a different direction. Porters work without talking under cranes whose counterweights start rising before the loads come down.

## The first desk

Foreigners are sent to the first desk at the foot of the piers, where a clerk writes each arrival into a ledger while his shadow writes along with him on the wall behind.

## The visitor bond

The clerk explains it before you agree to anything: a paper token, and a mark that goes on your shadow, with your lodging declared. With the bond taken, a boat carries you across into the city.
