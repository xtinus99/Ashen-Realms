## Getting there

It lies west along the coast from the Northern Containment, past bone beaches with no road and no markers.

## The crag

Outside the cave was a camp, very recently used.

## The den

Inside is a sea-cave as tall as a chapel, with a great arch to the sea on its far side and a pillar of rock in the middle. The tide comes in across the floor between the pools. In one wall is a crack, smeared white and covered in small handprints going both ways.

This was where **Isgarrow**, the dragon, lay, until the company and the Second Burial killed it in Session 57. It blew apart into fragments.
