## The rim

The oak forest ends at a line, as if cut, and beyond it is a bowl so wide its far side is blue with distance. A river slides over the lip in one long white fall into mist, and the ground at the lip trembles now and then and goes still. Evening comes early over it.

## The terraces

The forest goes down the bowl's sides in four great terraces, each older than the one above, the trees on the lowest steps wider than houses.

## The bones

At the bottom, through the mist: great curved ribs, a skull on its side, and a hand with its fingers curled, each finger as long as a ship. Farkas knew them for Giants of the first era.

## A night at the rim

The company short rested in a hollow between the roots of an old oak. Nothing came. In the morning there was frost on one side of the camp only, its edge as sharp as a drawn line. They found no way down.
