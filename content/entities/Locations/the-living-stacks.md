## Getting in

In Session 54 Viktor got into the Stacks by showing the marks of his patron, the Thousand-Celled, and Veezara got in by donating three of his scales. Their entry was good for 26 hours. Going back in after that would take another allowed entry, because the marks and the scales only work once.

## Inside

Under green light, cataloguers work at crystal tables below high shelves. A stooped woman in black handed a folio back to an attendant, corrected something, and went back to her work.

At another table a woman with ash-silver hair was checking a crystal record against a written passage. Viktor recognised her as **Cyrenne**, his mother. She looked up as an attendant passed, and her eyes stopped on him, stylus held over the unfinished line. Viktor tried to convince her he was her son. She refused to accept it, and stayed at her work.

## What they learned

Veezara learned the history of the Ashen Realms there and came away with a summary of the older ages. They also learned that one sign of the Mother of Openings' arrival is that things start growing holes.

Then he and Viktor left the Stacks and went to find the others.
