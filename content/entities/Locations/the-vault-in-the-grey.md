## Getting there

Cattermole's map was just a line, from the casino out across the grey to a vault. The grey is ash-coloured dust, pale bare trees and grey waterfalls in the distance, and in places the black rock ceiling comes lower. The line ended at a sinkhole with little iron grave markers in rows along its rim. A narrow ledge runs down the inside wall to a round iron door, and the company opened it themselves.

## Behind the round door

A long hall full of curios, every one of them broken, as if nobody had looked after any of it in decades, and no gold. Grey, see-through clerks who fade into mist below the knee work there. One asked the company for their number, took "sixty-seven", noticed they had no tags in their ears, and told them to get that seen to. At the far end a corridor runs between floor-to-ceiling shelves of jarred eyes, and every eye turns to follow you.

## Three doors

Three round iron doors in a row, each with a brass face that wants to play a game, and each with a price for losing:

- **Higher or lower:** three right calls in a row opens it. A wrong call costs a tooth.
- **The shell game:** find the pea. A wrong guess costs a year of your life.
- **Liar's dice:** the loser's voice is taken for the day.

The company paid a tooth, a year of Veezara's life, and Farkas's voice, which he got back from the shelves.

## The shelves

Aisles of shelving run off into the dark, packed with tagged jars of eyes, hourglasses, horns tied with string, stoppered bottles, sealed envelopes and numbered drawers, with the grey clerks pushing ladders along them.

## At the bottom

<img src="images/S58 Hall.webp" alt="A round iron chamber with a giant clockwork safe door and a chained book on a lectern" class="content-image">

The only thing the company could see at the bottom was a book, chained down, with a light shining on it. When Veezara went up to it the light turned red, and the chained giant came for them. They killed it and two smaller chained figures, cut the book free, and carried it out for Cattermole, along with four things off the shelves.
