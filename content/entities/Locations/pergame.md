The largest city in the world, on a great slow river in the river plains, the heartland. The country round it is the most crowded there is, farms and villages close together, each with a well and a shrine with her name cut over it. Four great stone bridges cross the river. The city has markets, a thousand red-tiled roofs, and its houses of healing along the waterfront, and the wind off it smells of woodsmoke, bread, river water and something sharp, like herbs boiled in vinegar. It is ruled by a council of eight.

## The church

<img src="images/S62 The Church.webp" alt="An old ivy-covered church of grey stone on a terrace above a river city, veiled stone figures and candles in niches" class="content-image">

Pergame has one church, on a stone terrace at the top of the ridge above the river: grey stone with ivy up its walls and no tower. Along its outer wall stand stone figures of a veiled woman in niches, with candles, flowers and bread left at their feet. Inside is one long hall on plain round pillars, with no benches and no altar, only a plain stone step and, cut into the wall behind it, a veiled woman walking. The prayer is *She see me. She keep me. She walk by.*

## The square before the fountain

<img src="images/S62 The Square.webp" alt="A rain-wet market square in front of a running stone fountain, with a timber guillotine on a raised platform" class="content-image">

A market square in front of a great stone fountain, where the city's executions are done.
