A long leaf-shaped blade of bronze drawn in Lemnor's deep forge, with a faint dark ripple frozen in the metal. At the guard sits a ring of bronze with three settings, and in each a cloudy crystal from the crown of the Mnemon Hart the company killed in the Idea-Dunes: smoky red, bruised violet and cold blue. Something moves inside each one. In the red, a blow lands and lands again. In the violet, a flash of something cast. In the blue, a figure crossing a room in a stride too long to be real.

A lit facet glows. A spent one goes dark and cloudy for a moment, like a stone that has forgotten something and is trying to remember it.

The name is Lemnor's: in the dwarves' speech, to *mind* a thing is to remember it.

## Properties

- **Damage:** 1d8 slashing (versatile 1d10). **+1 to attack and damage rolls** (fire-forged).
- **Three facets, one per round.** A facet you use goes dark until the end of your next turn, so you can't use the same facet two turns running; the other two stay ready. Anyone who looks can see which ones are lit.
- **Blow (smoky red).** When you hit a creature with this sword, the hit deals an extra **3d10** slashing damage.
- **Word (bruised violet).** *Reaction.* When you are targeted by a spell attack, or are the only target of a spell, the facet catches it. You are unaffected, and the spell flies at a **random creature within 60 feet of you other than you**: friend, foe or the caster, whoever the roll lands on. It uses the caster's spell attack bonus and spell save DC. A spell with an area of effect can't be caught.
- **Passage (cold blue).** *Bonus action.* You move up to your speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

## Session 63

In Lemnor, Farkas gave Gaucelm the three facets and asked for them to be set in a new longsword. Gaucelm is forging it himself. Because the facets come from the company's own world, the finished sword will come home through the portal.

## Session 64

The company came back to Lemnor for it. Gaucelm had it finished in a long wooden case on grey felt; Farkas paid him 800 gp and took it.
