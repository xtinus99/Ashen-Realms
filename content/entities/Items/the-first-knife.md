A short blade of grey stone, sharpened so thin the edge is nearly clear, set in a handle of white bone worn smooth by many hands. The first scar-speaker of Acheront cut the first name with it. It has passed from queen to queen ever since, and none of them has used it.

## Properties

**Damage:** 1d4 piercing (finesse, light, thrown 20/60). **+3 to attack and damage rolls.**

**Name-Cut (1/dawn).** When you hit a creature with an attack using this weapon, you can cut its true name into the wound. The target must succeed on a **DC 19 Charisma saving throw** or be **name-cut** for 1 minute. While name-cut, the target:

- can't become invisible, and any invisibility on it ends;
- can't change its shape or form. If it's in any form other than its true one, it returns to its true form. This includes forms from the *polymorph* spell, the Shapechanger trait, Wild Shape, and a face or skin taken from another creature. If a spell caused the form, the spell ends;
- loses any illusion or magical disguise on it, which ends;
- can't teleport or leave its current plane by any means;
- can't hide from you, and you always know its location while you're on the same plane.

Once you use this property, you can't use it again until the next dawn.

**Reader of Skin.** While attuned to the knife, you can read any writing, mark, tattoo, brand or scar on a creature's skin, whatever its language or script.

## Session 64

Queen Elspeth gave it to Karusa from her own collection, for clearing the hallway in Demelza's wall.
