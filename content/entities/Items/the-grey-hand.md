A glove of the Hoarder's own skin, smooth, grey and always cold.

## Properties

**Lift.** As an action, touch something written: a page, a scroll, a contract, an inscription, a glyph, a brand or tattoo on a willing creature. The writing comes off it and onto the glove's palm. The glove holds one writing at a time.

**Set down.** As an action, touch a surface and the writing goes onto it, exactly as it was. A spell written into the writing (a *glyph of warding*, a scroll) still works when it's set down.

**Unwrite.** Once per long rest, when you hit a creature with a melee attack while wearing the glove, it makes a DC 17 Wisdom save. On a failure it loses one prepared spell, chosen at random, until it finishes a long rest.

## Session 64

It dropped from the Hoarder when Farkas and Anvil killed it at the bottom of the hallway in Demelza's wall.
