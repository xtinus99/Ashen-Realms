# Session 49 — Splinters on the Sand

## Session Overview

- **Party Level:** 7
- **Location:** The Pallid Crossroads → the Pallid Waste → Ironwatch Post → the Femur Road → Marrowgate
- **Party Members:** Farkas, Veezara, Viktor — plus Karusa
- **PC Deaths:** None
- **Theme:** Out of the Waste and into Ossathar, a city that writes down everyone who arrives. Within a day the four of them were on its arena slate as well.
- **Date:** ~late Gorravane, 2,836 AS
- **XP:** Farkas, Veezara and Karusa +1,475 each; Viktor +275

---

## Session Log

### Breakfast Before the White

At the Ivory Inn, Veezara ordered two breakfasts of eggs, toast and soup. Farkas asked for eggs and bread in whatever quantity the kitchen could manage. Viktor joined them, and over the table Veezara asked about his books. This time Viktor showed him his separate incantation book. It was in Deep Speech, which Veezara could read about as well as he had read the Abyssal one, so he handed it back.

The four chose Ossathar and left together. Behind them were the clinic, the forge, the two market stalls and the Crossroads' broken wall. Ahead was white ground under an ivory sky. Farkas led, Veezara and Viktor came after him, and Karusa took the rear.

The town came apart behind them a piece at a time. First the gaps went out of its wall, then the smoke lost its chimneys, and then the buildings were gone. Their footsteps started arriving several paces behind them, as if they came from somewhere back past Karusa. Nothing was there, and after a while the sound caught up again.

### A Shape Beyond the Camp

They stopped after the day's march, with nothing but white around the camp. On the first watch Veezara could barely tell the ground from the distance. On the second, Farkas spotted something low and chalk-pale moving across the far flank.

He woke Karusa. She followed it with her eyes and made out a single creature, crossing rather than circling, too far off to name.

> “It hasn't turned toward us. I can't name it from here.”<br>— Karusa

Farkas handed her the watch. Karusa had finished her trance during the earlier watches, so she kept both of the remaining shifts herself. The creature blurred back into the Waste and never came back. They sat out the rest of the dark and moved on when Aethranox's light arrived.

### Names in the Ledger

On the next march, sound caught up with their feet and the colour stayed put. The soft white hardened into calcium plates, and wheel grooves gathered into a road that led to a bone-walled fort. Ironwatch Post had a heavy gate, a watchtower and one warning bell.

<img src="images/Ironwatch Post.webp" alt="Ironwatch Post on the Ossathari approach" class="content-image">

The post's commander met them at the toll-house with her ledger already open. Bone plate climbed one side of her jaw and ran across her hands.

> “Names, one at a time. Number travelling. Cargo. Intended destination. Anything you're carrying that came alive out of the forest.”<br>— the Ironwatch commander

They gave four names, declared no cargo and said Marrowgate. Nobody made any trouble over the salted paw. The commander took the answers, the quartermaster took 4 gp, and the gate opened. The first thing Ossathar wrote down about them was an arrival entry.

### The Femur Road

At Joint's End the wheel grooves turned into a highway six freight wagons wide, running along a colossal ossified ridge.

<img src="images/The Femur Road.webp" alt="The broad bone highway of the Femur Road" class="content-image">

The surface switched back and forth between bare plate and old marrow channels. Quarry loads, food wagons and military freight each kept to their assigned lanes and got counted as they passed the brass-white distance pins.

At one course, bells stopped the civilian traffic for a funeral convoy. A covered body went past under its household marks while its name and destination were read out. A drover ate beside his waiting team. Then the convoy cleared and the road got moving again.

They passed the Eightieth Course, the Midpoint, Condyle's Edge and Joint's Beginning. By night, Marrowgate was in front of them.

### Soft Beds in a Bone City

<img src="images/Marrowgate - City Overview.webp" alt="Marrowgate rising through three immense vertebrae" class="content-image">

The capital was three vertebrae the size of mountains. A freight gate let them into the lowest one, where polished calcium streets climbed under a ceiling high enough to hold weather. Lifts ran on braided sinew cables. Skeletal and half-skeletal citizens were out and about in the first dark-hours, and farther up, a crowd was roaring somewhere in the city's outer ribs.

The Ironwatch ledger had already done its job, so there was no second interview and no second toll. Each of them got a chalk-white Unclaimed place-token. They went to the Marrow Rest, which had imported wooden doors, padded chairs and wool mattresses. In a city made of bone, all that softness looked expensive.

<img src="images/S49 Marrow Rest.webp" alt="The common room of the Marrow Rest" class="content-image">

> “Beds are soft, broth's grey, both are honest. Card-talk stays in the common room.”<br>— the keeper of the Marrow Rest

They took a private room at 4 sp a head. Downstairs, Farkas ate roasted ossen and drank milk. Then all four followed the evening spectators uphill.

### The Bonecage

<img src="images/The Bonecage.webp" alt="The Bonecage and its tiers of spectators" class="content-image">

The Bonecage stuck out of the middle vertebra through an enormous opening. It was a fused ribcage holding tier after tier of spectators above a floor of bone-sand. Fracture-red marked the Ribward side, and charcoal and old ivory marked the Marrowmen. The Warden's throne-box was empty.

At reception they asked whether Vortegas fought. A clerk told them his last bout had been sixty years ago, against the Pallbearer. The challenger lost, promised to come back, and jumped out of the arena. They tried to place the title from history and got nowhere.

They went in as spectators. A fight was already going on down on the sand, and they watched it from the public ribs for a while before heading to the Ribward lodge.

### The Ribward Lodge

<img src="images/S49 Ribward Matchmaker.webp" alt="Ferrin Skelde of the Ribward lodge" class="content-image">

Ferrin Skelde sized them up at the lodge. They asked to enter as a team, and he looked to Karusa to hear her agree for herself.

> “Mine too.”<br>— Karusa

He registered all four as Ribward Splinters. Before giving them a No Rules card, he offered them a Wildcard. Ferrin never says what's behind a Wildcard; that's the point of it. They said no. Nobody else signed up, so the Bitter Standard took it. It was Acromel, and it wiped them out. Acromel was hauled away in its cage, still eating one of the team's female orcs.

The company's first opponents would be the Closed Rank.

### The Closed Rank

<img src="images/The Closed Rank.webp" alt="The Closed Rank, the company's first Bonecage opponents" class="content-image">

Raska, Vraan, Soska and Orruk were a mercenary formation, the survivors who had stayed together. The floor was the Marrow Sluice, its channels broken up by a central island and four dry outer islands. The company could win by holding the ground or by beating the other side, and surrender was legal.

The company won. Viktor's Eldritch Blast killed Soska, and the other three lived.

That was the first credited win for all four of them. The purse was 75 gp and came to 60 gp after the casualty deduction. They collected it and went back to the Rest.

### The Grinding

At midnight Marrowgate sounded through every fixed piece of bone it had. Walls, cistern rims, lift housings and vertebral courses all answered in one long grinding note. The inn went quiet until it passed. The company finished its long rest.

When Farkas, Veezara and Karusa went back to the arena, Viktor was still asleep in their room.

### The Brothers on Open Sand

The three took the card against Viggo and Munro, who had thirteen paired wins and four losses between them. One was a shield-bearing defender and the other a risk-taker with a spear and a weighted net. They were at their best fighting close together and using each other's openings.

<img src="images/S49 Viggo.webp" alt="Viggo of the Marrowmen" class="content-image">

<img src="images/S49 Munro.webp" alt="Munro of the Marrowmen" class="content-image">

Farkas, Veezara and Karusa won the card and 100 gp, which put their records at 2–0. Viktor's stayed at 1–0. They went back to the Marrow Rest to check on him. The session ended there, without a second long rest.
