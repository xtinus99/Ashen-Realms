# Session 52 — Cell by Cell

## Session Overview

- **Party Level:** 9 at opening; all four 10 at close
- **Location:** The Marrow Markets → the Bonecage → the Marrow Rest → the Cerebrax road → a tomb in the mountain
- **Party Members:** Farkas, Veezara, Viktor — plus Karusa
- **PC Deaths:** None
- **Theme:** A surrender on the arena sand, then something much worse inside a mountain. Viktor called for help, got it, and paid for it permanently.
- **Date:** ~late Gorravane, 2,836 AS
- **XP:** +6,820 each

---

## Session Log

### Midnight at the Bonecage

The company picked up where it had left off, in a shop in the Marrow Markets just after Viktor bought his bracers. After a long rest, all four went back to the Bonecage at midnight.

The Grinding passed as they reached the arena. Lifts stopped and conversations paused while the shudder ran through the fused ribs, and then the noise of the concourse came back.

They went to the Ribward lodge and asked for Harrowmark.

### Harrowmark

<img src="images/Eldran Vey.webp" alt="Eldran Vey of Harrowmark" class="content-image">

Ferrin named the pair: Eldran Vey and Brugg of the Marrowmen. He didn't sugar it. Two Rib fighters at once was a lot more dangerous than a first try against a single one. It was a No Rules card for 1,600 gp, open sand, surrender legal, and a win wouldn't promote anyone by itself.

They took it. Ferrin gave them the posted tell: Brugg's winds came in three phases, and standing beside one of Eldran's three waymarks sheltered you from two of them. Break a waymark and that shelter was gone, along with one of the places Eldran could step to.

<img src="images/Brugg.webp" alt="Brugg, the second fighter in Harrowmark" class="content-image">

The Public Draw added nothing. Harrowmark beat all four of them, and the company surrendered. Harrowmark took the money and the company kept its gear. Farkas, Veezara and Karusa went to 8–1, Viktor to 7–1. So much for the undefeated run.

### Back to the Rest

They went back to the Marrow Rest, paid for their rooms and slept it off. The keeper already knew how the card had gone. Nothing in Marrowgate travels faster than a Bonecage result.

Karusa went out to the market and came back with a +1 dagger and a Cloak of Protection.

Then they set out on foot for Cerebrax. The fitted Ossathari road gave out onto an exposed bone ridge, and they camped beside it.

### Scratching in the Mountain

At camp they heard scratching from the white mountain wall to the north. They went to look, and found it wasn't coming from the wall. It was coming from inside the mountain.

They broke the rock open. On the other side a skeleton was looking straight at them, still scratching. It came out at them. Viktor tried talking to it and got attacked instead. While they were putting it down, two more shambled out after it, and that was the last of those.

So they went in.

<img src="images/The Legion Tomb - Cavern Interior.webp" alt="The interior of the tomb inside the mountain" class="content-image">

Inside was an army of the dead: skeletons, mummies and skeletal knights, sixty-two of them. The mummies' paralysis battered Veezara, Viktor and Karusa, and the three of them barely made it back out. Farkas didn't. He ended up trapped in a corner with the whole onslaught coming at him and nowhere to go.

### Viktor's Last Resort

Outside, the other three got their breath back and made a plan to go in after Farkas. They knew they'd lose. So Viktor tried one last thing and reached out to his patron, and something answered.

<img src="images/Thousand-Celled Intercessor.webp" alt="The Thousand-Celled Intercessor, sent in answer to Viktor" class="content-image">

The Thousand-Celled Intercessor did what Viktor told it. It went into the tomb and destroyed every skeleton, mummy and knight in the place, and it got Farkas out of a fate that would have been grim. Nothing undead was left.

Then it pointed at Viktor, and two words arrived in his head.

> “Cell by cell.”<br>— the Thousand-Celled Intercessor

It came apart exactly that way, until there was nothing left of it. Viktor felt a deep ache, and then the bill came due: a permanent loss of 4 Dexterity. Only *wish* can give it back.

### The Reliquary

Deeper in, the company found the tomb's reliquary and took everything in it: weapons, armour, healing potions, a horn, a pile of old campaign coin, two diamonds and a bundle of old military records. They carried the lot outside and sat down for a short rest.

Cerebrax was still where they were headed.
