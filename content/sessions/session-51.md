# Session 51 — Legs for Sale

## Session Overview

- **Party Level:** Farkas, Veezara and Viktor 7; Karusa 8 at opening. All four 9 at close.
- **Location:** The Marrow Rest → the Marrow Markets → the Bonecage → the Eternal Archive → the Markets
- **Party Members:** Farkas, Veezara, Viktor — plus Karusa
- **PC Deaths:** None
- **Theme:** A stranger at the inn with his own opinion about the sun, three more arena wins, and two trophies made into something to sell.
- **Date:** ~late Gorravane, 2,836 AS
- **XP:** +5,125 each

---

## Session Log

### The Man at the Table

<img src="images/S51 Elf at the Bar.webp" alt="The unnamed golden-haired elf at his table in the Marrow Rest" class="content-image">

At the Marrow Rest, Viktor looked around for someone interesting and found a golden-haired elf sitting alone. He went over, asked who he was and introduced himself. The elf took one look at a half-elf and that was that.

> “Fuck off, half-breed.”<br>— the golden-haired elf

Viktor, visibly offended, tried Devour Memory on him without components. It slid right off him. The elf just stared at him, and it was obvious he knew Viktor had tried something, components or not.

Veezara came over to apologise for Viktor. That went about as well.

> “Fuck off, lizard. You are not even fit to kiss my feet.”<br>— the golden-haired elf

When Veezara brought up Aethranox, the elf called his faith hogwash and named Sael’oroun as the sun god. He had a glowing sun weapon on him. Veezara didn't pursue the name or the weapon. He barked at the elf, spat down and walked away.

The elf answered with *power word stun*. Veezara collapsed and couldn't get up for a minute, though there was no injury and nothing lasting. So the stranger had dismissed Veezara's god, put him on the floor with one word, and still hadn't given his name.

### A Small Return

The company went to the Marrow Markets. Veezara bought a Potion of Healing for 50 gp and returned the gauntlets he had bought earlier, for the full 150 gp. Between the two deals he came out with a potion and a hundred gold more than he went in with.

Then it was back to the Bonecage.

### Iven's Last Card

<img src="images/Iven Drask.webp" alt="Iven Drask, Hollowheel" class="content-image">

Iven Drask, Hollowheel, faced all four of them on open sand. Surrender was allowed and the purse was 400 gp. The company beat him, and Iven died in the bout. His record ended at 12–8.

The victors took both of his Hollowheel legs, fused combustion chambers and all. The legs were this card's spoils. Farkas, Veezara and Karusa had their sixth win, Viktor his fifth.

### Three on the Nail

<img src="images/Three on the Nail.webp" alt="Elen Leth, Bel Torq and Oren Vosq, Three on the Nail" class="content-image">

Without resting, they took on Elen Leth, Bel Torq and Oren Vosq on Quarry Step. Ferrin warned them how the Nail-Riggers worked. Oren marked one target and the three of them concentrated on it, so breaking their sight lines or splitting their attention could break the sequence. Oren carried Nailcaster, a compact gun of bone and brass.

The Public Draw came up Heavy Purse, which raised the prize to 320 gp. The company won again. This time all three opponents survived, and none of their registered equipment changed hands.

With those two cards on the record, Farkas, Veezara and Karusa stood at 7–0 and Viktor at 6–0.

### Books and Bones

Viktor went to the Eternal Archive for a quiet place to sit and read. Veezara went too, because Veezara is always suspicious of whatever Viktor is up to.

Farkas and Karusa took the legs to the Markets and paid 1,000 gp for a commission that would combine Iven's two legs with the White Silence's preserved paw into White-Hollow Stride. So the paw they had carried out of the forest and the legs they had won in the arena ended up as materials for the same piece.

The plan was to sell the finished graft, along with Viktor's Boots of the Unmoored. Nobody intended to have it installed.

### One More Wildcard

<img src="images/Cephalome.webp" alt="Cephalome, the company's final Wildcard opponent" class="content-image">

They regrouped and took a long rest before going back to the Bonecage. The next offer was a Wildcard on open sand with a fixed 400 gp purse. Ferrin didn't say what was behind it, and they signed anyway. What came out was Cephalome.

The company beat it and killed it, collected the purse, and got another line on the slate. Farkas, Veezara and Karusa were now 8–0, which meant they could ask for Rib promotion matches. Viktor, at 7–0, still needed one more win. All four were still Knuckles.

### The Sale

<img src="images/The Marrow Markets.webp" alt="The Marrow Markets, where the graft and boots were sold" class="content-image">

White-Hollow Stride was ready after the card. The company collected it from the boneshaper and took it, along with Viktor's boots, to a shop nearby. Viktor tried to talk the proprietor up to 4,500 gp. He rolled a natural 1, the offer came down to 3,500 gp for both, and the company took it.

The legs, the paw and the old boots were all gone now. At the same counter, Viktor bought Bracers of Defence for 1,750 gp. That was where Session 51 ended, with the company together and all four of them at Level 9.
