# Session 57 — Dragon's Second Funeral
<!-- cover: images/S57 Drey.webp -->

## Session Overview

- **Party Level:** 11, reaching 12 during the session
- **Location:** the Ash Yard → west along the north coast → the cave on the north crag → Ribhaven → the Rib Road → Marrowgate and the Bonecage
- **Party Members:** Farkas, Veezara, Viktor — plus Karusa
- **PC Deaths:** Veezara, briefly; he was brought straight back
- **Theme:** A carter's sighting leads to a dragon that keeps getting up, a share of its hoard, and a long way home.
- **Date:** ~late Gorravane, 2,836 AS
- **XP:** +3,750 each (the dragon kill counted as an eight-way split with the Second Burial)

## Session Log

### The Ash Yard

The company stood in the Ash Yard: empty transport cages along the sides, tents for the soldiers, and the dome in the middle. Nobody was going to get a long rest here.

### The Carter

<img src="images/S57 Carter.webp" alt="The Bonecage carter at the Ash Yard" class="content-image">

A carter saw them and came over. He was lean and wind-chapped, with dark curly hair and the Bonecage's ivory badge sewn onto a carter's leather coat.

> “Oh, you're from the Bonecage. I've seen you fight.”<br>“Are you from Crownfall? I saw a party of four walking towards the mountains, west of here.”<br>— the carter

He didn't know why they had gone west. Something about a contract.

### West Along the Coast

The company went after them, round the west side of the dome, down to the shore and west along the bone beaches with the sea on their right. There was no road and nothing to mark the way.

In the second half of the walk, a Bonestorm came off the Wastes toward the sea: a white wall of bone-dust, hissing, with voices in it. They had a few minutes' warning. There was a rib-arch on the beach ahead and a sea-cave in the rocks behind. They kept going.

The storm caught them in the open, and everyone held. The dust scoured them, though less than it might have, and the voices in it stayed voices.

### Ambush on the Road

Further on they stopped to rest. During Viktor's watch, three ossivores ambushed them.

<img src="images/S57 Ossivore.webp" alt="An ossivore, a scavenger built from other creatures' bones" class="content-image">

The company killed all three, made camp, and went on.

### The Cave

When they reached the cave on the crag, they found camping supplies outside it. Somebody had been there very recently.

### The Den

Inside was a sea-cave as tall as a chapel, open to the sea on its far side through a great arch. The tide was coming in across the wet floor between the pools. A pillar of rock stood in the middle, and around it, up and fighting, was the dragon.

It was a skeleton the size of a building, wearing the dead who had come to collect it like armour. Brass seals sat in its eye sockets. White dust sifted from its breastbone, its left wing and its brow. In the wall off to one side was a crack, smeared white and covered in small handprints going both ways.

Four people were fighting it. A broad man held the front behind a coffin he was using as a shield. A woman fought it with a shear that split into two usable blades. A second woman used a harpoon to land devastating blows from afar, then pulled it back to dislocate the dragon's limbs. Behind them, a man with tanks on his back held a long steel spike.

<img src="images/S57 Daughter of Augustin.webp" alt="The Daughter of Augustin and her shears" class="content-image">

The company went in.

### It Kept Getting Up

The dragon went down and got up again three times, and each time it came back with less of itself: first without one wing, then without the other, then without its tail. The fourth time it fell, it stayed down and burst in a wave of necrotic force thirty feet across.

The burst killed Veezara.

> “Get him up, it is not yet his time.”<br>— the man with the coffin

The man with the tanks cast a spell over him, and Veezara came back.

The dragon had blown apart. The four from Crownfall stood watching the crack, waiting for it to come back. It didn't.

> “Huh. It's not getting up.”<br>— the man with the coffin

From what was left, the four secured an orb of necrotic energy.

### The Hoard

The man with the coffin thanked them. They had a share coming, he said: some of the dragon's hoard, lying between the rocks, and a quarter of the job's pay, about 3,000 gp.

The company took four things from the hoard:

- a Ring of Free Action;
- a Belt of Dwarvenkind;
- a Scimitar of Speed;
- a Scarab of Protection.

The scimitar went to Karusa.

### Names

The company gave their names. He said each name back once, then gave theirs.

> “Right. I'm Drey. Ysolde. Sigrun. That's Lazare with the tanks.”<br>“You eaten? There's stew up top. It's not much, but it's hot.”<br>— Drey

<img src="images/S57 Drey.webp" alt="Drey, with the coffin he fights behind" class="content-image">

### Stew on the Ledge

Up on the ledge, Drey got the fire going and served the stew. Lazare cleaned their scrapes with salve.

> “Hold still. Nearly done.”<br>— Lazare

<img src="images/S57 Lazare.webp" alt="Lazare and his tanks" class="content-image">

> “It kept getting back up. This time it didn't.” … “I'd like to know why before I tell the Hall it's done.”<br>— Drey

The company took a short rest on the ledge.

### Goodbyes

<img src="images/S57 Sigrun.webp" alt="Sigrun, the harpooner" class="content-image">

When the four were ready to go, Drey asked if the company wanted to come along.

> “We're off back to Crownfall. You're welcome to come if you're heading that way.”<br>— Drey

The company said no and wished them well.

> “Keep those scrapes clean.”<br>— Lazare

Sigrun nodded and said nothing. The four left.

### West to the Docks

The company kept going west along the coast. The crags and the edge of the Wastes dropped behind them, the shore opened out among the great rib formations of the Rib Coast, and then Ribhaven's two ribs came up out of the sea.

<img src="images/S57 Ribhaven.webp" alt="Ribhaven, its two great ribs arching over the harbour" class="content-image">

The two ribs curved toward each other over the harbour like the bars of a cage. Under them were deep-water berths, bone-cranes and tide-bells, with the Harbour-Warden's assessors on the quay. Nobody there knew the company, and they didn't stop.

### The Rib Road

From the docks they took the road south to Marrowgate: salt wagons and fish carts in the lee of the coast ribs, salt terraces below the road with the brine setting into shapes like tiny finger joints, and wagons leaving the coast stopped and marked for the flesh they carried. At midnight every fixed bone structure along the road groaned at once, and everyone on the road stood still until it passed.

### Marrowgate

They showed their tokens at the gate as before and took a long rest in the city.

All four reached Level 12, and Karusa took up the scimitar from the hoard as her main weapon.

### The Ribward Lodge

<img src="images/The Bonecage.webp" alt="The Bonecage" class="content-image">

At the Bonecage, the company's Rib promotion match win over the Ossuary Concord was cut into the public slate with the other results. In the Ribward lodge the Card Office was open. Practice blows came through the wall from the training ground, and one voice was counting on the other side.

Ferrin was turning a young fighter away when they came in. The fighter's forearm was in a splint.

> “Structure assessment says that arm's cracked. Come back when it's knitted.”<br>— Ferrin, to the fighter

> “Ribs.” … “What are you after? A card?”<br>— Ferrin, to the company

### The Wildcard

They asked for a Wildcard. Ferrin never says what's behind one, and he didn't this time. The terms were an open bone-sand floor with no Public Draw. They signed.

The card ran in the first dark-hours. The arena's official confirmed the surrender words. Then the Deep Pen gate opened, and the Wildcard came up the freight ramp onto the sand: a Reticul. It started ploughing the floor.

<img src="images/S57 Reticul.webp" alt="The Reticul from the Deep Pens" class="content-image">

They beat it. The Reticul went still on the sand with its scoop down. The official ended the card. Deep Pen handlers came out with hooks, chains and lime to clear the floor, and the result was cut into the slate before the next bout. The purse was 1,000 gp.

The company went back to the inn and took a long rest. That was where the session ended.
