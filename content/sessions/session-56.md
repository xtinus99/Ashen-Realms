# Session 56 — Laughter in the Skull

## Session Overview

- **Party Level:** 11
- **Location:** Marrowgate → Last Tally → Calvaria → Last Tally → the Northern Containment
- **Party Members:** Farkas, Veezara, Viktor — plus Karusa
- **PC Deaths:** None
- **Theme:** The company make Rib, find a city of pilgrims waiting to be chosen, and get one look inside the northern dome.
- **Date:** ~late Gorravane, 2,836 AS
- **XP:** +6,500 each

---

## Session Log

### The Ossuary Concord

At the Marrow Rest the clean-crews had finished sounding the walls, and the company left for the Bonecage to ask for a Rib promotion match.

Ferrin finished an argument about a replacement part before he got to them. All four had the record for it. He offered four solo opponents, and turned down another Rib pair, citing the Harrowmark surrender.

The company asked for the Ossuary Concord instead: Mera Avrelis, Gautier Brisemont and Vesh, the headline team of their own Ribward house, with a 52–7 record.

<img src="images/S56 Ossuary Concord.webp" alt="Mera Avrelis, Gautier Brisemont and Vesh of the Ossuary Concord" class="content-image">

Ferrin explained what tied the three together. Each wore an ivory Concordance Seal at the sternum. They could sense where their conscious partners were and what shape they were in, as long as they were nearby, and could take part of the harm meant for one another. Their strength changed as members went down. The replacement seals were still partway through a five-bout test before the trio could make another attempt at Sternum.

It was a No Rules card with a 1,200 gp purse. The company signed, and won. Mera and Vesh were killed. Gautier surrendered, and the official ended the bout on his word.

### Four New Ribs

The Ossuary Concord closed at 52–8 with its lineup destroyed. Gautier, the only survivor, kept his own rank. The bodies went to the Bone Press, and the company took Mera's Lattice Rod and Vesh's Hooked Gauntlet.

All four party members became Ribward Ribs. Farkas, Veezara and Karusa went to 12–1, Viktor to 11–1, and the purse was 1,200 gp. Their first credited win against Rib opposition had also won them the rank.

They went back to the Marrow Rest, took a long rest and headed up the north road. At Last Tally they left the March gate for later and took the Pilgrim Trace to Calvaria first.

### The City in the Skull

<img src="images/S56 Calvaria.webp" alt="Calvaria, the city within the great skull" class="content-image">

Past the Trace gate the paving gave out into rubble. They crossed the skull-country without meeting anything and reached Calvaria.

At the road entrance, Warden Hesse asked each of them for a secret.

<img src="images/S56 Gate Warden.webp" alt="Warden Hesse holding the barrier at Calvaria's road gate" class="content-image">

Farkas, Viktor and Karusa each gave one, nothing that mattered, and went through. Veezara refused. Hesse took hold of him and put him back outside the arch.

### Poor Tam, Outside the Arch

<img src="images/S56 Poor Tam.webp" alt="Poor Tam, the fool encountered at Calvaria's gate" class="content-image">

Outside, Veezara saw Poor Tam and asked if he could help him get into the city. Tam told him a joke. Veezara couldn't resist it and laughed helplessly for a full minute.

Then Tam talked Hesse round, and Veezara was let in.

### The Pilgrims

Most of the people inside were pilgrims. They had come for what lies at the heart of Calvaria: the Maker's voice, they said.

The company found one of them praying, like all the rest. Every so often, he told them, someone is chosen to go in. The last two who went in haven't come out.

Later that day, a pilgrim was chosen.

The company found lodging in the Jaw and took a long rest.

### The Chosen Pilgrim

The next day they saw him again: the pilgrim who had been chosen, walking the street with a look of happy, unaware glee. They confronted him. All he did was laugh, muttering about the sun and moon, and dragons.

None of it made any sense to them, and they decided to leave Calvaria.

### The Card That Went Blank

Tam offered them a game: make him laugh, and draw one card from the Ashen Deck. Farkas, Veezara and Viktor all failed. Karusa managed it.

She drew the Open Grave. It could bring back one dead mortal, however long dead and whatever had happened to the body, as long as the soul was free and willing to return.

Karusa said a name too quietly for anyone to catch. Nothing appeared beside her. The card's face faded blank, and the draw was spent.

> “I knew it wouldn't work... of course you would be unwilling...”<br>— Karusa

### Back to the March Gate

They walked back down the Trace, and nothing attacked them on the way.

At Last Tally their return went into the ledger. Then they took the March gate onto the sealed road to the Northern Containment. No writ errand got in the way this time.

The cages going out were scrubbed clean. The teams coming in carried hooked poles and burning lime. Every shelter faced away from the north.

### The Dome

At the Ash Yard the company saw the massive dome. They asked the two guards on duty whether they could go in. Not without the commander's say-so, the guards told them.

<img src="images/S56 Northern Containment.webp" alt="The Northern Containment: rings of bone ribs around a dead village, under guard" class="content-image">

The commander was in a tent, doing paperwork. When they asked, he scoffed. They could go in, he said. It would be their funeral. A group of guards had gone into the hole a few weeks ago and never come back, so they could do what they liked with that.

They went in. All manner of abominable creatures walked around inside the dome, and none of them paid the company any attention. In the middle was a hole, with creatures climbing out of it and going back down.

One look at those things was enough. The company decided to leave, and the session ended in the Ash Yard.
