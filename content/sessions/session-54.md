# Session 54 — Mothers and Strangers
<!-- cover: images/S54 Goodknight.webp -->

## Session Overview

- **Party Level:** 10
- **Location:** Velladine Manor → the northern Unclaimed → the Outer Folds → Cerebrax and its outskirts
- **Party Members:** Farkas, Veezara, Viktor — plus Karusa
- **PC Deaths:** None
- **Theme:** The manor's music stops. Two private conversations end in refusals, and the company splits up at Cerebrax's gate.
- **Date:** ~late Gorravane, 2,836 AS
- **XP:** +3,340 to the party; +7,100 to Farkas

## Session Log

### The Family Dance

<img src="images/S53 Reception.webp" alt="Aurelian's reception before the music stopped" class="content-image">

Aurelian turned from his guests and offered Miravel his hand. She took it on the next measure. Tavian and Neris stood at the side of the dance floor and watched their parents begin.

Behind the musicians' screen the tune went on, with the players' bows half a beat behind its notes. Above the floor, the little silver couple on the music box kept turning.

> “You are welcome to join us. Hobb will show you back to your rooms whenever you wish.”<br>— Aurelian

Once they'd heard everything they wanted from Aurelian, the company went up to rest. Farkas shared Viktor's room; Veezara and Karusa took the one next door. The waltz followed them upstairs and didn't get any quieter.

### The Bundle — Veezara's Room

During the rest, Karusa's breath caught. A hard shudder went through her shoulders and arms. Her eyes were closed, and she held a cloth bundle tight against her breastbone. The wrapping slipped far enough to show a small, decayed finger.

Veezara saw it happen and persuaded her to show him what she was holding. Karusa opened her palm and eased the cloth back.

> “It belonged to my child.”<br>— Karusa

He tried to learn more. She folded the cloth back over it and closed her hand.

> “No. That is all I'm telling you.”<br>— Karusa

Veezara accepted that and didn't press her. She kept the finger. Farkas and Viktor were in the other room and saw none of it. All four completed their long rest.

### Without the Music

When they woke, the music box was off. The rooms were cold and musty. The comfortable beds had become sagging frames and the folded finery brittle grey scraps. Dust lay over the furniture, and where the polish had been there was split plaster and bare stone.

The company searched Aurelian's room and found it empty. They sensed him down in his coffin and left him undisturbed. They took nothing, damaged nothing, and walked out of the silent manor toward Cerebrax.

### Something on the Skyline

Several hours into the walk, a many-limbed figure rose over a ridge about a mile to the north. Its central mass hung hundreds of feet off the ground. Long, unequal limbs folded sideways and backwards before coming down into the pale earth. Dust went up wherever one landed, and the grinding sound reached them a little after.

<img src="images/S54 Skyline Walker.webp" alt="The many-limbed figure that crossed the skyline" class="content-image">

It kept to its own line.

> “It hasn't turned towards us.”<br>— Karusa

The company walked on and left it behind.

### A Spare Seat

Toward evening, out in the long pale rises of the Outer Folds, the company saw a carriage up ahead come to a halt, and the people with it started making camp for the night. Escorting it was a broad man in rounded, banded armour, one shoulder plate missing, with a crooked golden sun on his faded tabard. With him were a lean, bald older man and the travellers from the carriage.

The company went over. The armoured man lifted his visor on a weathered face and a greying moustache, introduced himself as Goodknight, and told them he was a knight.

<img src="images/S54 Goodknight.webp" alt="Goodknight, the armoured escort who shared his stew" class="content-image">

“A very sound proposal,” he said when the company sat down to eat with him, and he unstrapped a spare folding stool from the mule. He filled their bowls with hot stew before his own. His companion handed the bowls round, counted them, and went back to the carriage.

<img src="images/S54 Humphrey.webp" alt="Humphrey, Goodknight's companion" class="content-image">

> “That's Humphrey. Keeps my armour together.”<br>— Goodknight

After a while he asked whether the company had come far.

They slept at the camp. At first light Goodknight stood a little apart with his visor up and his arms spread toward the warmth, then went back to stirring the pot. After breakfast the company travelled on with the carriage to Cerebrax.

### The Assessor's Gate

At Cerebrax they were told to stand in line. The black road ran into a vaulted fold of pale tissue, hardened like waxed stone. Desks stood in two rows under hanging vellum and columns of diagrams. Styluses scratched, and the stamps kept coming down.

<img src="images/S54 Assessors Gate.webp" alt="The queue and assessment desks at Cerebrax's gate" class="content-image">

An assessor in slate-grey, with three styluses pinned in her hair, stamped a traveller's report and pointed the company to the back of the queue.

<img src="images/S54 Gate Assessor.webp" alt="The gate assessor at her desk, hands resting on a closed ledger" class="content-image">

> “End of the line, please. One traveller at a time.”<br>— the gate assessor

At the gate, Viktor tried talking to one of the guards telepathically. The guard wasn't having it and told him to stop. Brancalic, the city's language, passes entirely mind to mind, and Viktor couldn't speak it: his invocation let him understand Brancalic, but not say anything back in it.

When their turn came, the company paid in gold and was let in. Viktor and Veezara went into the city. Farkas wanted to explore outside instead, and Karusa followed him without his knowing.

### The Man at the Edge — Farkas Outside

Near the approach, three dog-sized creatures were grinding their way through crystal grit. Their spiral shells had translucent facets, and as they turned, a face or a hand closing a door showed for a moment in the crystal. Farkas went past and left them to it.

<img src="images/S54 Crystal Grazers.webp" alt="One of the crystal-shelled creatures grazing in the grit" class="content-image">

Further round, a pale man in a cream coat stood at the edge of a three-hundred-foot drop with a notebook under one arm. When Farkas came near, the man brightened, excited and cheerful, and asked if Farkas would like to join him.

Farkas told him it was pointless. So the man jumped.

He fell all the way to the shelf below. About fifteen minutes later he came climbing back up over the edge, saw Farkas, and asked: since Farkas didn't want to join him, maybe his friend would?

“What friend?” Farkas asked. Then he spotted Karusa behind some stones. Seeing she'd been spotted, she came over, and the man put the same question to her. She gave him a firm no.

So he jumped again. Farkas sighed and left him to his experiment.

Walking on, Farkas saw an eighteen-foot figure of milk-white crystal hovering across the plain about two hundred yards off, a hollow prism turning in its chest. It passed without turning toward him, and he let it go.

<img src="images/S54 Crystal Figure.webp" alt="The hovering figure of milk-white crystal Farkas saw on the plain" class="content-image">

### The Woman at the Table — Viktor and Veezara Inside

Viktor got into the Living Stacks by showing the marks of his patron, the Thousand-Celled, and Veezara got in by donating three of his scales. Their entry was good for 26 hours. Going back in after that would take another allowed entry, because the marks and the scales only work once. Under green light, cataloguers worked at crystal tables below high shelves. A stooped woman in black handed a folio back to an attendant, corrected something, and went back to her work.

<img src="images/S54 Living Stacks.webp" alt="Crystal tables and memory shelves in the Living Stacks" class="content-image">

At another table, a woman with ash-silver hair was checking a crystal record against a written passage. Viktor recognised her as Cyrenne, his mother. She looked up as an attendant passed, and her eyes stopped on him, stylus held over the unfinished line.

<img src="images/S54 Cyrenne.webp" alt="Cyrenne, recognised by Viktor in the Living Stacks" class="content-image">

Viktor tried to convince her he was her son. She refused to accept it, and stayed at her work.

In the Stacks, Veezara learned the history of the Ashen Realms and came away with a summary of the older ages. They also learned that one sign of the Mother of Openings' arrival is that things start growing holes.

Then he and Viktor left the Stacks and went to find the others.

### The Idea-Dunes

Meanwhile, Karusa had stopped hiding and was walking beside Farkas through the near Idea-Dunes: white-grey ridges banded with green glass, and a black road running through the drifts with gold inlays pointing back to the gate. Viktor and Veezara caught up with them on that road.

<img src="images/S54 Idea-Dunes.webp" alt="The pale dunes outside Cerebrax, with a black road running through them" class="content-image">

### The White Hart

Farkas spotted a hart in the distance and told the others about it.

<img src="images/S54 Mnemon Hart.webp" alt="The six-legged Mnemon Hart with three coloured antler facets" class="content-image">

It was a towering white deer with six legs and split hooves. Three cloudy facets sat among its branching antlers, one smoky red, one bruised violet and one cold blue. It was grazing on a pale ridge across a shallow basin and lifted its crown between mouthfuls.

The company fought the Mnemon Hart and killed it.

### A Different Night for Farkas

Viktor, Veezara and Karusa went back into Cerebrax and completed long rests. Farkas stayed outside. In the night, right outside the city, a Choroid Harvester attacked him. He killed it, decided it wasn't safe to rest out in the open, and walked to a nearby farm.

<img src="images/S54 Choroid Harvester.webp" alt="The Choroid Harvester killed by Farkas outside Cerebrax" class="content-image">

He hadn't finished his rest when the session ended.
