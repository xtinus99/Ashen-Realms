# Session 55 — Designation, O.R.V.E.K

## Session Overview

- **Party Level:** 10, reaching 11 by session close
- **Location:** Cerebrax → Marrowgate → Last Tally → a weaver cave → Marrowgate
- **Party Members:** Farkas, Veezara, Viktor — plus Karusa; O.R.V.E.K accompanies the northern journey
- **PC Deaths:** None
- **Theme:** A traveller's medicine errand takes the company into the skull-country, and then three more cards drag them back to the sand.
- **Date:** ~late Gorravane, 2,836 AS
- **XP:** +15,775 each

## Session Log

### The Walker Ahead

The company left Cerebrax together on the Ossathari road. Farkas still hadn't had the rest he missed at the farm. Behind them the city's skull-glow hung green over pale folds of ground, and ahead, crystal grit had banked up along the black road.

Someone was walking the same way: tall, broad, with a curved frame rising over his shoulders like a lantern handle. The company closed on him until they could hear heavy footsteps and chain shifting under plate.

<img src="images/S55 Road Companion.webp" alt="O.R.V.E.K on the road out of Cerebrax" class="content-image">

He turned without breaking stride. His head was a long metal shape with a thin amber slit in it, and a small oil flame burned behind a brass grille in his breastplate. The armour was smoke-black with worn brass edges, the cloak was ragged, and a poleaxe rode sheathed along the carrying frame.

He was a warforged. Apart from Anvil, none of them had ever seen another, so they confronted him.

> “Designation, O.R.V.E.K.”<br>— O.R.V.E.K

They asked where he was going.

> “O.R.V.E.K must gather medicine located east of Ossathar. Medicine known as bone poppy.”<br>— O.R.V.E.K

The company decided to go with him. On the road he told them about his work, and why he hadn't been doing it.

> “Function of O.R.V.E.K: escort travellers. Travellers reach Cerebrax safely. Father's health has declined. O.R.V.E.K has been unable to perform function.”<br>— O.R.V.E.K

The bone poppy was for his father.

> “Bone poppy will help father remember. Father sometimes forgets O.R.V.E.K.”<br>— O.R.V.E.K

Viktor thought the poppies might come in handy. His mother didn't remember him either.

At dusk O.R.V.E.K refilled the lantern in his chest, and at camp he stood motionless and awake through his own rest.

### Three in the Chalk Cut

<img src="images/S55 Dazhul.webp" alt="A dazhul of the chalk country" class="content-image">

In a shaded chalk cut the company ran into three dazhuls. O.R.V.E.K destroyed all three. Afterward he slid the poleaxe back into the frame over his shoulders and checked his lantern, and the curved outline was back above him.

Farkas hauled one carcass away for the acid sack inside it, reckoned to be worth about a thousand gold. The other two bodies stayed in the cut beside the water.

### Back Through Marrowgate

Their old Unclaimed tokens got them into Marrowgate. All four took a long rest, and Farkas finally got the recovery he'd been waiting for. The acid sack sold for 800 gp, a little under the estimate.

Then they kept going north with O.R.V.E.K. The bone paving ran out of the city toward Last Tally, sounded by road crews and counted at every post.

### The Hospice at the End of the Paving

At Last Tally the road-watch checked their tokens at the south gate. Past the north gates the paving broke up into rubble. Veiled pilgrims waited at a swap bench, there were boots on the hospice shelf, and a chalk tally marked the March gate. At the black-stone Trace gate a warden stood with her maul resting head-down.

<img src="images/S55 Loveday.webp" alt="Loveday, tending the hospice at Last Tally" class="content-image">

O.R.V.E.K headed for the hospice. Inside were forty cots, a stove, and Loveday moving quietly between the sleepers with a tin lamp. He waited until she had finished with a patient, then asked her for bone poppies for his father's medicine.

Loveday sold him three dried heads. He needed only two, so he gave the third to Viktor. Viktor wanted more, thinking he'd be making the same medicine, and asked Loveday where he could find them. She pointed them to a cave, and O.R.V.E.K agreed to come along and keep them safe.

### Bone-Silk and Poppies

Past the paving there was only white dust and skull rubble. A cave opened in a low rise of chalk and bone, its mouth strung with threads as hard as ivory. O.R.V.E.K led them through the widest gap and held a strand aside with one gauntlet. It bent, but it didn't break.

The chamber ran a long way back into the rise. Bridges of bone-silk crossed it at two heights, standing wall panels split up the back of the floor, and cocoons hung from the roof. One fresh bundle hung low by the left wall with a pair of boots showing underneath and a chalk number on one sole. At the back, pale poppies grew out of picked bones.

<img src="images/S55 Skeletal Weaver.webp" alt="A skeletal weaver among its bone-silk threads" class="content-image">

Something clicked overhead, and more clicking answered it from around the cave. Seven weavers came for the company: two Blessed, one Ancient and four Skeletal. All seven died.

The fresh cocoon held a dead pilgrim. The party took a Ring of Resistance against psychic damage off the body and picked four bone poppies from the back of the cave. There was nobody in there left to save.

### The Roads Separate

They went back to Last Tally and took a long rest in Loveday's hospice. In the morning O.R.V.E.K left with his medicine, bound back for Cerebrax, and the company took the south road to Marrowgate.

They slept at the Marrow Rest, then went to the Bonecage. Farkas, Veezara and Karusa already had enough wins to ask for a Rib promotion match. Viktor was one short, so for now they asked for a No Rules card.

### The Reed-Cutters

<img src="images/The Bonecage.webp" alt="The Bonecage, where the company fought three cards in one night" class="content-image">

The card was Sszarik, Issik and Varru, the Reed-Cutters. All four signed, and the Public Draw left the purse open at 150 gp.

The company won. Farkas killed Issik and Karusa killed Varru. Veezara talked Sszarik into surrendering. Sszarik looked at his dead broodmates and then killed himself.

None of the Reed-Cutters walked out. The records went to 9–1 for Farkas, Veezara and Karusa and 8–1 for Viktor, which put all four in line for the next promotion match.

### Two More Wildcards

<img src="images/S55 Tarnal.webp" alt="The Tarnal faced in the first Wildcard" class="content-image">

Next they took a Wildcard without knowing what they would face. What came out onto the sand was a Tarnal. The company killed it for a fixed 400 gp purse.

<img src="images/S55 Skin-Sleeved.webp" alt="The Skin-Sleeved, the final opponent of the night" class="content-image">

The third card was another Wildcard, for a 600 gp purse. What came out was the Skin-Sleeved. The company beat it.

Three cards in one night came to 1,150 gp in purses, with records of 11–1 for Farkas, Veezara and Karusa and 10–1 for Viktor. They went back to the Marrow Rest and took a long rest. Each of them could now have a Rib promotion match. Nobody had asked for one yet.
