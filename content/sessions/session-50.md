# Session 50 — I'll Be Back

## Session Overview

- **Party Level:** 7; Karusa reaches Rogue 8
- **Location:** The Marrow Rest → the Bonecage → the Eternal Archive → the Marrow Markets → the Marrow Rest
- **Party Members:** Farkas, Veezara, Viktor — plus Karusa
- **PC Deaths:** None
- **Theme:** Three wins and a promotion to Knuckle. The Pallbearer came back for another try at Vortegas, and a guard suggested they look north.
- **Date:** ~late Gorravane, 2,836 AS
- **XP:** +4,212 each; 2 XP unassigned

---

## Session Log

### Back to Four

Farkas, Veezara and Karusa came back from their second arena win to find Viktor up and able to leave his room. He still had one level of Exhaustion, but there were four of them again. The three fighters had not rested since their card, and they went back to the Bonecage anyway.

Ferrin offered them a Wildcard. He never says what's behind one, and they took it anyway.

### The First Step Up

<img src="images/Grolch.webp" alt="Grolch, the company's first Wildcard of the session" class="content-image">

The arena's chirurgeon noted Viktor's exhaustion and cleared him to fight. The Pen Floor had two containment recesses and two marked jars of lime-acid, and what came out onto it was Grolch.

The four beat Grolch and took 120 gp. The purse mattered less than the new entry on the slate. Every one of them could now ask for a Knuckle promotion match, which meant they had earned a shot at promotion.

### Sixty Years, the Same Promise

They were on their way out when a fresh wave of cheering turned them around. Vortegas had taken the Warden's Seat and ordered the arena cleared. Under a cold black corona, a many-armed figure vaulted onto the floor.

The day before, a clerk had told them the story. Sixty years ago the Pallbearer challenged Vortegas, lost, and promised to come back. Now the challenger was standing in front of the Bone-Warden again.

They traded blows. Vortegas won. The Pallbearer made the same promise and leapt out of the arena.

> “I'll be back.”<br>— the Pallbearer

All the company had was still the title. They had watched the return and learned neither a name nor a reason for it.

### Unknown, or Unreadable?

<img src="images/The Eternal Archive.webp" alt="The Eternal Archive in Marrowgate" class="content-image">

At the Eternal Archive's Index Spine, Viktor asked the archive keeper for something strange or unknown. The keeper wanted to know which he meant: unknown to Viktor, or unknown to the Archive? He could provide the public record of the Pallbearer's earlier challenge. There was also a job going, involving a sealed historical catalogue that was losing information inside its supporting wall.

<img src="images/S50 Archive Keeper.webp" alt="The archive keeper at the Index Spine" class="content-image">

Viktor said he meant writing that ordinary people couldn't read, Deep Speech maybe. The keeper steered that toward scripts and provenance. A catalogue search could find material by language, and a private text could be compared under supervision. For more serious provenance work, he named the Quiet Folio in Cerebrax.

The company left without paying for a search, showing him a book or taking the catalogue job. They now had a practical reason to go to Cerebrax. Viktor's texts, meanwhile, had still not been looked at.

### Three Empty Vials

In the Soft Arcade, Viktor showed an apothecary the black veins in his arm. The seller couldn't name the cause and offered amputation, with no promise that taking the limb off would stop the blackening. Viktor said no.

He asked about blood instead. The shop had none for sale, but its proprietor would buy specimens from the arena. Viktor left with three empty stoppered vials.

Back at the Marrow Rest, everyone took a long rest and Viktor's exhaustion ended. When he woke he tried the Abyssal Book again, still couldn't read it, and told the page what he thought of that:

> “Fuck.”<br>— Viktor

Downstairs he ordered an egg, grilled ossen and milk. Then the four went to claim their promotion match.

### The Two Endings

<img src="images/The Two Endings.webp" alt="Rilsa Noll and Tob Kren, the Two Endings" class="content-image">

Rilsa Noll and Tob Kren met them on Quarry Step. If the company beat the pair or got them to surrender, all four entrants would make Knuckle. The Public Draw left the 200 gp purse as it was.

The company won. Rilsa died and Tob surrendered. The Two Endings' record closed on that card at 10–5, with only one of the partners still alive. It wasn't a Death Card, so Rilsa's registered equipment stayed out of the victors' spoils.

Four new Ribward Knuckles went up on the slate. Before Rilsa's body was taken away, Viktor filled his first vial with her blood.

### Back into the Deep Pens

<img src="images/Acromel.webp" alt="Acromel, encountered again in the Deep-Pen Wildcard" class="content-image">

They took another card without resting, a one-off Wildcard in the Deep Pens with the purse fixed at 400 gp. What came out was Acromel and Dolich together, so the company ended up facing the creature from the Wildcard it had turned down the day before.

<img src="images/S50 Dolich.webp" alt="Dolich, the second Deep-Pen specimen" class="content-image">

During the bout something else came out of Acromel, which gave them one more opponent. The company beat all three. Acromel and Dolich were killed. The arena counted the third as part of Acromel's card, so it changed neither the result nor the purse.

Viktor filled his remaining vials from the two specimens. All four now had the two wins against Knuckle opposition that they needed. Farkas, Veezara and Karusa stood at 5–0, Viktor at 4–0.

### North

The same apothecary bought all three blood samples for 140 gp, and Viktor walked out with no samples and no vials either. Azalea's separate vial of his own blood was still back at the Pallid Crossroads.

After he rejoined the others, they asked a guard about the quarry. It was for mining bone and calcite, he said. When they asked whether anything was amiss, he pointed them somewhere else.

> “If you're looking for something amiss, maybe try north. Where those ‘things’ came from.”<br>— an unnamed guard

That was all he gave them, with no site, no route and no explanation. They went back to the Marrow Rest with a direction and took another long rest. Karusa advanced to Rogue 8. Where they went next was still up to them.
