## Session Log

### A House Beyond the Road

The short rest outside the tomb was done. Behind them the stair sat dark between its weathered helms. The scratching had stopped, and nothing followed them out onto the white stone of the basin.

They carried on toward Cerebrax on foot. The road turned up no travellers and no trouble. Then Veezara raised his spyglass and found warm windows beyond the bone ridge.

<img src="images/S53 Manor Exterior.webp" alt="Velladine Manor beyond its open iron gates" class="content-image">

A narrow lane climbed a sheltered rise to a three-storey manor. Pale walls and open iron gates enclosed black trees, clipped hedges and a carriage circle. A carriage waited under the portico with no animal in its traces. For a house standing in empty country, it was remarkably well kept.

The company turned off the road. On the way up they began to hear a delicate, formal waltz, the sort of tune a bedside music box plays, except this one carried clearly over the whole estate. It got no louder as they crossed the grounds.

The doors opened before anyone knocked. An elderly man in immaculate black was waiting in the amber hall.

<img src="images/S53 Hobb.webp" alt="Mr. Hobb at the open doors of Velladine Manor" class="content-image">

> “Welcome to Velladine Manor. His Lordship is delighted that the road has brought us company. May I have the names by which you wish to be announced?”<br>— Mr. Hobb

### Too Late for the Reception

They gave Hobb their names. It was after six, and the evening reception had already started. His Lordship would not receive new guests halfway through an introduction, Hobb explained, so their party would be tomorrow at six.

He led them past silver lamps and family portraits to guest rooms that were already made up: washed basins, turned beds, writing desks, evening clothes folded and waiting. Veezara went to sleep in the room he shared with Karusa, and she stayed awake to watch over him. Farkas and Viktor went downstairs.

### The Same Laugh Twice

<img src="images/S53 Drawing Room.webp" alt="The drawing-room guests at Velladine Manor" class="content-image">

The drawing room held a dozen well-dressed guests spread between the fire, a hunting portrait and two long sofas. Dark wine caught the lamplight. Near the far wall, a pair gave the same short laugh twice, at the same interval, with the same tilt of the head. Nobody remarked on it.

Hobb introduced Lady Cecily Vaund, who warned against hurrying a third movement; Justice Brannick, who insisted the western boundary levy was temporary; and Ruy Varro, a fencer bragging by the fireplace.

Viktor asked Ruy about the next evening. Hobb would announce the guests at six, Ruy said, before the waltz and supper, and Lord Velladine would come down later with Miravel, Tavian and Neris. Then he went back to his boast.

> “Aurelian will not touch me before the fifth exchange.”<br>— Ruy Varro

His wrist turned too far as he gestured at the fire, then settled back under his cuff. Viktor asked him what year it was.

> “Fourteen hundred and thirty-six, Aedwynn's Walk.”<br>— Ruy Varro

Outside, it was 2,836 AS. Ruy had answered without a moment's hesitation, from a different age.

### What Was Alive

Before going upstairs, Viktor tested the guests with an ability. They were not alive. He and Farkas took them for ghosts, though the result didn't say what they actually were.

The two went back to Viktor's room and completed a long rest. With the door shut, the waltz was just as clear as before. Later they looked over the family portraits in the hall, whose painted eyes moved together with the music.

<img src="images/S53 Aurelian Portrait.webp" alt="The portrait of Aurelian Velladine examined by Viktor" class="content-image">

The master of the house was painted tall and pallid, in formal black, with a faded wine-red ribbon tying back his hair and a slender sword at his side. Viktor rolled a 23 on Medicine and came away suspecting a vampire. He told the others.

### The Empty Stables

Farkas went out to have a look around the grounds. Two carriage handlers were sitting out front, watching one of the butlers work on the garden.

He got talking with them and asked who they were. Carriage handlers, they said. They had driven some of the guests up to the manor, and the horses were over in the stables.

Farkas went and had a look. The stables were empty. Not one horse. He thought that was strange, but he didn't push it. He thanked the two of them and went back inside.

### Behind the Wine Barrel

Viktor persuaded Hobb that he might be able to arrange a trade, which got him a tour of the wine collection. Behind one barrel, a section of wall caught his eye.

Once Hobb had gone, the party sent Karusa back to look quietly. One stone was loose. Behind it was a button, and pressing it opened a concealed door onto stairs that went further down, below the cellar.

She went down and came back unnoticed with her report.

> “Loose stone. Button behind it. The wall opens. Stairs down. One coffin. A family photograph beside it.”<br>— Karusa

The coffin was empty and looked seldom used. The company didn't open it or dig any further, and nobody looked closely at the photograph.

### The Music Box

Then the company found where the waltz was coming from: a music box hanging above one of the pedestals in the middle of the ballroom. It had been playing the exact same tune the whole time they'd been in the house.

<img src="images/S53 Music Box.webp" alt="The music box in the ballroom: a black box with two small silver dancers inside its open lid" class="content-image">

They asked one of the butlers about it. It was a gift from the lord's wife, he told them. It was to be handled with care, and nobody was allowed to touch it.

So they switched it off while nobody was looking. A butler came rushing over, turned it straight back on, and asked them to please be more careful.

After that they went back to wait for the reception.

### Six O'Clock

<img src="images/S53 Reception.webp" alt="The evening reception at Velladine Manor" class="content-image">

At six, Hobb came to escort them in and announce them. They entered the ballroom and stayed through the opening waltz. About twenty minutes in, Aurelian came down in formal black with Miravel on his arm, and Tavian and Neris one stair behind.

> “My wife, Miravel. Our son, Tavian. Our daughter, Neris. This house has had too many evenings without new names in it. You honour us by lending it yours.”<br>— Aurelian Velladine

He greeted the four guests. When they asked about the world outside the estate, he seemed not to recognise that any of it had changed, and answered as if the manor's preserved world were still the present one.

### The Name Veezara Asked For

Pressed further, Aurelian told them that more than four thousand years ago he had fought for his city. A group of vampires invited him to join them, and he refused. He was turned, and his wife and children were killed in front of him.

Veezara asked who had done it. Aurelian named Faustus Sangrave.

Aurelian wanted vengeance. The company judged that a straight fight with Faustus would be a death sentence, and the conversation brought them no closer to him. They came to see their host as a frail man turned against his will, rather than an evil to be struck down in his own house.

Viktor also asked about his blackened veins. Aurelian had seen marks like them on members of a cult he destroyed in the South, a cult that had taken people from a nearby village for sacrifices. He gave no name for the cult, and no place or date.

The company asked to stay one night, and Aurelian agreed. He would be fine, he said, as long as he could see his wife.

Before the evening was out, Viktor took another look at the music box. It was magical, and not in just one way. He could make out several kinds of magic coming off it, mostly transmutation and illusion.

They stayed on at the reception as his guests. Nobody promised him vengeance, and the night he had granted them hadn't started yet.
